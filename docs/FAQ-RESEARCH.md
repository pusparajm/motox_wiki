# Motorcycle FAQ research

Research date: 2026-11-21

## Page types reviewed

Model pages were reviewed across sport, commuter, scooter, adventure, cruiser and electric bikes on:

- Price-led motorcycle marketplaces
- Specification databases
- Review and road-test publications
- Rider-fit and ergonomics references
- Owner and used-buying guides

## FAQ patterns found

### Market and purchase questions

- On-road and ex-showroom price
- EMI and down payment
- Current offers
- Variants and colour options
- Dealer availability
- Launch date

These are market-specific. MotoX Wiki does not store local pricing, finance or dealer data, so these were not added.

### Specification questions

- Engine displacement
- Power and torque
- Top speed where verified
- Kerb weight
- Seat height
- Fuel-tank capacity
- Tyres and wheel type
- ABS or combined braking
- Suspension front and rear
- Brakes front and rear
- Transmission and final drive
- Dimensions, wheelbase and ground clearance

### Ownership and service questions

- Service interval
- Engine-oil capacity and grade
- Fork-oil grade, quantity or air gap
- Fork seal, dust seal and slide-bush sizes
- Rear shock type
- Brake fluid and coolant
- Reliability and used-buying checks

### Rider-fit and use questions

- Beginner suitability
- A2 compatibility
- Fit for shorter riders
- City, touring, pillion or off-road use
- Heat, vibration and riding posture

These need either measured data or a clearly stated editorial basis. MotoX Wiki answers beginner and A2 questions from power, weight and seat height, but avoids blanket comfort, heat or reliability claims.

### Electric-specific questions

- Range
- Battery capacity
- Charging time and fast-charging support
- Top speed
- Licence requirement
- Battery warranty

These are valuable, but the current catalogue does not hold enough verified electric drivetrain data for most models.

## What is generated

The model-page FAQ generator produces data-backed answers for:

- Power and torque
- Engine displacement
- Top speed where listed
- Seat height
- Kerb weight
- Calculated power-to-weight ratio
- Fuel capacity
- Tyres
- ABS or combined braking
- Front and rear suspension
- Front and rear brakes
- Service interval
- Engine oil capacity and grade
- Fork oil data
- Fork seal size
- Rear shock type
- Beginner-shortlist fit, using power, weight, seat-height and category rules
- European A2 power and power-to-weight limits
- Seat-height band for shorter-rider context

A question is emitted only when the underlying field exists and is not a placeholder value.

## What is deliberately left out

- Prices, EMI, offers, colours and dealer data: market-specific and not stored in the catalogue.
- Mileage and fuel economy: not verified across enough models.
- Comfort, heat, vibration and reliability: requires testing or a cited owner survey.
- Generic “which is better” questions: handled by dedicated comparison pages instead.
- Launch dates and future-model questions: not a stable specification reference.

## Related page patterns

- Specification catalogues with deep dimensions and model history.
- Review sites with ownership and used-buying sections.
- Rider-fit references with seat-height and ergonomics data.
- Price-led marketplaces with FAQ schema and comparison links.
