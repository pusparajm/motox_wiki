Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 40 Nm @ 5,500 rpm.

What tyres does the Himalayan 450 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-21 front, 140/80-17 rear.

What brakes does the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450 use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 270 mm disc.

What is the service interval for the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450?

The listed service interval is 10,000 km / 12 months. Confirm the schedule for your market and model year.

What battery does the Himalayan 450 use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What spark plug does the Himalayan 450 use?

The listed spark plug is Replace every 20,000 km. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the Himalayan 450 use?

The listed chain or final drive is Chain. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Royal Enfield Himalayan 450?

Engine oil capacity is 1.80 L (1.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
