CBR600RR vs CBR650R

Supersport comparison · 2013–2017 vs 2024-Present

The CBR600RR is the sharper track-focused 600, while the CBR650R is the more usable road bike with a broader powerband and comfortable ergonomics.

This is a specification comparison using the catalogue entries for Honda CBR600RR (2013–2017) and Honda CBR650R (2024-Present). It is not a ride review; specifications can change by model year and market.

What the numbers say

  • Power: 118 hp (88 kW) @ 13,500 rpm vs 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm. The Honda CBR600RR lists the higher claimed power (24 hp).
  • Torque: 66 Nm @ 11,250 rpm vs 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm. The Honda CBR600RR lists the higher claimed torque (3 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 194 kg vs 211 kg. The Honda CBR600RR lists the lower kerb weight (17 kg).
  • Seat height: 820 mm vs 810 mm. The Honda CBR650R lists the lower seat height (10 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 18 L vs 15.4 L. The Honda CBR600RR lists the larger fuel capacity (2.6 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 608 vs 445 hp per tonne. The Honda CBR600RR has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
