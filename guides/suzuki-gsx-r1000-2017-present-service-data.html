Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 117.6 Nm @ 10,800 rpm.

What tyres does the GSX-R1000 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, Brembo radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the GSX-R1000 use?

Front: EBC FA447HH · SBS 841/901 · Brembo 07BB37 · Vesrah VD-9031. Rear: EBC FA436 · SBS 834/991 · Brembo 07HO59 · Vesrah VD-174. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the GSX-R1000 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9EIA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the GSX-R1000 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 120 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2019–2020, 2023; air gap 100 mm — 2019–2020, 2023. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.
