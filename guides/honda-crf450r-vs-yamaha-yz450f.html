What the numbers say

  • Power: 57 hp (42 kW) vs 59 hp (44 kW). The Yamaha YZ450F lists the higher claimed power (2 hp).
  • Torque: 47 Nm vs 48 Nm. The Yamaha YZ450F lists the higher claimed torque (1 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 111 kg vs 109 kg. The Yamaha YZ450F lists the lower kerb weight (2 kg).
  • Seat height: 960 mm vs 965 mm. The Honda CRF450R lists the lower seat height (5 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 6.3 L vs 6.2 L. The Honda CRF450R lists the larger fuel capacity (0.1 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 514 vs 541 hp per tonne. The Yamaha YZ450F has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 80/100-21 front, 120/80-19 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 5-speed, chain final drive.
