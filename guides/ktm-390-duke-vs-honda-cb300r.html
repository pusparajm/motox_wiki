What the numbers say

  • Power: 44.5 hp (33 kW) @ 8,500 rpm vs 31 hp (23 kW) @ 8,500 rpm. The KTM 390 Duke lists the higher claimed power (13.5 hp).
  • Torque: 39 Nm @ 6,500 rpm vs 27.5 Nm @ 7,500 rpm. The KTM 390 Duke lists the higher claimed torque (11.5 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 165 kg vs 144 kg. The Honda CB300R lists the lower kerb weight (21 kg).
  • Seat height: 820 mm vs 799 mm. The Honda CB300R lists the lower seat height (21 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 15 L vs 10.1 L. The KTM 390 Duke lists the larger fuel capacity (4.9 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 270 vs 215 hp per tonne. The KTM 390 Duke has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
