The Ninja 500 replaces the 400 with a larger, torquier engine while keeping the same lightweight formula.

What the numbers say

  • Power: 44.8 hp (33 kW) @ 10,000 rpm vs 51 hp (38 kW) @ 10,000 rpm. The Kawasaki Ninja 500 lists the higher claimed power (6.2 hp).
  • Torque: 38 Nm @ 8,000 rpm vs 42.6 Nm @ 7,500 rpm. The Kawasaki Ninja 500 lists the higher claimed torque (4.6 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 168 kg vs 171 kg. The Kawasaki Ninja 400 lists the lower kerb weight (3 kg).
  • Power-to-weight: about 267 vs 298 hp per tonne. The Kawasaki Ninja 500 has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which has more power, the Kawasaki Ninja 400 or the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

The Kawasaki Ninja 500 lists 51 hp (38 kW) @ 10,000 rpm, compared with 44.8 hp (33 kW) @ 10,000 rpm for the Kawasaki Ninja 400.

Which is lighter, the Kawasaki Ninja 400 or the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

The Kawasaki Ninja 400 lists 168 kg, compared with 171 kg for the Kawasaki Ninja 500.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja 400 and Kawasaki Ninja 500?

Using the listed power and kerb weight, the calculated ratios are about 267 hp per tonne for the Kawasaki Ninja 400 and 298 hp per tonne for the Kawasaki Ninja 500. The Kawasaki Ninja 500 has the higher listed ratio.

Are the Kawasaki Ninja 400 and Kawasaki Ninja 500 both supersport motorcycles?

Yes. Both are listed in the Supersport category in the catalogue.

Do the Kawasaki Ninja 400 and Kawasaki Ninja 500 use the same tyre sizes?

Yes. Both list 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.
