MT-07 vs MT-09

Naked comparison · 2018–Present vs 2015-2018

The MT-07 is lighter and cheaper to run; the MT-09 adds a three-cylinder engine, more power and more equipment.

This is a specification comparison using the catalogue entries for Yamaha MT-07 (2018–Present) and Yamaha MT-09 (2015-2018). It is not a ride review; specifications can change by model year and market.

What the numbers say

  • Power: 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm vs 115 hp (85 kW) @ 10,000 rpm. The Yamaha MT-09 lists the higher claimed power (41 hp).
  • Torque: 68 Nm @ 6,500 rpm vs 87.5 Nm @ 8,500 rpm. The Yamaha MT-09 lists the higher claimed torque (19.5 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 184 kg vs 193 kg. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the lower kerb weight (9 kg).
  • Seat height: 805 mm vs 815 mm. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the lower seat height (10 mm).
  • Power-to-weight: about 402 vs 596 hp per tonne. The Yamaha MT-09 has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which has more power, the Yamaha MT-07 or the Yamaha MT-09?

The Yamaha MT-09 lists 115 hp (85 kW) @ 10,000 rpm, compared with 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm for the Yamaha MT-07.

Which is lighter, the Yamaha MT-07 or the Yamaha MT-09?

The Yamaha MT-07 lists 184 kg, compared with 193 kg for the Yamaha MT-09.

Which has a lower seat height, the Yamaha MT-07 or the Yamaha MT-09?

The Yamaha MT-07 lists 805 mm, compared with 815 mm for the Yamaha MT-09.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha MT-07 and Yamaha MT-09?

Using the listed power and kerb weight, the calculated ratios are about 402 hp per tonne for the Yamaha MT-07 and 596 hp per tonne for the Yamaha MT-09. The Yamaha MT-09 has the higher listed ratio.

Are the Yamaha MT-07 and Yamaha MT-09 both naked motorcycles?

Yes. Both are listed in the Naked category in the catalogue.

Do the Yamaha MT-07 and Yamaha MT-09 use the same tyre sizes?

Yes. Both list 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.
