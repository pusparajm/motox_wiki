MT-07 vs MT-09

Yamaha MT-07 vs Yamaha MT-09

The MT-07 is lighter and cheaper to run; the MT-09 adds a three-cylinder engine, more power and more equipment.

On-paper differences

  • Power: Yamaha MT-07 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm vs Yamaha MT-09 115 hp (85 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.
  • Weight: 184 kg vs 193 kg.
  • Seat height: 805 mm vs 815 mm.
  • Fuel capacity: 14 L vs 14 L.
