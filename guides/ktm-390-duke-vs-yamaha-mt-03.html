What the numbers say

  • Power: 44.5 hp (33 kW) @ 8,500 rpm vs 42 hp (31 kW) @ 10,750 rpm. The KTM 390 Duke lists the higher claimed power (2.5 hp).
  • Torque: 39 Nm @ 6,500 rpm vs 29.6 Nm @ 9,000 rpm. The KTM 390 Duke lists the higher claimed torque (9.4 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 165 kg vs 169 kg. The KTM 390 Duke lists the lower kerb weight (4 kg).
  • Seat height: 820 mm vs 780 mm. The Yamaha MT-03 lists the lower seat height (40 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 15 L vs 14 L. The KTM 390 Duke lists the larger fuel capacity (1 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 270 vs 249 hp per tonne. The KTM 390 Duke has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
