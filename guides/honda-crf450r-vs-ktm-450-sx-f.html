Motocross comparison · 2021–Present vs 2022–Present

The KTM 450 SX-F lists 6 hp more, 2 Nm more torque, 7 kg less weight, a 2 mm lower seat and 0.7 L more fuel capacity than the Honda CRF450R.

What the numbers say

  • Power: 57 hp (42 kW) vs 63 hp (47 kW). The KTM 450 SX-F lists the higher claimed power (6 hp).
  • Torque: 47 Nm vs 49 Nm. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the higher claimed torque (2 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 111 kg vs 104 kg. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the lower kerb weight (7 kg).
  • Seat height: 960 mm vs 958 mm. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the lower seat height (2 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 6.3 L vs 7 L. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the larger fuel capacity (0.7 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 514 vs 606 hp per tonne. The KTM 450 SX-F has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Transmission: both list 5-speed, chain final drive.
