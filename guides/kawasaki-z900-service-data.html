Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z900?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 98.6 Nm @ 7,700 rpm.

What tyres does the Z900 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What battery does the Z900 use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Z900 use?

Front: EBC FA347HH · SBS 782 · Brembo 07KA23 · Vesrah VD-443. Rear: EBC FA174 · SBS 657 · Brembo 07HO53 · Vesrah VD-163. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Z900 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9EIA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the Z900 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 114 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Z900?

Engine oil capacity is 3.6 L (3.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
