Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 400?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 38 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What tyres does the Ninja 400 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja 400 use?

Front: 310 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the Ninja 400 use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Ninja 400 use?

Front: SBS 955HS · Brembo 07KA31. Rear: SBS 638LS · Brembo 07YA22. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Ninja 400 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LMAR9G. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the Ninja 400 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 106 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 400?

Engine oil capacity is 2.0 L (2.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
