What the numbers say

  • Power: 59 hp (44 kW) vs 63 hp (47 kW). The KTM 450 SX-F lists the higher claimed power (4 hp).
  • Torque: 48 Nm vs 49 Nm. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the higher claimed torque (1 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 109 kg vs 104 kg. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the lower kerb weight (5 kg).
  • Seat height: 965 mm vs 958 mm. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the lower seat height (7 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 6.2 L vs 7 L. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the larger fuel capacity (0.8 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 541 vs 606 hp per tonne. The KTM 450 SX-F has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Transmission: both list 5-speed, chain final drive.
