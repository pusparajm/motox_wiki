What the numbers say

  • Power: 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm vs 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm. The Honda CB650R lists the higher claimed power (20 hp).
  • Torque: 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm vs 68 Nm @ 6,500 rpm. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the higher claimed torque (5 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 208 kg vs 184 kg. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the lower kerb weight (24 kg).
  • Seat height: 810 mm vs 805 mm. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the lower seat height (5 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 15.4 L vs 14 L. The Honda CB650R lists the larger fuel capacity (1.4 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 452 vs 402 hp per tonne. The Honda CB650R has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
