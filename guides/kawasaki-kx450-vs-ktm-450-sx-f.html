Kawasaki KX450 vs KTM 450 SX-F

Motocross comparison · 2024–Present vs 2022–Present

The KTM 450 SX-F lists 5 hp more, 6 kg less weight, a 2 mm lower seat and 0.8 L more fuel capacity than the Kawasaki KX450. Both list 49 Nm of torque.

This is a specification comparison using the catalogue entries for Kawasaki KX450 (2024–Present) and KTM 450 SX-F (2022–Present). It is not a ride review; specifications can change by model year and market.

What the numbers say

  • Power: 58 hp (43 kW) vs 63 hp (47 kW). The KTM 450 SX-F lists the higher claimed power (5 hp).
  • Kerb weight: 110 kg vs 104 kg. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the lower kerb weight (6 kg).
  • Seat height: 960 mm vs 958 mm. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the lower seat height (2 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 6.2 L vs 7 L. The KTM 450 SX-F lists the larger fuel capacity (0.8 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 527 vs 606 hp per tonne. The KTM 450 SX-F has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Transmission: both list 5-speed, chain final drive.
