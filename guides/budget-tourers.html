  • Listed front suspension travel: 130–210 mm across the group.

How to shortlist

Start with seat height and kerb weight, then compare tank size and listed power. For off-road use, check front suspension travel, tyre sizes and brake specification; for touring, prioritise fuel capacity, seat height and weather protection.
