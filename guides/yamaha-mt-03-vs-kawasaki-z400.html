What the numbers say

  • Power: 42 hp (31 kW) @ 10,750 rpm vs 45 hp (33 kW) @ 10,000 rpm. The Kawasaki Z400 lists the higher claimed power (3 hp).
  • Torque: 29.6 Nm @ 9,000 rpm vs 37 Nm @ 8,000 rpm. The Kawasaki Z400 lists the higher claimed torque (7.4 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 169 kg vs 167 kg. The Kawasaki Z400 lists the lower kerb weight (2 kg).
  • Seat height: 780 mm vs 785 mm. The Yamaha MT-03 lists the lower seat height (5 mm).
  • Power-to-weight: about 249 vs 269 hp per tonne. The Kawasaki Z400 has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
