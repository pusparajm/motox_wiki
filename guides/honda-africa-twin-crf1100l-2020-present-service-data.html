Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 105 Nm @ 6,250 rpm.

What tyres does the CRF1100 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-21 front, 150/70-18 rear.

What brakes does the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 256 mm disc.

What battery does the CRF1100 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ14S. Alternative listed codes: 12V 6Ah lithium (2024+). Battery rating: 12V 11.2Ah (2020–2023). Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the CRF1100 use?

Front: SBS 940HS. Rear: EBC FA174 · SBS 657LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the CRF1100 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 124 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2026; air gap 130 mm — 2026. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.
