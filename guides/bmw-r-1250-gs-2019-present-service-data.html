Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the BMW R 1250 GS?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 143 Nm @ 6,250 rpm.

What tyres does the R 1250 GS use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-19 front, 170/60-17 rear.

What brakes does the BMW R 1250 GS use?

Front: 305 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 276 mm disc.

What battery does the R 1250 GS use?

The listed battery code is YTX14-BS. Alternative listed codes: GYZ16H, YTX14H-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the R 1250 GS use?

Front: EBC FA724HH · SBS 960 · Brembo 07GR90 · Vesrah VD-9088. Rear: EBC FA209/2 · SBS 176/674/1005 · Brembo 07BB03 · Vesrah VD-958. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the R 1250 GS use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LMAR8AI-10. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW R 1250 GS?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
