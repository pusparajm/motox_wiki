Honda CRF450R vs Kawasaki KX450

Motocross comparison · 2021–Present vs 2024–Present

The Kawasaki KX450 lists 1 hp more, 2 Nm more torque and 1 kg less weight than the Honda CRF450R. Both list a 960 mm seat; the CRF450R has 0.1 L more fuel capacity.

What the numbers say

  • Power: 57 hp (42 kW) vs 58 hp (43 kW). The Kawasaki KX450 lists the higher claimed power (1 hp).
  • Torque: 47 Nm vs 49 Nm. The Kawasaki KX450 lists the higher claimed torque (2 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 111 kg vs 110 kg. The Kawasaki KX450 lists the lower kerb weight (1 kg).
  • Fuel capacity: 6.3 L vs 6.2 L. The Honda CRF450R lists the larger fuel capacity (0.1 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 514 vs 527 hp per tonne. The Kawasaki KX450 has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 80/100-21 front, 120/80-19 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 5-speed, chain final drive.
