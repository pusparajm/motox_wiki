What the numbers say

  • Power: 90.5 hp (67.5 kW) @ 9,500 rpm vs 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm. The Honda CB750 Hornet lists the higher claimed power (16.5 hp).
  • Torque: 75 Nm @ 7,250 rpm vs 68 Nm @ 6,500 rpm. The Honda CB750 Hornet lists the higher claimed torque (7 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 190 kg vs 184 kg. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the lower kerb weight (6 kg).
  • Seat height: 795 mm vs 805 mm. The Honda CB750 Hornet lists the lower seat height (10 mm).
  • Fuel capacity: 15.2 L vs 14 L. The Honda CB750 Hornet lists the larger fuel capacity (1.2 L).
  • Power-to-weight: about 476 vs 402 hp per tonne. The Honda CB750 Hornet has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
