What the numbers say

  • Power: 105 hp (77 kW) @ 9,000 rpm vs 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm. The KTM 790 Duke lists the higher claimed power (31 hp).
  • Torque: 87 Nm @ 8,000 rpm vs 68 Nm @ 6,500 rpm. The KTM 790 Duke lists the higher claimed torque (19 Nm).
  • Kerb weight: 169 kg vs 184 kg. The KTM 790 Duke lists the lower kerb weight (15 kg).
  • Seat height: 825 mm vs 805 mm. The Yamaha MT-07 lists the lower seat height (20 mm).
  • Power-to-weight: about 621 vs 402 hp per tonne. The KTM 790 Duke has the higher calculated ratio.
  • Tyres: both list 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.
  • Transmission: both list 6-speed, chain final drive.
