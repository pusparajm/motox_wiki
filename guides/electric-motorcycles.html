  • Listed front suspension travel: 150–310 mm across the group.

How to shortlist

Start with seat height and kerb weight, then compare battery capacity and listed power. For off-road use, check front suspension travel, tyre sizes and brake specification; for touring, prioritise battery capacity, seat height and weather protection.
