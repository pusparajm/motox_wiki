Motorcycle Torque Specifications

Fastener torque data for 254 models.

Motorcycle “torque specs” can mean two different things: engine torque (the performance figure) and fastener torque (the tightening value for bolts and nuts). This page is about the second: service-manual torque values for axles, brakes, engine covers and other fasteners.
