Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the KTM 390 Adventure?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 37 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What tyres does the 390 Adventure use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/90-19 front, 130/80-17 rear.

What brakes does the KTM 390 Adventure use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 230 mm disc.

What battery does the 390 Adventure use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the 390 Adventure use?

Front: Brembo 07GR18. Rear: Brembo 07BB02. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the 390 Adventure use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 118 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the KTM 390 Adventure?

Engine oil capacity is 1.7 L (1.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
