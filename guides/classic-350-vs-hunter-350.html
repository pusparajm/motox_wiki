Classic 350 vs Hunter 350

Retro comparison · 2021–Present vs 2022-Present

The Classic 350 is the traditional, relaxed Royal Enfield; the Hunter 350 is lighter, shorter and more city-focused.

What the numbers say

  • Kerb weight: 195 kg vs 181 kg. The Royal Enfield Hunter 350 lists the lower kerb weight (14 kg).
