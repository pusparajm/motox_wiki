Maintenance checklist

  • Engine oil — check the level and condition; replace it at the interval in the owner's manual.
  • Oil filter — replace it with the oil change where the manual specifies a filter change.
  • Chain and sprockets — check slack, lubrication and wear.
  • Tyres — check pressures, tread depth and sidewall condition.
  • Brakes — check pad thickness, disc condition and fluid level.
  • Battery — check terminals, charge and the hold-down.
  • Fasteners — check axle, caliper and handlebar clamp bolts.
  • Cooling system — check the coolant level and hoses when the engine is cold.
  • Valve clearance — check at the manufacturer's stated interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Honda CB650R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What tyres does the 650R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

What brakes does the Honda CB650R use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the 650R use?

The listed battery code is YTZ10S. Battery rating: 12V 8.6Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the 650R use?

Front: EBC FA679HH · SBS 241/940 · Vesrah VD-187. Rear: EBC FA496 · SBS 881 · Vesrah VD-147/2. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the 650R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 118 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda CB650R?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Honda CB650R use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2019, 2026. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.
