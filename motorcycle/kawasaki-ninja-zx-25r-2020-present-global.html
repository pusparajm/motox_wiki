Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the ZX25R make?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R is rated at 50 hp (37 kW) @ 15,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 22.9 Nm @ 14,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R is listed with a 249 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 183 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R?

Using the listed 50 hp (37 kW) and 183 kg, the calculated ratio is about 273 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 37.3 kW and 0.20 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the ZX25R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R use?

Front: 37 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R use?

Front: 310 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the ZX25R use?

Front: Brembo 07SU27. Rear: Brembo 07HO36. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the ZX25R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 110 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-25R?

Engine oil capacity is 2.4 L (2.5 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
