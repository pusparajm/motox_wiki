Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX-R125 make?

The Suzuki GSX-R125 is rated at 15 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-R125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.5 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX-R125?

The Suzuki GSX-R125 is listed with a 124 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-R125?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-R125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 134 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX-R125?

Using the listed 15 hp (11 kW) and 134 kg, the calculated ratio is about 112 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.

What tyres does the Suzuki GSX-R125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/80-17 front, 130/70-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki GSX-R125 have ABS?

The Suzuki GSX-R125 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki GSX-R125 use?

Front: 37 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX-R125 use?

Front: 290 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.
