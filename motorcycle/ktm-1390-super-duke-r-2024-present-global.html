Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 1390 Super Duke R make?

The KTM 1390 Super Duke R is rated at 190 hp (140 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 1390 Super Duke R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 145 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 1390 Super Duke R?

The KTM 1390 Super Duke R is listed with a 1,350 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 1390 Super Duke R?

The seat height is listed as 834 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 1390 Super Duke R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 200 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 1390 Super Duke R?

Using the listed 190 hp (140 kW) and 200 kg, the calculated ratio is about 950 hp per tonne (0.71 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 1390 Super Duke R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 190 hp (140 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 834 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 1390 Super Duke R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 141.7 kW and 0.71 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 1390 Super Duke R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 834 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 1390 Super Duke R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.

What tyres does the 1390 Super Duke R use?

Does the KTM 1390 Super Duke R have ABS?
