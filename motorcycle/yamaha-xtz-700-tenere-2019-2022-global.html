How much horsepower (BHP) does the XTZ 700 Tenere make?

How much does the Yamaha XTZ 700 Tenere weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha XTZ 700 Tenere?

Using the listed 72 hp (53 kW) and 204 kg, the calculated ratio is about 353 hp per tonne (0.26 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha XTZ 700 Tenere suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 72 hp (53 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 204 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 875 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha XTZ 700 Tenere A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 53.7 kW and 0.26 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha XTZ 700 Tenere suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 875 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
