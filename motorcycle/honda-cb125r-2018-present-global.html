Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CB125R make?

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CB125R?

The Honda CB125R is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB125R?

The seat height is listed as 816 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB125R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 130 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CB125R?

Using the listed 14.8 hp (11 kW) and 130 kg, the calculated ratio is about 114 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CB125R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 816 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CB125R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.0 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda CB125R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 816 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB125R?

Does the Honda CB125R have ABS?

What brakes does the Honda CB125R use?
