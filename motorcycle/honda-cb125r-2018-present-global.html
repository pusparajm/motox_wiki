Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CB125R make?

The Honda CB125R is rated at 14.8 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB125R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.6 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB125R?

The seat height is listed as 816 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB125R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 130 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CB125R take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.2 L (1.3 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB125R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 10.1 L.
