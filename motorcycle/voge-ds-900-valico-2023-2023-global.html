Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the DS 900 Valico make?

The Voge DS 900 Valico is rated at 95 hp @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Voge DS 900 Valico?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 95 Nm @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Voge DS 900 Valico?

The Voge DS 900 Valico is listed with a 900 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Voge DS 900 Valico?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Voge DS 900 Valico weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 238 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Voge DS 900 Valico?

Using the listed 95 hp and 238 kg, the calculated ratio is about 399 hp per tonne (0.30 kW/kg).

Is the Voge DS 900 Valico suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 95 hp @ 8,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 238 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Voge DS 900 Valico A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.8 kW and 0.30 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Voge DS 900 Valico suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Voge DS 900 Valico?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the DS 900 Valico use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-21 front, 150/70-17 rear.

What suspension does the Voge DS 900 Valico use?

What brakes does the Voge DS 900 Valico use?
