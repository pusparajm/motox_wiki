Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CB650R make?

The Honda CB650R is rated at 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB650R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB650R?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB650R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 208 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CB650R take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB650R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.4 L.
