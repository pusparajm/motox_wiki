Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 650R make?

The Honda CB650R is rated at 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB650R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CB650R?

The Honda CB650R is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB650R?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB650R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 208 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CB650R?

Using the listed 94 hp (70 kW) and 208 kg, the calculated ratio is about 452 hp per tonne (0.34 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CB650R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 208 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CB650R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.1 kW and 0.34 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CB650R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB650R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.4 L.

What tyres does the 650R use?

Does the Honda CB650R have ABS?
