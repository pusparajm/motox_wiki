Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda Grom make?

The Honda Grom is rated at 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) @ 7,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Grom?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 10.5 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda Grom?

The seat height is listed as 761 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Grom weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 104 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda Grom take?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Grom?

Fuel capacity is listed as 6 L.
