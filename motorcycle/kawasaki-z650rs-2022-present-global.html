Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Z650RS make?

The Kawasaki Z650RS is rated at 68 hp (50 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 64 Nm (47 lb-ft) @ 6,700 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Engine oil capacity is 2.4 L (2.5 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z650RS?
