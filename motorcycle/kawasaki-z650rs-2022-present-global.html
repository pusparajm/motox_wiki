Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Z650RS make?

The Kawasaki Z650RS is rated at 68 hp (50 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 64 Nm @ 6,700 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

The Kawasaki Z650RS is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z650RS weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 187 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

Using the listed 68 hp (50 kW) and 187 kg, the calculated ratio is about 364 hp per tonne (0.27 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z650RS suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 68 hp (50 kW) @ 8,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Z650RS A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 50.7 kW and 0.27 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Z650RS suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z650RS?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12 L.

What tyres does the Z650RS use?

Does the Kawasaki Z650RS have ABS?
