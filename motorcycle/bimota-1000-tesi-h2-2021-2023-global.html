What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Bimota 1000 Tesi H2?

Using the listed 228 hp and 214 kg, the calculated ratio is about 1,065 hp per tonne (0.79 kW/kg).

Is the Bimota 1000 Tesi H2 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 228 hp @ 11,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 214 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 840 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Bimota 1000 Tesi H2 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 170.0 kW and 0.79 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Bimota 1000 Tesi H2 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 840 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Bimota 1000 Tesi H2?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Bimota 1000 Tesi H2 use?
