Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati DesertX make?

The Ducati DesertX is rated at 110 hp (81 kW) @ 9,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati DesertX?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 92 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Ducati DesertX?

The seat height is listed as 875 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati DesertX weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 223 kg.

What engine oil does the Ducati DesertX take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati DesertX?

Fuel capacity is listed as 21 L.
