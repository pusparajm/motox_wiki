Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 250 Duke make?

The KTM 250 Duke is rated at 31 hp (23 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 250 Duke?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 24 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 250 Duke?

The KTM 250 Duke is listed with a 249 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 250 Duke?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 250 Duke weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 162 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 250 Duke?

Using the listed 31 hp (23 kW) and 162 kg, the calculated ratio is about 191 hp per tonne (0.14 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 250 Duke suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 250 Duke A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 23.1 kW and 0.14 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the KTM 250 Duke suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 250 Duke?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.4 L.

What tyres does the KTM 250 Duke use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the KTM 250 Duke have ABS?

The KTM 250 Duke is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the KTM 250 Duke use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the KTM 250 Duke use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 230 mm disc.

What engine oil does the KTM 250 Duke take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.7 L (1.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
