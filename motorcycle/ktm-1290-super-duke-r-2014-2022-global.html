Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 1290 Super Duke R make?

What is the seat height of the KTM 1290 Super Duke R?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 1290 Super Duke R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 210 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 1290 Super Duke R?

Using the listed 173.3 hp (129.2 kW) and 210 kg, the calculated ratio is about 825 hp per tonne (0.62 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 1290 Super Duke R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 173.3 hp (129.2 kW) is above the 50 hp limit; 210 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 835 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 1290 Super Duke R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 129.2 kW and 0.62 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 1290 Super Duke R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 835 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
