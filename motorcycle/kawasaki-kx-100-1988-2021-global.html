Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KX 100?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 40 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KX 100?

The Kawasaki KX 100 is listed with a 100 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KX 100?

The seat height is listed as 871 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KX 100 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 71 kg.

Is the Kawasaki KX 100 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 871 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KX 100?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.68 L.

What tyres does the KX 100 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 70/100-19 front, 90/100-16 rear.

What suspension does the Kawasaki KX 100 use?

What brakes does the Kawasaki KX 100 use?

Front: Single disc. Rear: Single disc.

What brake pads does the KX 100 use?

Front: SBS 544RSI. Rear: SBS 592RSI. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the KX 100 use?
