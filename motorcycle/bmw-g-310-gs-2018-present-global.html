Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the G 310 GS make?

The BMW G 310 GS is rated at 34 hp (25 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW G 310 GS?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 28 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW G 310 GS?

The BMW G 310 GS is listed with a 313 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW G 310 GS?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW G 310 GS weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 169.5 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW G 310 GS?

Using the listed 34 hp (25 kW) and 169.5 kg, the calculated ratio is about 201 hp per tonne (0.15 kW/kg).

Is the BMW G 310 GS suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 835 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW G 310 GS A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 25.4 kW and 0.15 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the BMW G 310 GS suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 835 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW G 310 GS?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.

What tyres does the G 310 GS use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 150/70-17 rear.

Does the BMW G 310 GS have ABS?

The BMW G 310 GS is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the BMW G 310 GS use?

What brakes does the BMW G 310 GS use?
