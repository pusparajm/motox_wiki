Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the V-Strom 800 make?

The Suzuki V-Strom 800 is rated at 83 hp (61 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki V-Strom 800?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 78 Nm @ 6,800 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki V-Strom 800?

The Suzuki V-Strom 800 is listed with a 776 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki V-Strom 800?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki V-Strom 800 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 223 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki V-Strom 800?

Using the listed 83 hp (61 kW) and 223 kg, the calculated ratio is about 372 hp per tonne (0.28 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 800 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 83 hp (61 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 223 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 800 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 61.9 kW and 0.28 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 800 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki V-Strom 800?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the V-Strom 800 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 150/70-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki V-Strom 800 have ABS?

The Suzuki V-Strom 800 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki V-Strom 800 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki V-Strom 800 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 260 mm disc.

What chain does the V-Strom 800 use?
