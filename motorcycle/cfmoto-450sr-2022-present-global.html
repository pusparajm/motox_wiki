Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the CFMOTO 450SR make?

The CFMOTO 450SR is rated at 50 hp (37 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the CFMOTO 450SR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 39 Nm @ 7,600 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the CFMOTO 450SR?

The CFMOTO 450SR is listed with a 449 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the CFMOTO 450SR?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the CFMOTO 450SR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 179 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the CFMOTO 450SR?

Using the listed 50 hp (37 kW) and 179 kg, the calculated ratio is about 279 hp per tonne (0.21 kW/kg).

Is the CFMOTO 450SR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the CFMOTO 450SR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 37.3 kW and 0.21 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the CFMOTO 450SR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the CFMOTO 450SR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the CFMOTO 450SR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the CFMOTO 450SR have ABS?

The CFMOTO 450SR is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the CFMOTO 450SR use?

Front: 37 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the CFMOTO 450SR use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.
