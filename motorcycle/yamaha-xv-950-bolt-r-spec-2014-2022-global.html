Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec make?

The Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec is rated at 54 hp @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 80.4 Nm @ 3,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec?

The Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec is listed with a 950 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 246 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec?

Using the listed 54 hp and 246 kg, the calculated ratio is about 220 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 54 hp @ 5,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 246 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 40.3 kW and 0.16 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 690 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13 L.

What tyres does the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/90-19 front, 150/80-16 rear.

What suspension does the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec use?

Front: Telescopic fork 41mm, 120 mm (4.7 inches). Rear: Dual piggyback shocks, 70 mm (2.8 inches).

What brakes does the Yamaha XV 950 Bolt R-Spec use?

Front: Single disc. Wave-type disc. Rear: Single disc. Wave-type disc.
