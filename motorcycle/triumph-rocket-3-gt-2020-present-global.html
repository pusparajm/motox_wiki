Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Triumph Rocket 3 GT make?

The Triumph Rocket 3 GT is rated at 165 hp (123 kW) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Rocket 3 GT?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 221 Nm @ 4,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Rocket 3 GT?

The seat height is listed as 750 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Rocket 3 GT weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 294 kg.

What engine oil does the Triumph Rocket 3 GT take?

Engine oil capacity is 5.5 L (5.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Rocket 3 GT?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.
