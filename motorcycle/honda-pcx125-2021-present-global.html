Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda PCX125 make?

The Honda PCX125 is rated at 12.5 hp (9.3 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda PCX125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.8 Nm (9 lb-ft) @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda PCX125?

The seat height is listed as 764 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda PCX125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 132 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda PCX125 take?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda PCX125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 8.1 L.
