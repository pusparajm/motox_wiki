What is the seat height of the Honda CB650F?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CB650F?

Using the listed 86 hp (64 kW) and 208 kg, the calculated ratio is about 413 hp per tonne (0.31 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CB650F suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 86 hp (64 kW) @ 11,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 208 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CB650F A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 64.1 kW and 0.31 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CB650F suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
