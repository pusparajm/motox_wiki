Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CB650F make?

The Honda CB650F is rated at 86 hp (64 kW) @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB650F?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 60 Nm (44 lb-ft) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB650F?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB650F weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 208 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CB650F take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB650F?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.3 L.
