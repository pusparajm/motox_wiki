What is the engine displacement of the Fantic XEF 450?

What is the seat height of the Fantic XEF 450?

The seat height is listed as 955 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the Fantic XEF 450 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 955 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
