Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Versys 1000 make?

The Kawasaki Versys 1000 is rated at 118 hp (88 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Versys 1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 102 Nm @ 7,700 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Versys 1000?

The Kawasaki Versys 1000 is listed with a 1,043 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Versys 1000?

The seat height is listed as 845 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Versys 1000 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 239 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Versys 1000?

Using the listed 118 hp (88 kW) and 239 kg, the calculated ratio is about 494 hp per tonne (0.37 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Versys 1000 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 118 hp (88 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 239 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 845 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Versys 1000 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 88.0 kW and 0.37 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Versys 1000 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 845 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Versys 1000?

Fuel capacity is listed as 21 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki Versys 1000 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Versys 1000 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Versys 1000 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Versys 1000 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Versys 1000 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Versys 1000?

Engine oil capacity is 4.2 L (4.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
