Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 650 Seiemmezzo make?

The Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo is rated at 60 hp @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 56 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo?

The Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo is listed with a 650 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 214 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo?

Using the listed 60 hp and 214 kg, the calculated ratio is about 280 hp per tonne (0.21 kW/kg).

Is the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 60 hp @ 8,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 214 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 44.7 kW and 0.21 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.

What tyres does the 650 Seiemmezzo use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-18 front, 160/60-17 rear.

What suspension does the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo use?

Front: 43 mm KYB USD fork, 120 mm travel. Rear: KYB monoshock, 118 mm travel.

What brakes does the Moto Morini 650 Seiemmezzo use?

Front: Double 298 mm discs, Brembo, ABS. Rear: Single 255 mm disc, Brembo, ABS.

What brake pads does the 650 Seiemmezzo use?

Front: SBS 674HS. Rear: SBS 671LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
