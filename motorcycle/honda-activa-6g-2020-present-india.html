Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda Activa 6G make?

The Honda Activa 6G is rated at 7.7 hp (5.7 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Activa 6G?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 8.9 Nm @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda Activa 6G?

The seat height is listed as 765 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Activa 6G weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 106 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda Activa 6G take?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Activa 6G?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.3 L.
