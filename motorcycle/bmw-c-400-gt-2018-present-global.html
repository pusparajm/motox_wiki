Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW C 400 GT make?

The BMW C 400 GT is rated at 34 hp (25 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW C 400 GT?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 35 Nm (26 lb-ft) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the BMW C 400 GT?

The seat height is listed as 775 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW C 400 GT weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 206 kg.

What engine oil does the BMW C 400 GT take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.0 L (2.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW C 400 GT?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12.8 L.
