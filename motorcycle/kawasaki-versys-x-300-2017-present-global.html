Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Versys-X 300 make?

The Kawasaki Versys-X 300 is rated at 39 hp (29 kW) @ 11,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Versys-X 300?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 26 Nm @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Versys-X 300?

The Kawasaki Versys-X 300 is listed with a 296 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Versys-X 300?

The seat height is listed as 845 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Versys-X 300 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 180 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Versys-X 300?

Using the listed 39 hp (29 kW) and 180 kg, the calculated ratio is about 217 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Versys-X 300 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 845 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Versys-X 300 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 29.1 kW and 0.16 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki Versys-X 300 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 845 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Versys-X 300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Versys-X 300 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/90-19 front, 130/80-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Versys-X 300 have ABS?
