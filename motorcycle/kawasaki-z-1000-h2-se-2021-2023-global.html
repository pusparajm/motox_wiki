Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Z 1000 H2 SE make?

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE?

The Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE is listed with a 1000 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 221 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE?

Using the listed 140.1 hp and 221 kg, the calculated ratio is about 634 hp per tonne (0.47 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 140.1 hp @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 221 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 104.5 kW and 0.47 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Z 1000 H2 SE suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
