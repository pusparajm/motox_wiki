Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki KX 65 make?

The Kawasaki KX 65 is rated at 12.3 hp (9.0 kW)).

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KX 65?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 9.7 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KX 65?

The Kawasaki KX 65 is listed with a 65 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KX 65?

The seat height is listed as 759 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KX 65 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 60 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki KX 65?

Using the listed 12.3 hp (9.0 kW)) and 60 kg, the calculated ratio is about 205 hp per tonne (0.15 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki KX 65 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Motocross category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki KX 65 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 9.2 kW and 0.15 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki KX 65 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 759 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KX 65?

Fuel capacity is listed as 3.79 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki KX 65 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 60/100-14 front, 80/100-12 rear.

What suspension does the Kawasaki KX 65 use?

Front: 33mm leading axle telescopic fork with 4-way rebound damping, 210 mm (8.3 inches). Rear: 240 mm (9.4 inches).

What brakes does the Kawasaki KX 65 use?

Front: Single disc. Rear: Single disc.

What fork oil does the Kawasaki KX 65 use?

The listed fork oil specification is SAE 10W, 0.25-0.25 litres (0.26-0.27 US quarts) per leg. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Kawasaki KX 65 use?

What rear shock does the Kawasaki KX 65 use?
