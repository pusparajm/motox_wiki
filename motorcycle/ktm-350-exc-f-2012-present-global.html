Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 350 EXC-F make?

The KTM 350 EXC-F is rated at 45 hp (33 kW).

What is the torque of the KTM 350 EXC-F?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 36 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 350 EXC-F?

The KTM 350 EXC-F is listed with a 350 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 350 EXC-F?

The seat height is listed as 960 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 350 EXC-F weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 106 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 350 EXC-F?

Using the listed 45 hp (33 kW) and 106 kg, the calculated ratio is about 425 hp per tonne (0.32 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 350 EXC-F suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 960 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Enduro category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 350 EXC-F A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 33.6 kW and 0.32 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 350 EXC-F suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 960 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 350 EXC-F?

Fuel capacity is listed as 9 L.

What tyres does the KTM 350 EXC-F use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-21 front, 140/80-18 rear.

What suspension does the KTM 350 EXC-F use?

Front: WP XPLOR 48 mm fork, 300 mm travel. Rear: WP XPLOR single shock, 310 mm travel.

What brakes does the KTM 350 EXC-F use?

Front: 260 mm disc. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the KTM 350 EXC-F?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the KTM 350 EXC-F use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2012, 2021–2022, 2024; air gap 390 mm — 2024 (latest). Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the KTM 350 EXC-F use?

The listed fork oil seal is 48.00 × 57.90 × 9.50 mm (WP, NOK) — 2012, 2021–2022, 2024.

What rear shock does the KTM 350 EXC-F use?

The listed rear shock is WP (50/18) Mono Tube PDS Piggyback — 2012; WP (46/18) Mono Tube XPLOR Piggyback — 2021–2022; WP (46/18) Mono Tube XACT PDS Piggyback — 2024.
