Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CB500X make?

The Honda NX500 is rated at 47 hp (35 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda NX500?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 43 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda NX500?

The Honda NX500 is listed with a 471 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda NX500?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda NX500 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 199 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda NX500?

Using the listed 47 hp (35 kW) and 199 kg, the calculated ratio is about 236 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Honda NX500 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda NX500 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.0 kW and 0.18 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda NX500 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda NX500?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.5 L.

What tyres does the CB500X use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Honda NX500 have ABS?

The Honda NX500 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda NX500 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock, preload adjustable.

What brakes does the Honda NX500 use?

Front: 296 mm dual discs, 2-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the CB500X use?

The listed battery code is YTZ8V. Battery rating: 12V 7Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What spark plug does the CB500X use?

The listed spark plug is CPR8EA-9 (NGK). Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the CB500X use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 112 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda NX500?

Engine oil capacity is 3.2 L (3.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
