Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the S 1000 R make?

The BMW S 1000 R is rated at 165 hp (121 kW) @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW S 1000 R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 114 Nm @ 9,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW S 1000 R?

The BMW S 1000 R is listed with a 999 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW S 1000 R?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW S 1000 R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 199 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW S 1000 R?

Using the listed 165 hp (121 kW) and 199 kg, the calculated ratio is about 829 hp per tonne (0.62 kW/kg).

Is the BMW S 1000 R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 165 hp (121 kW) @ 11,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW S 1000 R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 123.0 kW and 0.62 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW S 1000 R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW S 1000 R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16.5 L.

What tyres does the S 1000 R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the BMW S 1000 R have ABS?

The BMW S 1000 R is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the BMW S 1000 R use?

Front: 45 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the BMW S 1000 R use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the S 1000 R use?

Front: EBC FA630HH · SBS 900 · Brembo 07GR90 · Vesrah VD-9070. Rear: EBC FA213 · Brembo 07BB02. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the S 1000 R use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LMAR9D-J. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the S 1000 R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 120 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW S 1000 R?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
