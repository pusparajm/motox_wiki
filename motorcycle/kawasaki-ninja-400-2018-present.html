Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Ninja 400 make?

The Kawasaki Ninja 400 is rated at 44.8 hp (33 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 400?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 38 Nm (28 lb-ft) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja 400?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja 400 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 168 kg.

What engine oil does the Kawasaki Ninja 400 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.0 L (2.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 400?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.
