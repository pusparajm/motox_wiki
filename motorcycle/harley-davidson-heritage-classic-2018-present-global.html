Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Heritage Classic make?

The Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic is rated at 94 hp (70 kW) @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 155 Nm @ 3,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic?

The Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic is listed with a 1,868 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic?

The seat height is listed as 680 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 330 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic?

Using the listed 94 hp (70 kW) and 330 kg, the calculated ratio is about 285 hp per tonne (0.21 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 94 hp (70 kW) @ 4,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 330 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.1 kW and 0.21 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 680 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18.9 L.

What tyres does the Heritage Classic use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 150/80-16 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic use?

Front: 49 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Heritage Classic use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 292 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Heritage Classic use?

Front: EBC FA296HH · Brembo 07HD20. Rear: EBC FA710V · SBS 949 · Brembo 07HD23 · Vesrah VD-9086. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
