How much does the Ducati 959 Panigale Corse weigh?

Using the listed 157 hp (115 kW) and 200 kg, the calculated ratio is about 785 hp per tonne (0.59 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati 959 Panigale Corse suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 157 hp (115 kW) @ 10,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati 959 Panigale Corse A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 117.1 kW and 0.59 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati 959 Panigale Corse suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
