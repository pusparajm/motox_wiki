Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the R 1300 GS make?

The BMW R 1300 GS is rated at 145 hp (107 kW) @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW R 1300 GS?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 149 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW R 1300 GS?

The BMW R 1300 GS is listed with a 1,300 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW R 1300 GS?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW R 1300 GS weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 237 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW R 1300 GS?

Using the listed 145 hp (107 kW) and 237 kg, the calculated ratio is about 612 hp per tonne (0.46 kW/kg).

Is the BMW R 1300 GS suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 145 hp (107 kW) @ 7,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 237 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW R 1300 GS A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 108.1 kW and 0.46 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW R 1300 GS suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW R 1300 GS?

Fuel capacity is listed as 19 L.

What tyres does the R 1300 GS use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-19 front, 170/60-17 rear.

Does the BMW R 1300 GS have ABS?

The BMW R 1300 GS is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the BMW R 1300 GS use?

Front: Telelever, 190 mm travel. Rear: Paralever, 200 mm travel.

What brakes does the BMW R 1300 GS use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 285 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW R 1300 GS?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
