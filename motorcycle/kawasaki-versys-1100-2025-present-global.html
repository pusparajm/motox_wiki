Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Versys 1100 make?

The Kawasaki Versys 1100 is rated at 135 hp (99 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Versys 1100?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 112 Nm @ 7,600 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Versys 1100?

The Kawasaki Versys 1100 is listed with a 1,099 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Versys 1100?

The seat height is listed as 840 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Versys 1100 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 259 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Versys 1100?

Using the listed 135 hp (99 kW) and 259 kg, the calculated ratio is about 521 hp per tonne (0.39 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Versys 1100 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 135 hp (99 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 259 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 840 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Versys 1100 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 100.7 kW and 0.39 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Versys 1100 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 840 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Versys 1100?

Fuel capacity is listed as 21 L.

What tyres does the Versys 1100 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Versys 1100 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Versys 1100 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Versys 1100 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork, electronic adjustment. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Versys 1100 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What chain does the Versys 1100 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 118 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Versys 1100?

Engine oil capacity is 4.2 L (4.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
