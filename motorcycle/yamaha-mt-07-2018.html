Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha MT-07 make?

The Yamaha MT-07 is rated at 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-07?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 68 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-07?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha MT-07 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 184 kg.

What engine oil does the Yamaha MT-07 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.6 L (2.7 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha MT-07?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.
