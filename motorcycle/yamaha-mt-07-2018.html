Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the MT-07 make?

The Yamaha MT-07 is rated at 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-07?

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha MT-07?

The Yamaha MT-07 is listed with a 689 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-07?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha MT-07 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 184 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha MT-07?

Using the listed 74 hp (55 kW) and 184 kg, the calculated ratio is about 402 hp per tonne (0.30 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha MT-07 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 74 hp (55 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 805 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha MT-07 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 55.2 kW and 0.30 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha MT-07 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 805 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
