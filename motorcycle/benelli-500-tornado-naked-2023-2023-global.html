Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 500 Tornado Naked make?

The Benelli 500 Tornado Naked is rated at 47.6 hp @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 46 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked?

The Benelli 500 Tornado Naked is listed with a 500 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked?

The seat height is listed as 784 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 199 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked?

Using the listed 47.6 hp and 199 kg, the calculated ratio is about 239 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked qualifies at 47.6 hp @ 8,500 rpm, 199 kg and 784 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.5 kW and 0.18 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 784 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the 500 Tornado Naked use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-ZR17 front, 160/60-ZR17 rear.

What suspension does the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked use?

Front: Upside-down forks Ø 50 mm with rebound brake adjustable, 125 mm (4.9 inches). Rear: Swing arm with lateral shock absorber with spring preload adjustment and hydraulic rebound brake adjustable, 131 mm (5.2 inches).

What brakes does the Benelli 500 Tornado Naked use?

Front: Double disc. ABS. Rear: Single disc. ABS.

What brake pads does the 500 Tornado Naked use?

Front: SBS 762HS. Rear: SBS 832LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
