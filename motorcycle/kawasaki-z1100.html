Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Z1100 make?

The Kawasaki Z1100 is rated at 136 hp (100 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z1100?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 105 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z1100?

The Kawasaki Z1100 is listed with a 1,099 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z1100?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z1100 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 221 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z1100?

Using the listed 136 hp (100 kW) and 221 kg, the calculated ratio is about 615 hp per tonne (0.46 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z1100 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 136 hp (100 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 221 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Z1100 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 101.4 kW and 0.46 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Z1100 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z1100?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Z1100 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Z1100 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Z1100 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Z1100 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Z1100 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What chain does the Z1100 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Z1100?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
