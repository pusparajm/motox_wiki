Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG make?

The Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG is rated at 9.5 hp (7 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 9.7 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 150 kg.

What engine oil does the Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L at service; 1.1 L dry and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Bajaj Freedom 125 CNG?

Fuel capacity is listed as 2 kg CNG + 2 L petrol.
