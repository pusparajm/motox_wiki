Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the GSX1300R make?

The Suzuki GSX1300R is rated at 197 hp (145 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 155 Nm @ 7,200 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

The Suzuki GSX1300R is listed with a 1,340 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX1300R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 266 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

Using the listed 197 hp (145 kW) and 266 kg, the calculated ratio is about 741 hp per tonne (0.55 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki GSX1300R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 197 hp (145 kW) @ 9,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 266 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 805 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Sport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki GSX1300R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 146.9 kW and 0.55 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki GSX1300R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 805 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 21 L.

What tyres does the GSX1300R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki GSX1300R have ABS?

The Suzuki GSX1300R is listed with Dual-channel ABS (later models).

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX1300R use?

What battery does the GSX1300R use?

What brake pads does the GSX1300R use?
