Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX1300R make?

The Suzuki GSX1300R is rated at 197 hp (145 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 155 Nm (114 lb-ft) @ 7,200 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX1300R?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX1300R weigh?

What engine oil does the Suzuki GSX1300R take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
