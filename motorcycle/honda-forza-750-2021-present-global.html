Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Forza 750 make?

The Honda Forza 750 is rated at 58 hp (43 kW) @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Forza 750?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 69 Nm @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Forza 750?

The Honda Forza 750 is listed with a 745 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Forza 750?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Forza 750 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 224 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Forza 750?

Using the listed 58 hp (43 kW) and 224 kg, the calculated ratio is about 259 hp per tonne (0.19 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Forza 750 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 58 hp (43 kW) @ 6,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 224 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda Forza 750 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 43.3 kW and 0.19 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda Forza 750 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Forza 750?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.2 L.

What tyres does the Forza 750 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-15 rear.

Does the Honda Forza 750 have ABS?

The Honda Forza 750 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Forza 750 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Honda Forza 750 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the Forza 750 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ12S. Battery rating: 12V 11Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What chain does the Forza 750 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 114 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Forza 750?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
