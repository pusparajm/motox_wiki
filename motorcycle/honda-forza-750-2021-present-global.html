Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda Forza 750 make?

The Honda Forza 750 is rated at 58 hp (43 kW) @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Forza 750?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 69 Nm @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Forza 750?

The Honda Forza 750 is listed with a 745 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Forza 750?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Forza 750 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 224 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Forza 750?

Using the listed 58 hp (43 kW) and 224 kg, the calculated ratio is about 259 hp per tonne (0.19 kW/kg).

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Forza 750?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.2 L.

What tyres does the Honda Forza 750 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-15 rear.

Does the Honda Forza 750 have ABS?

The Honda Forza 750 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Forza 750 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Honda Forza 750 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What engine oil does the Honda Forza 750 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
