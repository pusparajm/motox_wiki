What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CMX 500 Rebel?

Using the listed 45.6 hp and 191 kg, the calculated ratio is about 239 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CMX 500 Rebel suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda CMX 500 Rebel qualifies at 45.6 hp @ 8,500 rpm, 191 kg and 690 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda CMX 500 Rebel A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 34.0 kW and 0.18 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda CMX 500 Rebel suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 690 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
