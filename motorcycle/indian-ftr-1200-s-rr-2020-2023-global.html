Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the FTR 1200 S RR make?

The Indian FTR 1200 S RR is rated at 120 hp @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Indian FTR 1200 S RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 118 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Indian FTR 1200 S RR?

The Indian FTR 1200 S RR is listed with a 1200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Indian FTR 1200 S RR?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Indian FTR 1200 S RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 233 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian FTR 1200 S RR?

Using the listed 120 hp and 233 kg, the calculated ratio is about 515 hp per tonne (0.38 kW/kg).

Is the Indian FTR 1200 S RR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 120 hp @ 8,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 233 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian FTR 1200 S RR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 89.5 kW and 0.38 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian FTR 1200 S RR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What brake pads does the FTR 1200 S RR use?

Front: SBS 900. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
