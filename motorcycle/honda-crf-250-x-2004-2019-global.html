Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CRF 250 X?

The Honda CRF 250 X is listed with a 250 cc engine.

What battery does the CRF 250 X use?

The listed battery code is YTX5L-BS. Alternative listed codes: YTZ7S. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the CRF 250 X use?

Front: SBS 694SI. Rear: SBS 777SI. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What fork oil does the Honda CRF 250 X use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2004, 2014; quantity 340 cc — 2004, 2014. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Honda CRF 250 X use?

The listed fork oil seal is 47.00 × 58.00 × 10.00 mm (SHOWA, NOK) — 2004, 2014.

What rear shock does the Honda CRF 250 X use?

The listed rear shock is Showa (50/16) Mono Tube Piggyback — 2004, 2014.
