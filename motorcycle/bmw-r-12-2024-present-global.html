Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the R 12 make?

The BMW R 12 is rated at 95 hp (70 kW) @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW R 12?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 110 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW R 12?

The BMW R 12 is listed with a 1,170 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW R 12?

The seat height is listed as 755 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW R 12 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 227 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW R 12?

Using the listed 95 hp (70 kW) and 227 kg, the calculated ratio is about 419 hp per tonne (0.31 kW/kg).

Is the BMW R 12 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 95 hp (70 kW) @ 6,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 227 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW R 12 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.8 kW and 0.31 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW R 12 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 755 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW R 12?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the R 12 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/90-19 front, 150/80-16 rear.

Does the BMW R 12 have ABS?

The BMW R 12 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the BMW R 12 use?

Front: 45 mm inverted fork. Rear: Paralever single shock.

What brakes does the BMW R 12 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 265 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW R 12?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
