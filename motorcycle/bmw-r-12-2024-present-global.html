Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW R 12 make?

The BMW R 12 is rated at 95 hp (70 kW) @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW R 12?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 110 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the BMW R 12?

The seat height is listed as 755 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW R 12 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 227 kg.

What engine oil does the BMW R 12 take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW R 12?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.
