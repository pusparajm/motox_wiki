Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CB750 Hornet make?

The Honda CB750 Hornet is rated at 90.5 hp (67.5 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB750 Hornet?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 75 Nm @ 7,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CB750 Hornet?

The Honda CB750 Hornet is listed with a 755 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB750 Hornet?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB750 Hornet weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 190 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CB750 Hornet?

Using the listed 90.5 hp (67.5 kW) and 190 kg, the calculated ratio is about 476 hp per tonne (0.36 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CB750 Hornet suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 90.5 hp (67.5 kW) @ 9,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CB750 Hornet A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 67.5 kW and 0.36 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CB750 Hornet suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB750 Hornet?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.2 L.

What tyres does the Honda CB750 Hornet use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Honda CB750 Hornet have ABS?

The Honda CB750 Hornet is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Honda CB750 Hornet use?

Front: 41 mm inverted Showa fork. Rear: Single shock, preload adjustable.

What brakes does the Honda CB750 Hornet use?

Front: 296 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.
