Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide make?

The Harley-Davidson Street Glide is rated at 93 hp (69 kW) @ 5,020 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 158 Nm @ 3,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

The Harley-Davidson Street Glide is listed with a 1,868 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

The seat height is listed as 715 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 375 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Using the listed 93 hp (69 kW) and 375 kg, the calculated ratio is about 248 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Fuel capacity is listed as 22.7 L.

What tyres does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/60-19 front, 180/55-18 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Street Glide is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide use?

Front: 49 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide use?
