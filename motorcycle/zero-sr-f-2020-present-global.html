Is the Zero SR/F suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 110 hp (82 kW) @ 5,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 223 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Zero SR/F A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 82.0 kW and 0.37 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Zero SR/F suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 787 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Zero SR/F?

What tyres does the Zero SR/F use?

Does the Zero SR/F have ABS?

What brakes does the Zero SR/F use?
