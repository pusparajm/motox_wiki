Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 390 Adventure make?

The KTM 390 Adventure is rated at 43 hp (32 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 390 Adventure?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 37 Nm (27 lb-ft) @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the KTM 390 Adventure?

The seat height is listed as 855 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 390 Adventure weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 172 kg.

What engine oil does the KTM 390 Adventure take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.7 L (1.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 390 Adventure?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14.5 L.
