Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the RSV4 make?

The Aprilia RSV4 is rated at 217 hp (160 kW) @ 13,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia RSV4?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 125 Nm @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia RSV4?

The Aprilia RSV4 is listed with a 1,099 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia RSV4?

The seat height is listed as 845 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia RSV4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 202 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia RSV4?

Using the listed 217 hp (160 kW) and 202 kg, the calculated ratio is about 1,074 hp per tonne (0.80 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia RSV4 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 217 hp (160 kW) @ 13,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 202 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 845 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia RSV4 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 161.8 kW and 0.80 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Aprilia RSV4 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 845 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia RSV4?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.9 L.

What tyres does the RSV4 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 200/55-17 rear.

Does the Aprilia RSV4 have ABS?

The Aprilia RSV4 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Aprilia RSV4 use?

Front: 43 mm Öhlins inverted fork. Rear: Öhlins single shock.

What brakes does the Aprilia RSV4 use?

Front: 330 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the RSV4 use?

Front: SBS 706HS. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the RSV4 use?

What chain does the RSV4 use?
