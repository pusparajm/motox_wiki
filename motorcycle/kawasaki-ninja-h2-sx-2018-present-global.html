Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX make?

The Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX is rated at 197 hp (147 kW) @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 137 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX?

The Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX is listed with a 998 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 267 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX?

Using the listed 197 hp (147 kW) and 267 kg, the calculated ratio is about 738 hp per tonne (0.55 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 197 hp (147 kW) @ 11,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 267 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 835 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 146.9 kW and 0.55 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 835 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX?

Fuel capacity is listed as 19 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja H2 SX use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.
