Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the ZX10R make?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R is rated at 203 hp (149 kW) @ 13,200 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 114.9 Nm @ 11,400 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R is listed with a 998 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 207 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

Using the listed 203 hp (149 kW) and 207 kg, the calculated ratio is about 981 hp per tonne (0.73 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 203 hp (149 kW) @ 13,200 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 207 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 835 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 151.4 kW and 0.73 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 835 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the ZX10R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R use?

Front: 43 mm inverted Balance Free Fork. Rear: Balance Free Rear Cushion.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R use?

Front: 330 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the ZX10R use?

Front: Brembo 07BB37. Rear: Brembo 07HO59. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the ZX10R use?

The listed spark plug is NGK SILMAR9B9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the ZX10R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 114 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
