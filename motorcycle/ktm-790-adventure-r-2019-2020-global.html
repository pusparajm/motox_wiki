What is the seat height of the KTM 790 Adventure R?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 790 Adventure R?

Using the listed 95 hp and 212 kg, the calculated ratio is about 448 hp per tonne (0.33 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 790 Adventure R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 95 hp @ 8,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 212 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 880 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 790 Adventure R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.8 kW and 0.33 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 790 Adventure R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 880 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
