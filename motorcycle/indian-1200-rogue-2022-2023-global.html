Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 1200 Rogue make?

The Indian 1200 Rogue is rated at 100 hp @ 8,100 rpm.

What is the torque of the Indian 1200 Rogue?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 97 Nm @ 5,900 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Indian 1200 Rogue?

The Indian 1200 Rogue is listed with a 1200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Indian 1200 Rogue?

The seat height is listed as 649 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Indian 1200 Rogue weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 247 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian 1200 Rogue?

Using the listed 100 hp and 247 kg, the calculated ratio is about 405 hp per tonne (0.30 kW/kg).

Is the Indian 1200 Rogue suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 100 hp @ 8,100 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 247 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian 1200 Rogue A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 74.6 kW and 0.30 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian 1200 Rogue suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 649 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What brake pads does the 1200 Rogue use?

Front: SBS 674. Rear: SBS 675. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
