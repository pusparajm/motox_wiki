Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the SYM ADX 125 make?

The SYM ADX 125 is rated at 12.5 hp (9.3 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the SYM ADX 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.5 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the SYM ADX 125?

The SYM ADX 125 is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the SYM ADX 125?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the SYM ADX 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 136 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the SYM ADX 125?

Using the listed 12.5 hp (9.3 kW) and 136 kg, the calculated ratio is about 92 hp per tonne (0.07 kW/kg).

Is the SYM ADX 125 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the SYM ADX 125 qualifies at 12.5 hp (9.3 kW) @ 8,500 rpm, 136 kg and 795 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the SYM ADX 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 9.3 kW and 0.07 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the SYM ADX 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the SYM ADX 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.5 L.

What tyres does the SYM ADX 125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-13 front, 130/70-13 rear.

Does the SYM ADX 125 have ABS?

The SYM ADX 125 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the SYM ADX 125 use?

Front: 37 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the SYM ADX 125 use?

Front: 260 mm disc. Rear: 230 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the SYM ADX 125?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
