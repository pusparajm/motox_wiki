Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the MT-25 make?

The Yamaha MT-25 is rated at 35.5 hp (26 kW) @ 12,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-25?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 23.6 Nm @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha MT-25?

The Yamaha MT-25 is listed with a 249 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-25?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha MT-25 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 169 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha MT-25?

Using the listed 35.5 hp (26 kW) and 169 kg, the calculated ratio is about 210 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha MT-25 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Yamaha MT-25 qualifies at 35.5 hp (26 kW) @ 12,000 rpm, 169 kg and 780 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Yamaha MT-25 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 26.5 kW and 0.16 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha MT-25 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha MT-25?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the MT-25 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 140/70-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha MT-25 have ABS?

The Yamaha MT-25 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha MT-25 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha MT-25 use?

What battery does the MT-25 use?

What spark plug does the MT-25 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LMAR8A-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the MT-25 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 pitch. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L drain; 2.1 L with filter; 2.4 L dry and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
