Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 502C make?

The Benelli 502C is rated at 47.6 hp (35 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli 502C?

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli 502C?

The Benelli 502C is listed with a 500 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli 502C?

The seat height is listed as 750 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benelli 502C weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 217 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benelli 502C?

Using the listed 47.6 hp (35 kW) and 217 kg, the calculated ratio is about 219 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Benelli 502C suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 217 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Benelli 502C A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.5 kW and 0.16 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benelli 502C suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 750 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli 502C?

Fuel capacity is listed as 21.5 L.

What tyres does the 502C use?

Does the Benelli 502C have ABS?

What suspension does the Benelli 502C use?

What brakes does the Benelli 502C use?
