Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Ninja 1000SX make?

The Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX is rated at 142 hp (105 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 111 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX?

The Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX is listed with a 1,043 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 235 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX?

Using the listed 142 hp (105 kW) and 235 kg, the calculated ratio is about 604 hp per tonne (0.45 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 142 hp (105 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 235 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 105.9 kW and 0.45 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX?

Fuel capacity is listed as 19 L.

What tyres does the Ninja 1000SX use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What battery does the Ninja 1000SX use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Ninja 1000SX use?

Front: EBC FA379HH · SBS 806 · Brembo 07SU27 · Vesrah VD-355. Rear: EBC FA192 · SBS 687 · Brembo 07KA16 · Vesrah VD-434. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Ninja 1000SX use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9EIA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the Ninja 1000SX use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 1000SX?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
