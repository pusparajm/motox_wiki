Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the S 1000 RR make?

The BMW S 1000 RR is rated at 199 hp (146 kW) @ 13,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 113 Nm @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW S 1000 RR?

The BMW S 1000 RR is listed with a 999 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW S 1000 RR?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW S 1000 RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 208 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Using the listed 199 hp (146 kW) and 208 kg, the calculated ratio is about 957 hp per tonne (0.71 kW/kg).

Is the BMW S 1000 RR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 199 hp (146 kW) @ 13,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 208 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW S 1000 RR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 148.4 kW and 0.71 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW S 1000 RR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.5 L.

What tyres does the S 1000 RR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the BMW S 1000 RR have ABS?
