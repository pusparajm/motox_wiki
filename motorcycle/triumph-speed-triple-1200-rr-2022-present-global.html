Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Speed Triple 1200 RR make?

The Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR is rated at 177 hp (132 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 125 Nm @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR?

The Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR is listed with a 1,160 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 199 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR?

Using the listed 177 hp (132 kW) and 199 kg, the calculated ratio is about 889 hp per tonne (0.66 kW/kg).

Is the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 177 hp (132 kW) @ 10,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Sport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 132.0 kW and 0.66 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.5 L.

What tyres does the Speed Triple 1200 RR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR have ABS?

The Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR use?

Front: 43 mm Öhlins semi-active fork. Rear: Öhlins semi-active shock.

What brakes does the Triumph Speed Triple 1200 RR use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, Brembo Stylema radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Speed Triple 1200 RR use?

Front: SBS 901HS. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the Speed Triple 1200 RR use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 120 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.
