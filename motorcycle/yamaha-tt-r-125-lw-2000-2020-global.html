Is the Yamaha TT-R 125 LW suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 805 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha TT-R 125 LW A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 7.2 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha TT-R 125 LW suitable for shorter riders?
