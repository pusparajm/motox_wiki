Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Gixxer SF 250 make?

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250?

The Suzuki Gixxer SF 250 is listed with a 249 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 161 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250?

Using the listed 26.5 hp (19.8 kW) and 161 kg, the calculated ratio is about 165 hp per tonne (0.12 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 19.8 kW and 0.12 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki Gixxer SF 250?
