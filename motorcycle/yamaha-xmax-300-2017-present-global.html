Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the XMAX 300 make?

The Yamaha XMAX 300 is rated at 28 hp (20 kW) @ 7,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha XMAX 300?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 29 Nm @ 5,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha XMAX 300?

The Yamaha XMAX 300 is listed with a 292 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha XMAX 300?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha XMAX 300 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 183 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha XMAX 300?

Using the listed 28 hp (20 kW) and 183 kg, the calculated ratio is about 153 hp per tonne (0.11 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha XMAX 300 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Yamaha XMAX 300 qualifies at 28 hp (20 kW) @ 7,250 rpm, 183 kg and 795 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Yamaha XMAX 300 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 20.9 kW and 0.11 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha XMAX 300 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha XMAX 300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13 L.

What tyres does the XMAX 300 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-15 front, 140/70-14 rear.

Does the Yamaha XMAX 300 have ABS?

The Yamaha XMAX 300 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha XMAX 300 use?

Front: 33 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Yamaha XMAX 300 use?

Front: 267 mm disc. Rear: 245 mm disc.

What battery does the XMAX 300 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ8V. Battery rating: 12V 7Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the XMAX 300 use?

Front: EBC SFA705 · Brembo 07YA52. Rear: EBC SFA706 · Brembo 07YA53. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the XMAX 300 use?

The listed spark plug is LMAR8A-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha XMAX 300?

Engine oil capacity is 1.5 L (1.6 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
