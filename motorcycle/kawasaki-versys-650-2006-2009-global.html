Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Versys 650 make?

The Kawasaki Versys 650 is rated at 64 hp (47 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 61 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

The Kawasaki Versys 650 is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

The seat height is listed as 840 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Versys 650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 208 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

Using the listed 64 hp (47 kW) and 208 kg, the calculated ratio is about 308 hp per tonne (0.23 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Versys 650 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 64 hp (47 kW) @ 8,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 208 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 840 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Versys 650 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 47.7 kW and 0.23 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Versys 650 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 840 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

Fuel capacity is listed as 19 L.

What tyres does the Versys 650 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Versys 650 have ABS?
