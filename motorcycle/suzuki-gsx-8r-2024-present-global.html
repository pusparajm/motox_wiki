Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the GSX-8R make?

The Suzuki GSX-8R is rated at 82 hp (61 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-8R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 78 Nm @ 6,800 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX-8R?

The Suzuki GSX-8R is listed with a 776 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-8R?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-8R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 205 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX-8R?

Using the listed 82 hp (61 kW) and 205 kg, the calculated ratio is about 400 hp per tonne (0.30 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki GSX-8R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 82 hp (61 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 205 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki GSX-8R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 61.1 kW and 0.30 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki GSX-8R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-8R?
