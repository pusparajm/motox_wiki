Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the KLX 230 SE make?

The Kawasaki KLX 230 SE is rated at 19 hp (14 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 19.5 Nm @ 6,400 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE?

The Kawasaki KLX 230 SE is listed with a 230 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE?

The seat height is listed as 880 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 134 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE?

Using the listed 19 hp (14 kW) and 134 kg, the calculated ratio is about 142 hp per tonne (0.11 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 880 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 14.2 kW and 0.11 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 880 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.5 L.

What tyres does the KLX 230 SE use?

Does the Kawasaki KLX 230 SE have ABS?
