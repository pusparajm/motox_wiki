Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the RS 457 make?

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia RS 457?

The Aprilia RS 457 is listed with a 457 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia RS 457?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia RS 457 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 175 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia RS 457?

Using the listed 47.6 hp (35 kW) and 175 kg, the calculated ratio is about 272 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia RS 457 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia RS 457 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.5 kW and 0.20 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Aprilia RS 457 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
