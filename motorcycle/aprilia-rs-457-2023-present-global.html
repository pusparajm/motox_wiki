Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Aprilia RS 457 make?

The Aprilia RS 457 is rated at 47.6 hp (35 kW) @ 9,400 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia RS 457?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 43.5 Nm (32 lb-ft) @ 6,700 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia RS 457?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia RS 457?
