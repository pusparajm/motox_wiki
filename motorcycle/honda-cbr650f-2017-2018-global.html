Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 650R make?

The Honda CBR650F is rated at 90 hp (67 kW) @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR650F?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 64 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CBR650F?

The Honda CBR650F is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR650F?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR650F weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 213 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CBR650F?

Using the listed 90 hp (67 kW) and 213 kg, the calculated ratio is about 423 hp per tonne (0.32 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CBR650F suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 90 hp (67 kW) @ 11,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 213 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CBR650F A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 67.1 kW and 0.32 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CBR650F suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR650F?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.3 L.

What tyres does the 650R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Honda CBR650F have ABS?

What brakes does the Honda CBR650F use?
