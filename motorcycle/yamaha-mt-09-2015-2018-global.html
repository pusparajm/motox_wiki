Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the MT-09 make?

The Yamaha MT-09 is rated at 115 hp (85 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-09?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 87.5 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha MT-09?

The Yamaha MT-09 is listed with a 847 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-09?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha MT-09 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 193 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha MT-09?

Using the listed 115 hp (85 kW) and 193 kg, the calculated ratio is about 596 hp per tonne (0.44 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha MT-09 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 115 hp (85 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha MT-09 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 85.8 kW and 0.44 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha MT-09 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha MT-09?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the MT-09 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha MT-09 have ABS?

The Yamaha MT-09 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha MT-09 use?

What brakes does the Yamaha MT-09 use?
