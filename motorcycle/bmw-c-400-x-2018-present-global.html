Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW C 400 X make?

The BMW C 400 X is rated at 34 hp (24.8 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW C 400 X?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 35 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW C 400 X?

The BMW C 400 X is listed with a 350 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW C 400 X?

The seat height is listed as 775 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW C 400 X weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 204 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW C 400 X?

Using the listed 34 hp (24.8 kW) and 204 kg, the calculated ratio is about 167 hp per tonne (0.12 kW/kg).

Is the BMW C 400 X suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 204 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW C 400 X A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 25.4 kW and 0.12 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the BMW C 400 X suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 775 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW C 400 X?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12.8 L.

What tyres does the BMW C 400 X use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-15 front, 150/70-14 rear.

Does the BMW C 400 X have ABS?

The BMW C 400 X is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the BMW C 400 X use?

Front: 35 mm telescopic fork, 110 mm travel. Rear: Double-sided swingarm, 112 mm travel.

What brakes does the BMW C 400 X use?

Front: 265 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 265 mm disc, 1-piston caliper.

What is the service interval for the BMW C 400 X?

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW C 400 X?

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L (1.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, API SJ. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
