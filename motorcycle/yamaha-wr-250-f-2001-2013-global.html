Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha WR 250 F?

The Yamaha WR 250 F is listed with a 250 cc engine.

What battery does the WR 250 F use?

The listed battery code is YTZ7S. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the WR 250 F use?

Front: SBS 694SI. Rear: SBS 604SI. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What fork oil does the Yamaha WR 250 F use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2003, 2013; air gap 130 mm — 2003; 132 mm — 2013. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Yamaha WR 250 F use?

The listed fork oil seal is 46.00 × 58.10 × 9.50/11.50 mm (KYB, NOK) — 2003; 48.00 × 58.10 × 8.50/10.50 mm (KYB, NOK) — 2013.
