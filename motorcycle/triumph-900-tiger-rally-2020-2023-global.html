Is the Triumph 900 Tiger Rally suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 107 hp (79 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 223 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810/830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph 900 Tiger Rally A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 79.8 kW and 0.36 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Triumph 900 Tiger Rally suitable for shorter riders?
