Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the TVS Jupiter 125 make?

The TVS Jupiter 125 is rated at 9 hp (6.7 kW) @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the TVS Jupiter 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 10.5 Nm (8 lb-ft) @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the TVS Jupiter 125?

The seat height is listed as 765 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the TVS Jupiter 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 115 kg.

What engine oil does the TVS Jupiter 125 take?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the TVS Jupiter 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.1 L.
