Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW F 800 GS make?

The BMW F 800 GS is rated at 87 hp (64 kW) @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW F 800 GS?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 91 Nm (67 lb-ft) @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the seat height of the BMW F 800 GS?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW F 800 GS weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 227 kg.

What engine oil does the BMW F 800 GS take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.5 L (3.7 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW F 800 GS?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14.5 L.
