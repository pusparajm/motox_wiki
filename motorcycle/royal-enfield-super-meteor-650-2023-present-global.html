How much power does the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650 make?

What is the seat height of the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650?

How much does the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650 weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650?

Using the listed 47 hp (35 kW) and 241 kg, the calculated ratio is about 195 hp per tonne (0.15 kW/kg).

Is the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 241 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.0 kW and 0.15 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Royal Enfield Super Meteor 650 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 740 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
