Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Tuono V4 make?

The Aprilia Tuono V4 is rated at 175 hp (129 kW) @ 11,350 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia Tuono V4?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 121 Nm @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia Tuono V4?

The Aprilia Tuono V4 is listed with a 1,099 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia Tuono V4?

The seat height is listed as 845 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia Tuono V4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 209 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia Tuono V4?

Using the listed 175 hp (129 kW) and 209 kg, the calculated ratio is about 837 hp per tonne (0.62 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia Tuono V4 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 175 hp (129 kW) @ 11,350 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 209 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 845 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia Tuono V4 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 130.5 kW and 0.62 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Aprilia Tuono V4 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 845 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia Tuono V4?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.9 L.

What tyres does the Tuono V4 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 200/55-17 rear.

Does the Aprilia Tuono V4 have ABS?

The Aprilia Tuono V4 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Aprilia Tuono V4 use?

Front: 43 mm Öhlins inverted fork. Rear: Öhlins single shock.

What brakes does the Aprilia Tuono V4 use?

Front: 330 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Tuono V4 use?

Front: EBC FA447HH · SBS 841/901 · Vesrah VD-9031. Rear: EBC FA266 · SBS 157/730 · Vesrah VD-964. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
