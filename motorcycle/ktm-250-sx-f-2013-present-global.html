Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 250 SX-F make?

The KTM 250 SX-F is rated at 48 hp (36 kW).

What is the torque of the KTM 250 SX-F?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 38 Nm.

What is the seat height of the KTM 250 SX-F?

The seat height is listed as 958 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 250 SX-F weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 101 kg.

What engine oil does the KTM 250 SX-F take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 250 SX-F?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7 L.
