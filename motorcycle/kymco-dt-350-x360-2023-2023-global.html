How much horsepower (BHP) does the DT 350 X360 make?

What is the seat height of the Kymco DT 350 X360?

How much does the Kymco DT 350 X360 weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kymco DT 350 X360?

Using the listed 28.6 hp and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 147 hp per tonne (0.11 kW/kg).

Is the Kymco DT 350 X360 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Kymco DT 350 X360 qualifies at 28.6 hp @ 7,500 rpm, 194 kg and 800 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Kymco DT 350 X360 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 21.3 kW and 0.11 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kymco DT 350 X360 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kymco DT 350 X360?
