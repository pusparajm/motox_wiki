Is the Ducati 1103 Panigale V4 SP2 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 215.5 hp @ 13,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati 1103 Panigale V4 SP2 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 160.7 kW and 0.81 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
