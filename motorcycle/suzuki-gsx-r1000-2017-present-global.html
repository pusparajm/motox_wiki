Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the GSX-R1000 make?

The Suzuki GSX-R1000 is rated at 199 hp (146 kW) @ 13,200 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 117.6 Nm @ 10,800 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

The Suzuki GSX-R1000 is listed with a 999.8 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 202 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Using the listed 199 hp (146 kW) and 202 kg, the calculated ratio is about 985 hp per tonne (0.73 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki GSX-R1000 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 199 hp (146 kW) @ 13,200 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 202 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki GSX-R1000 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 148.4 kW and 0.73 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki GSX-R1000 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.

What tyres does the GSX-R1000 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 have ABS?

The Suzuki GSX-R1000 is listed with Motion Track ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted Showa fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, Brembo radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.
