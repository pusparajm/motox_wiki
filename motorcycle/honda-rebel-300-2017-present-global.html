Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda Rebel 300 make?

The Honda Rebel 300 is rated at 27 hp (20 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Rebel 300?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 27 Nm (20 lb-ft) @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda Rebel 300?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Rebel 300 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 164 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda Rebel 300 take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L (1.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Rebel 300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11.2 L.
