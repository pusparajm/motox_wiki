Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Rebel 300 make?

The Honda Rebel 300 is rated at 27 hp (20 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Rebel 300?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 27 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Rebel 300?

The Honda Rebel 300 is listed with a 286 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Rebel 300?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Rebel 300 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 164 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Rebel 300?

Using the listed 27 hp (20 kW) and 164 kg, the calculated ratio is about 165 hp per tonne (0.12 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Rebel 300 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda Rebel 300 qualifies at 27 hp (20 kW) @ 8,000 rpm, 164 kg and 690 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda Rebel 300 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 20.1 kW and 0.12 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda Rebel 300 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 690 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Rebel 300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11.2 L.

What tyres does the Rebel 300 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 150/80-16 rear.

Does the Honda Rebel 300 have ABS?

The Honda Rebel 300 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Rebel 300 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Honda Rebel 300 use?

Front: 296 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the Rebel 300 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ8V. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What chain does the Rebel 300 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 108 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Rebel 300?

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L (1.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
