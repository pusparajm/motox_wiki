It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 105.0 hp (76.6 kW)) is above the 50 hp limit; 249 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Livewire A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 78.3 kW and 0.31 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Livewire suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 762 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
