Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 390 SMC R make?

The KTM 390 SMC R is rated at 44.5 hp (33 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 390 SMC R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 39 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 390 SMC R?

The KTM 390 SMC R is listed with a 399 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 390 SMC R?

The seat height is listed as 890 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 390 SMC R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 154 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 390 SMC R?

Using the listed 44.5 hp (33 kW) and 154 kg, the calculated ratio is about 289 hp per tonne (0.22 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 390 SMC R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 890 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 390 SMC R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 33.2 kW and 0.22 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 390 SMC R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 890 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 390 SMC R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 9 L.

What tyres does the 390 SMC R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the KTM 390 SMC R have ABS?

The KTM 390 SMC R is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the KTM 390 SMC R use?

Front: 43 mm inverted WP fork, 230 mm travel. Rear: WP single shock, 220 mm travel.

What brakes does the KTM 390 SMC R use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the 390 SMC R use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What chain does the 390 SMC R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 118 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the KTM 390 SMC R?

Engine oil capacity is 1.7 L (1.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
