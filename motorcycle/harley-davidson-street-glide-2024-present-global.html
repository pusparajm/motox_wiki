Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Street Glide make?

The Harley-Davidson Street Glide is rated at 107 hp (80 kW) @ 5,020 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 178 Nm @ 3,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

The Harley-Davidson Street Glide is listed with a 1,923 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

The seat height is listed as 715 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 363 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Using the listed 107 hp (80 kW) and 363 kg, the calculated ratio is about 295 hp per tonne (0.22 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Street Glide suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 107 hp (80 kW) @ 5,020 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 363 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Street Glide A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 79.8 kW and 0.22 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Street Glide suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 715 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Fuel capacity is listed as 22.7 L.

What tyres does the Street Glide use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/60-19 front, 180/55-18 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Street Glide is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide use?

Front: 49 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Street Glide use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 300 mm disc.

What is the service interval for the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

The listed service interval is 8,000 km / 12 months. Confirm the schedule for your market and model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Harley-Davidson Street Glide?

Engine oil capacity is 4.7 L (5 US qt) with filter and the recommended grade is SAE 20W-50. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
