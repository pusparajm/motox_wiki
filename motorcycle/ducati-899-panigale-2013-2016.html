Ducati 899 Panigale (2013-2016)

Supersport · 898 cc

The 899 Panigale introduced Ducati's monocoque chassis to the middleweight class, with a 148 hp V-twin.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati 899 Panigale make?

The Ducati 899 Panigale is rated at 148 hp (109 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati 899 Panigale?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 99 Nm (73 lb-ft) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Ducati 899 Panigale?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati 899 Panigale weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 193 kg.

What engine oil does the Ducati 899 Panigale take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati 899 Panigale?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.
