Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Nightster make?

The Harley-Davidson Nightster is rated at 90 hp (67 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Nightster?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 95 Nm @ 5,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Nightster?

The Harley-Davidson Nightster is listed with a 975 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Nightster?

The seat height is listed as 705 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Nightster weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 218 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Nightster?

Using the listed 90 hp (67 kW) and 218 kg, the calculated ratio is about 413 hp per tonne (0.31 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Nightster suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 90 hp (67 kW) @ 7,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 218 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Nightster A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 67.1 kW and 0.31 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Nightster suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 705 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Nightster?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11.7 L.

What tyres does the Nightster use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 150/80-16 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Nightster have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Nightster is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Nightster use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Nightster use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 260 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Nightster use?

Front: Brembo 07HD17. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
