Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Indian Challenger make?

The Indian Challenger is rated at 122 hp (91 kW) @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Indian Challenger?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 178 Nm @ 3,800 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Indian Challenger?

The Indian Challenger is listed with a 1,769 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Indian Challenger?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Indian Challenger weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 365 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian Challenger?

Using the listed 122 hp (91 kW) and 365 kg, the calculated ratio is about 334 hp per tonne (0.25 kW/kg).

Is the Indian Challenger suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 122 hp (91 kW) @ 5,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 365 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian Challenger A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 91.0 kW and 0.25 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian Challenger suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 690 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Indian Challenger?

Fuel capacity is listed as 22.7 L.

What tyres does the Indian Challenger use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/60-19 front, 180/60-16 rear.

Does the Indian Challenger have ABS?

The Indian Challenger is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Indian Challenger use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Indian Challenger use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 298 mm disc.
