Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha MT-125 make?

The Yamaha MT-125 is rated at 14.8 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.5 Nm (8.5 lb-ft) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-125?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.
