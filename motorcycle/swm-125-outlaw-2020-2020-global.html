How much power does the SWM 125 Outlaw make?

What is the engine displacement of the SWM 125 Outlaw?

The SWM 125 Outlaw is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the SWM 125 Outlaw?

The seat height is listed as 720 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the SWM 125 Outlaw weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 150 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the SWM 125 Outlaw?

Using the listed 15 hp and 150 kg, the calculated ratio is about 100 hp per tonne (0.07 kW/kg).

Is the SWM 125 Outlaw suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the SWM 125 Outlaw qualifies at 15 hp @ 10,500 rpm, 150 kg and 720 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the SWM 125 Outlaw A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.2 kW and 0.07 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the SWM 125 Outlaw suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 720 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the SWM 125 Outlaw?

What tyres does the SWM 125 Outlaw use?

What suspension does the SWM 125 Outlaw use?

What brakes does the SWM 125 Outlaw use?
