Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Z900 make?

The Kawasaki Z900 is rated at 125 hp (92 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z900?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 98.6 Nm @ 7,700 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z900?

The Kawasaki Z900 is listed with a 948 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z900?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z900 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 212 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z900?

Using the listed 125 hp (92 kW) and 212 kg, the calculated ratio is about 590 hp per tonne (0.44 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z900 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 125 hp (92 kW) @ 9,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 212 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Z900 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 93.2 kW and 0.44 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Z900 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z900?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Z900 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Z900 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What battery does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Z900?

Engine oil capacity is 3.6 L (3.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

What fork seal size does the Kawasaki Z900 use?

What rear shock does the Kawasaki Z900 use?
