Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the BMW R 1200 S?

The BMW R 1200 S is listed with a 1200 cc engine.

What battery does the R 1200 S use?

The listed battery code is YTX14-BS. Alternative listed codes: GYZ16H, YTX14H-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the R 1200 S use?

Front: SBS 796HS. Rear: SBS 742LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What fork oil does the BMW R 1200 S use?

Listed fork oil data: air gap 42 mm — 2007. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the BMW R 1200 S use?

The listed fork oil seal is 41.00 × 54.00 × 11.00 mm (SHOWA, NOK) — 2007.

What rear shock does the BMW R 1200 S use?

The listed rear shock is Ohlins (46/16) Mono Tube Piggyback — 2007.
