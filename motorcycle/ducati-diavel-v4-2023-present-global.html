Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati Diavel V4 make?

The Ducati Diavel V4 is rated at 168 hp (124 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati Diavel V4?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 126 Nm (93 lb-ft) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Ducati Diavel V4?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati Diavel V4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 234 kg.

What engine oil does the Ducati Diavel V4 take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati Diavel V4?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.
