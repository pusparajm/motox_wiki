Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the SP 125 make?

The Honda SP 125 is rated at 10.8 hp (8.0 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda SP 125?

What is the engine displacement of the Honda SP 125?

The Honda SP 125 is listed with a 124 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda SP 125?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda SP 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 117 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda SP 125?

Using the listed 10.8 hp (8.0 kW) and 117 kg, the calculated ratio is about 92 hp per tonne (0.07 kW/kg).

Is the Honda SP 125 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda SP 125 qualifies at 10.8 hp (8.0 kW) @ 7,500 rpm, 117 kg and 790 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda SP 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 8.1 kW and 0.07 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda SP 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda SP 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 10.5 L.

What tyres does the SP 125 use?
