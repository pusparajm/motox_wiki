Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the YZF-R15 V4 make?

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4?

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 142 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4?

Using the listed 18.4 hp (13.5 kW) and 142 kg, the calculated ratio is about 130 hp per tonne (0.10 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 13.7 kW and 0.10 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R15 V4 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
