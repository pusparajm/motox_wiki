Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Burgman 400 make?

The Suzuki Burgman 400 is rated at 31 hp (23 kW) @ 6,300 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 36 Nm @ 4,900 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

The Suzuki Burgman 400 is listed with a 400 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

The seat height is listed as 755 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki Burgman 400 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 215 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

Using the listed 31 hp (23 kW) and 215 kg, the calculated ratio is about 144 hp per tonne (0.11 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki Burgman 400 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 215 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki Burgman 400 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 23.1 kW and 0.11 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki Burgman 400 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 755 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.5 L.

What tyres does the Burgman 400 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-15 front, 150/70-13 rear.

Does the Suzuki Burgman 400 have ABS?

The Suzuki Burgman 400 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki Burgman 400 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki Burgman 400 use?

Front: 260 mm dual discs, 2-piston calipers. Rear: 210 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Burgman 400 use?

Front: Brembo 07076. Rear: EBC SFA412 · Brembo 07058. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Burgman 400 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR7EIA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

Engine oil capacity is 2.3 L (2.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
