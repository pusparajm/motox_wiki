Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki Burgman 400 make?

The Suzuki Burgman 400 is rated at 31 hp (23 kW) @ 6,300 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 36 Nm (27 lb-ft) @ 4,900 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

The seat height is listed as 755 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki Burgman 400 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 215 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki Burgman 400 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.3 L (2.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki Burgman 400?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.5 L.
