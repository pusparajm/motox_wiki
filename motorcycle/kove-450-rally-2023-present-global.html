Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kove 450 Rally make?

The Kove 450 Rally is rated at 51 hp (38 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kove 450 Rally?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 43 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kove 450 Rally?

The seat height is listed as 920 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kove 450 Rally weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 145 kg.

What engine oil does the Kove 450 Rally take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.4 L without filter; 1.6 L with filter; 1.8 L dry and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, API SG or higher, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kove 450 Rally?

Fuel capacity is listed as 30 L.
