Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda Super Cub C125 make?

The Honda Super Cub C125 is rated at 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Super Cub C125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 10.4 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Super Cub C125?

The Honda Super Cub C125 is listed with a 124 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Super Cub C125?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Super Cub C125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 110 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Super Cub C125?

Using the listed 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) and 110 kg, the calculated ratio is about 89 hp per tonne (0.07 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Super Cub C125 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda Super Cub C125 qualifies at 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) @ 7,500 rpm, 110 kg and 780 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda Super Cub C125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 7.3 kW and 0.07 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda Super Cub C125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Super Cub C125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 3.7 L.

What tyres does the Honda Super Cub C125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 70/90-17 front, 80/90-17 rear.

Does the Honda Super Cub C125 have ABS?

The Honda Super Cub C125 is listed with Single-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Super Cub C125 use?

Front: 26 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Honda Super Cub C125 use?

Front: 220 mm disc. Rear: 110 mm drum.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Super Cub C125?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
