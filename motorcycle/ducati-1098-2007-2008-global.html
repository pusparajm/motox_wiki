Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati 1098?

The Ducati 1098 is listed with a 1098 cc engine.

What battery does the 1098 use?

The listed battery code is YTX14-BS. Alternative listed codes: GYZ16H, YTX14H-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the 1098 use?

Front: SBS 841HS. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What fork oil does the Ducati 1098 use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2008; air gap 120 mm — 2008. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Ducati 1098 use?

The listed fork oil seal is 43.00 × 54.00 × 11.00 mm (SHOWA, NOK) — 2008.

What rear shock does the Ducati 1098 use?

The listed rear shock is Showa (46/14) Mono Tube Piggyback — 2008.
