Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Meteor 350 make?

The Royal Enfield Meteor 350 is rated at 20.2 hp (15 kW) @ 6,100 rpm.

What is the torque of the Royal Enfield Meteor 350?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 27 Nm @ 4,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Royal Enfield Meteor 350?

The Royal Enfield Meteor 350 is listed with a 349 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Royal Enfield Meteor 350?

The seat height is listed as 765 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 191 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Royal Enfield Meteor 350?

Using the listed 20.2 hp (15 kW) and 191 kg, the calculated ratio is about 106 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 qualifies at 20.2 hp (15 kW) @ 6,100 rpm, 191 kg and 765 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 15.1 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 765 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Royal Enfield Meteor 350?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

Does the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 have ABS?
