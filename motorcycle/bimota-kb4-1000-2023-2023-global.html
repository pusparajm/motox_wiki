Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the KB4 1000 make?

The Bimota KB4 1000 is rated at 140 hp @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Bimota KB4 1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 111 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Bimota KB4 1000?

The Bimota KB4 1000 is listed with a 1000 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Bimota KB4 1000?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Bimota KB4 1000 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Bimota KB4 1000?

Using the listed 140 hp and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 722 hp per tonne (0.54 kW/kg).

Is the Bimota KB4 1000 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 140 hp @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Bimota KB4 1000 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 104.4 kW and 0.54 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Bimota KB4 1000 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Bimota KB4 1000?

Fuel capacity is listed as 19.5 L.

What tyres does the KB4 1000 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

What suspension does the Bimota KB4 1000 use?

Front: Öhlins NIX30 43 mm USD fork, 130 mm travel. Rear: Öhlins TTX36 monoshock, 122 mm travel.

What brakes does the Bimota KB4 1000 use?

Front: Double 320 mm discs, Brembo, cornering ABS. Rear: Single 220 mm disc, Brembo, cornering ABS.

What brake pads does the KB4 1000 use?

Front: SBS 841HS. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
