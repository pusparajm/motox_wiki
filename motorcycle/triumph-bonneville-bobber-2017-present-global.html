Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Bonneville Bobber make?

The Triumph Bonneville Bobber is rated at 76 hp (57 kW) @ 6,100 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Bonneville Bobber?

What is the engine displacement of the Triumph Bonneville Bobber?

The Triumph Bonneville Bobber is listed with a 1,200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Bonneville Bobber?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Bonneville Bobber weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 251 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Triumph Bonneville Bobber?

Using the listed 76 hp (57 kW) and 251 kg, the calculated ratio is about 303 hp per tonne (0.23 kW/kg).

Is the Triumph Bonneville Bobber suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 76 hp (57 kW) @ 6,100 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 251 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph Bonneville Bobber A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 56.7 kW and 0.23 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Triumph Bonneville Bobber suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 690 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Bonneville Bobber?
