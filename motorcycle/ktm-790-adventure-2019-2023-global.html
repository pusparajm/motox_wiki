Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 790 Adventure make?

The KTM 790 Adventure is rated at 95 hp (70 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 790 Adventure?

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 790 Adventure?

The KTM 790 Adventure is listed with a 799 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 790 Adventure?

The seat height is listed as 830/850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 790 Adventure weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 189 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 790 Adventure?

Using the listed 95 hp (70 kW) and 189 kg, the calculated ratio is about 503 hp per tonne (0.37 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 790 Adventure suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 95 hp (70 kW) @ 8,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830/850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 790 Adventure A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.8 kW and 0.37 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 790 Adventure suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830/850 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
