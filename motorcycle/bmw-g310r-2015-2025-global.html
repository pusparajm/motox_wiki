Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the G 310 R make?

The BMW G 310 R is rated at 34 hp (25 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW G 310 R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 28 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW G 310 R?

The BMW G 310 R is listed with a 313 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW G 310 R?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW G 310 R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 158.5 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW G 310 R?

Using the listed 34 hp (25 kW) and 158.5 kg, the calculated ratio is about 215 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the BMW G 310 R suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the BMW G 310 R qualifies at 34 hp (25 kW) @ 9,500 rpm, 158.5 kg and 785 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the BMW G 310 R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 25.4 kW and 0.16 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the BMW G 310 R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW G 310 R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.

What tyres does the G 310 R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the BMW G 310 R have ABS?

The BMW G 310 R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What brakes does the BMW G 310 R use?
