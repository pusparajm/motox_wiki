Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati Panigale V4 make?

The Ducati Panigale V4 is rated at 216 hp (159 kW) @ 13,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati Panigale V4?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 120.9 Nm @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Ducati Panigale V4?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati Panigale V4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 191 kg.

What engine oil does the Ducati Panigale V4 take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati Panigale V4?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.
