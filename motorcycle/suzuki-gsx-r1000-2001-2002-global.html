Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the GSX-R1000 make?

The Suzuki GSX-R1000 is rated at 160 hp (119 kW) @ 10,800 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 110 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

The Suzuki GSX-R1000 is listed with a 988 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 168 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Using the listed 160 hp (119 kW) and 168 kg, the calculated ratio is about 952 hp per tonne (0.71 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki GSX-R1000 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 160 hp (119 kW) @ 10,800 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki GSX-R1000 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 119.3 kW and 0.71 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki GSX-R1000 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.

What tyres does the GSX-R1000 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

What suspension does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, 6-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the GSX-R1000 use?

The listed battery code is YTX12-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the GSX-R1000 use?

Front: EBC FA188 · SBS 686 · Brembo 07SU19 · Vesrah VD-349. Rear: EBC FA254 · SBS 715/765/984 · Brembo 07SU25 · Vesrah VD-436. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the GSX-R1000 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9E. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the GSX-R1000 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 120 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
