Is the Aprilia Tuareg 660 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 80 hp (59 kW) @ 9,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 204 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 860 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia Tuareg 660 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 59.7 kW and 0.29 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Aprilia Tuareg 660 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 860 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
