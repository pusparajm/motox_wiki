Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the FTR 1200 R carbon make?

The Indian FTR 1200 R carbon is rated at 120 hp (89 kW) @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 112 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon?

The Indian FTR 1200 R carbon is listed with a 1200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 235 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon?

Using the listed 120 hp (89 kW) and 235 kg, the calculated ratio is about 511 hp per tonne (0.38 kW/kg).

Is the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 120 hp (89 kW) @ 7,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 235 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 89.5 kW and 0.38 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13 L.

What tyres does the FTR 1200 R carbon use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-18 front, 180/55-18 rear.

Does the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon have ABS?

The Indian FTR 1200 R carbon is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Indian FTR 1200 R carbon use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 260 mm disc.

What brake pads does the FTR 1200 R carbon use?

Front: SBS 900. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
