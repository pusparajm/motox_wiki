Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the TNT 600i make?

The Benelli TNT 600i is rated at 82 hp (61 kW) @ 11,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli TNT 600i?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 54.6 Nm @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli TNT 600i?

The Benelli TNT 600i is listed with a 600 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli TNT 600i?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benelli TNT 600i weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 231 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benelli TNT 600i?

Using the listed 82 hp (61 kW) and 231 kg, the calculated ratio is about 355 hp per tonne (0.26 kW/kg).

Is the Benelli TNT 600i suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 82 hp (61 kW) @ 11,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 231 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Benelli TNT 600i A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 61.1 kW and 0.26 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benelli TNT 600i suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli TNT 600i?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the TNT 600i use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Benelli TNT 600i have ABS?

The Benelli TNT 600i is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Benelli TNT 600i use?

Front: 50 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Benelli TNT 600i use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 260 mm disc.

What is the service interval for the Benelli TNT 600i?

The listed service interval is Oil change every 5,000 km (first at 500–1,000 km). Confirm the schedule for your market and model year.

What battery does the TNT 600i use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What spark plug does the TNT 600i use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9E. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Benelli TNT 600i?

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L and the recommended grade is Fully synthetic, API SF or higher. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
