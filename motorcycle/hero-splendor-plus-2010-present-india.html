Is the Hero Splendor Plus suitable for beginners?

Is the Hero Splendor Plus A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 6.0 kW and 0.05 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Hero Splendor Plus suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
