Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 1200 Tiger XRX make?

The Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX is rated at 148 hp (110 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 130 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX?

The Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX is listed with a 1200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX?

The seat height is listed as 850/870 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 245 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX?

Using the listed 148 hp (110 kW) and 245 kg, the calculated ratio is about 604 hp per tonne (0.45 kW/kg).

Is the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 148 hp (110 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 245 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 850/870 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 110.4 kW and 0.45 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850/870 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the 1200 Tiger XRX use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-19 front, 170/60-17 rear.

Does the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX have ABS?

What suspension does the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX use?

What brakes does the Triumph 1200 Tiger XRX use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 282 mm disc.

What brake pads does the 1200 Tiger XRX use?

Front: SBS 900HS. Rear: SBS 611LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
