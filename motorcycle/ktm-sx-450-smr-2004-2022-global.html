Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the KTM SX 450 SMR?

What is the engine displacement of the KTM SX 450 SMR?

The KTM SX 450 SMR is listed with a 450 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM SX 450 SMR?

The seat height is listed as 855 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the KTM SX 450 SMR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 855 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM SX 450 SMR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7 L.

What tyres does the KTM SX 450 SMR use?

What brakes does the KTM SX 450 SMR use?
