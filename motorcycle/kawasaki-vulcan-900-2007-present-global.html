Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Vulcan 900 make?

The Kawasaki Vulcan 900 is rated at 50 hp (37 kW) @ 5,700 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Vulcan 900?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 78 Nm @ 3,700 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Vulcan 900?

The Kawasaki Vulcan 900 is listed with a 903 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Vulcan 900?

The seat height is listed as 680 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 277 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Vulcan 900?

Using the listed 50 hp (37 kW) and 277 kg, the calculated ratio is about 181 hp per tonne (0.13 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 277 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 37.3 kW and 0.13 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 680 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Vulcan 900?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the Vulcan 900 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 180/70-15 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Vulcan 900 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Vulcan 900 use?

Front: 300 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 300 mm disc.

What battery does the Vulcan 900 use?

The listed battery code is YTX12-BS. Battery rating: 12V 10Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Vulcan 900 use?

Front: EBC FA229 · SBS 705 · Vesrah VD-352. Rear: EBC FA231/2V · SBS 886/920 · Vesrah VD-435/2. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Vulcan 900 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CPR7EA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Vulcan 900?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
