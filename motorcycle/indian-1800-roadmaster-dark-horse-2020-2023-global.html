What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian 1800 Roadmaster Dark Horse?

Using the listed 92 hp and 403 kg, the calculated ratio is about 228 hp per tonne (0.17 kW/kg).

Is the Indian 1800 Roadmaster Dark Horse suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 92 hp is above the 50 hp limit; 403 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian 1800 Roadmaster Dark Horse A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 68.6 kW and 0.17 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian 1800 Roadmaster Dark Horse suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 673 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What brake pads does the 1800 Roadmaster Dark Horse use?

Front: SBS 782. Rear: SBS 630. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
