Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 500 Dragster make?

The Italjet 500 Dragster is rated at 43 hp @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Italjet 500 Dragster?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 43 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Italjet 500 Dragster?

The Italjet 500 Dragster is listed with a 500 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Italjet 500 Dragster?

The seat height is listed as 750 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the Italjet 500 Dragster suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 750 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Italjet 500 Dragster?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12 L.

What tyres does the 500 Dragster use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-15 front, 160/60-15 rear.

What suspension does the Italjet 500 Dragster use?

Front: 47 mm USD fork. Rear: Adjustable monoshock.

What brakes does the Italjet 500 Dragster use?

Front: Single 270 mm disc, ABS. Rear: Single 230 mm disc.

What brake pads does the 500 Dragster use?

Front: SBS 841HS. Rear: SBS 730LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
