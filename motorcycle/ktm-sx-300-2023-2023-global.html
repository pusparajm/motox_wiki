Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the KTM SX 300?

The KTM SX 300 is listed with a 300 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM SX 300?

The seat height is listed as 958 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the KTM SX 300 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 958 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM SX 300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.2 L.

What tyres does the SX 300 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 1.60-21 front, 2.15-19 rear.

What suspension does the KTM SX 300 use?

Front: WP XACT-USD, Ø 48 mm, 300 mm (11.8 inches). Rear: WP XACT Monoshock with linkage, 310 mm (12.2 inches).

What brakes does the KTM SX 300 use?

Front: Single disc. Brembo 2-piston floating caliper. Rear: Single disc. Brembo single-piston floating calliper, brake disc.

What brake pads does the SX 300 use?

Front: SBS 671RSI. Rear: SBS 791RSI. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the SX 300 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 pitch, 118 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What fork oil does the KTM SX 300 use?

The listed fork oil specification is SAE 5W, 0.38 litres (0.40 US quarts) per leg. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.
