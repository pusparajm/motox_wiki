Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the KLR650 make?

The Kawasaki KLR650 is rated at 39 hp (29 kW) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KLR650?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 52 Nm @ 4,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KLR650?

The Kawasaki KLR650 is listed with a 652 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KLR650?

The seat height is listed as 870 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KLR650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 207 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki KLR650?

Using the listed 39 hp (29 kW) and 207 kg, the calculated ratio is about 188 hp per tonne (0.14 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki KLR650 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 207 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 870 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki KLR650 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 29.1 kW and 0.14 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki KLR650 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 870 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KLR650?

Fuel capacity is listed as 23 L.

What tyres does the KLR650 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-21 front, 130/80-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki KLR650 have ABS?

The Kawasaki KLR650 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki KLR650 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork, 200 mm travel. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock, 200 mm travel.

What brakes does the Kawasaki KLR650 use?

Front: 300 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the KLR650 use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the KLR650 use?

Front: SBS 602HF. Rear: SBS 586SI. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the KLR650 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 110 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki KLR650?

Engine oil capacity is 2.3 L (2.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
