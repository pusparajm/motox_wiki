Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R make?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R is rated at 197 hp (147 kW) @ 13,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 113.5 Nm (84 lb-ft) @ 11,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 206 kg.

What engine oil does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.
