Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Ninja 250 make?

The Kawasaki Ninja 250 is rated at 39 hp (29 kW) @ 12,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 250?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 23.5 Nm @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja 250?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja 250 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 164 kg.

What engine oil does the Kawasaki Ninja 250 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.0 L (2.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 250?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.
