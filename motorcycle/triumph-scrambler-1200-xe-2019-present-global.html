Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE make?

The Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE is rated at 89 hp (66 kW) @ 7,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 110 Nm (81 lb-ft) @ 4,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE?

The seat height is listed as 870 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 230 kg.

What engine oil does the Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Scrambler 1200 XE?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.
