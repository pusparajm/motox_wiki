Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati Hypermotard 821 make?

The Ducati Hypermotard 821 is rated at 110 hp (81 kW) @ 9,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati Hypermotard 821?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 89 Nm @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati Hypermotard 821?

The Ducati Hypermotard 821 is listed with a 821 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Ducati Hypermotard 821?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati Hypermotard 821 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 204 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Ducati Hypermotard 821?

Using the listed 110 hp (81 kW) and 204 kg, the calculated ratio is about 539 hp per tonne (0.40 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati Hypermotard 821 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 110 hp (81 kW) @ 9,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 204 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati Hypermotard 821 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 82.0 kW and 0.40 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati Hypermotard 821 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati Hypermotard 821?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.

What tyres does the Ducati Hypermotard 821 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Ducati Hypermotard 821 have ABS?

The Ducati Hypermotard 821 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Ducati Hypermotard 821 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Ducati Hypermotard 821 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 245 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Ducati Hypermotard 821?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
