Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha Ténéré 700 make?

The Yamaha Ténéré 700 is rated at 72 hp (53 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha Ténéré 700?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 68 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha Ténéré 700?

The seat height is listed as 875 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha Ténéré 700 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 204 kg.

What engine oil does the Yamaha Ténéré 700 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.6 L (2.7 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha Ténéré 700?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.
