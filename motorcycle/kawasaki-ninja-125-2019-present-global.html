Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Ninja 125 make?

The Kawasaki Ninja 125 is rated at 15 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.7 Nm @ 7,700 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja 125?

The Kawasaki Ninja 125 is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja 125?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 148 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja 125?

Using the listed 15 hp (11 kW) and 148 kg, the calculated ratio is about 101 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 125 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.2 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.

What tyres does the Ninja 125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/80-17 front, 130/70-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja 125 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja 125 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja 125 use?

Front: 37 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja 125 use?

Front: 290 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Ninja 125 use?

Front: EBC FA197 · Brembo 07YA22. Rear: EBC FA197 · Brembo 07YA22. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Ninja 125 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LMAR8G. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the Ninja 125 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 428 O-ring, 122 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 125?

Engine oil capacity is 1.1 L (1.16 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
