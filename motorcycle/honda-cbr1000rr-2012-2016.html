Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 1000RR make?

The Honda CBR1000RR is rated at 178 hp (133 kW) @ 12,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR1000RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 112 Nm @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CBR1000RR?

The Honda CBR1000RR is listed with a 999 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR1000RR?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR1000RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 200 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CBR1000RR?

Using the listed 178 hp (133 kW) and 200 kg, the calculated ratio is about 890 hp per tonne (0.66 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CBR1000RR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 178 hp (133 kW) @ 12,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CBR1000RR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 132.7 kW and 0.66 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CBR1000RR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR1000RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.7 L.

What tyres does the 1000RR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

Does the Honda CBR1000RR have ABS?

The Honda CBR1000RR is listed with Combined ABS optional.

What suspension does the Honda CBR1000RR use?

Front: 43 mm inverted Big Piston Fork. Rear: Pro-Link single shock.

What brakes does the Honda CBR1000RR use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the 1000RR use?

The listed battery code is YTZ7S. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the 1000RR use?

Front: EBC FA447HH · SBS 841/901 · Brembo 07HO50 · Vesrah VD-9031. Rear: EBC FA436 · SBS 834/991 · Brembo 07HO59 · Vesrah VD-174. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the 1000RR use?

The listed spark plug is NGK IMR9E-9HES. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the 1000RR use?

The listed chain or final drive is 530 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda CBR1000RR?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
