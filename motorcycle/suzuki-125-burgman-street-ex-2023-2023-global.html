How much power does the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX make?

What is the seat height of the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX?

How much does the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX?

Using the listed 8.6 hp (6.4 kW) and 110 kg, the calculated ratio is about 78 hp per tonne (0.06 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX qualifies at 8.6 hp (6.4 kW) @ 6,750 rpm, 110 kg and 780 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 6.4 kW and 0.06 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki 125 Burgman Street EX?
