Is the Ducati 1158 Multistrada V4 Rally A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 126.8 kW and 0.49 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
