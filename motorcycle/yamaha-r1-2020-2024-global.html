Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha YZF-R1 make?

The Yamaha YZF-R1 is rated at 200 hp (147 kW) @ 13,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha YZF-R1?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 113 Nm @ 11,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha YZF-R1?

The Yamaha YZF-R1 is listed with a 998 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R1?

The seat height is listed as 860 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R1 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 201 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha YZF-R1?

Using the listed 200 hp (147 kW) and 201 kg, the calculated ratio is about 995 hp per tonne (0.74 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha YZF-R1 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 200 hp (147 kW) @ 13,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 201 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 860 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R1 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 149.1 kW and 0.74 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R1 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 860 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha YZF-R1?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Yamaha YZF-R1 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha YZF-R1 have ABS?

The Yamaha YZF-R1 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha YZF-R1 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork, Öhlins electronic adjustment. Rear: Link-type single shock, Öhlins electronic adjustment.

What brakes does the Yamaha YZF-R1 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What engine oil does the Yamaha YZF-R1 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.9 L (4.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Yamaha YZF-R1 use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2021, 2024; air gap 115 mm — 2021, 2024. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Yamaha YZF-R1 use?

The listed fork oil seal is 43.00 × 55.10 × 9.50/10.50 mm (KYB, NOK) — 2021, 2024.

What rear shock does the Yamaha YZF-R1 use?

The listed rear shock is KYB (40/14) Mono Tube Piggyback — 2021, 2024.
