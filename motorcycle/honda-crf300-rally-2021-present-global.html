Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CRF300 Rally make?

The Honda CRF300 Rally is rated at 27 hp (20 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 26.3 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

The seat height is listed as 885 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CRF300 Rally weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 153 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CRF300 Rally take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.5 L (1.6 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12.8 L.
