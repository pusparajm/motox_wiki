Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CRF300 Rally make?

The Honda CRF300 Rally is rated at 27 hp (20 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 26.3 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

The Honda CRF300 Rally is listed with a 286 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

The seat height is listed as 885 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CRF300 Rally weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 153 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

Using the listed 27 hp (20 kW) and 153 kg, the calculated ratio is about 176 hp per tonne (0.13 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CRF300 Rally suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 885 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CRF300 Rally A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 20.1 kW and 0.13 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda CRF300 Rally suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 885 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CRF300 Rally?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12.8 L.

What tyres does the CRF300 Rally use?

Does the Honda CRF300 Rally have ABS?
