Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CB300R make?

The Honda CB300R is rated at 31 hp (23 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB300R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 27.5 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CB300R?

The Honda CB300R is listed with a 286 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB300R?

The seat height is listed as 799 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB300R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 144 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CB300R?

Using the listed 31 hp (23 kW) and 144 kg, the calculated ratio is about 215 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CB300R suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda CB300R qualifies at 31 hp (23 kW) @ 8,500 rpm, 144 kg and 799 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda CB300R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 23.1 kW and 0.16 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda CB300R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 799 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB300R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 10.1 L.

What tyres does the CB300R use?

Does the Honda CB300R have ABS?
