Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 200 Duke make?

The KTM 200 Duke is rated at 25 hp (19 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 200 Duke?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 19.2 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the KTM 200 Duke?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 200 Duke weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 159 kg.

What engine oil does the KTM 200 Duke take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.7 L (1.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 200 Duke?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.4 L.
