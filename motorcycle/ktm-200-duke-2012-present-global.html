Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 200 Duke make?

The KTM 200 Duke is rated at 25 hp (19 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 200 Duke?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 19.2 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 200 Duke?

The KTM 200 Duke is listed with a 199.5 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 200 Duke?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 200 Duke weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 159 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 200 Duke?

Using the listed 25 hp (19 kW) and 159 kg, the calculated ratio is about 157 hp per tonne (0.12 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 200 Duke suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 200 Duke A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 18.6 kW and 0.12 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the KTM 200 Duke suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 200 Duke?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.4 L.

What tyres does the 200 Duke use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the KTM 200 Duke have ABS?

The KTM 200 Duke is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the KTM 200 Duke use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the KTM 200 Duke use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 230 mm disc.

What battery does the 200 Duke use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the 200 Duke use?

Front: SBS 877HS. Rear: SBS 675LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the 200 Duke use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LKAR8A-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the 200 Duke use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 118 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the KTM 200 Duke?

Engine oil capacity is 1.7 L (1.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
