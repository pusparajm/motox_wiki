Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Sportster S make?

The Harley-Davidson Sportster S is rated at 121 hp (90 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 125 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

The Harley-Davidson Sportster S is listed with a 1,252 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

The seat height is listed as 755 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Sportster S weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 228 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

Using the listed 121 hp (90 kW) and 228 kg, the calculated ratio is about 531 hp per tonne (0.40 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Sportster S suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 121 hp (90 kW) @ 7,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 228 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Sportster S A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 90.2 kW and 0.40 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Sportster S suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 755 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11.8 L.

What tyres does the Sportster S use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 160/70-17 front, 180/70-16 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Sportster S have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Sportster S is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Sportster S use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Sportster S use?

Front: 320 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 260 mm disc.

What is the service interval for the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

The listed service interval is 8,000 km / 12 months. Confirm the schedule for your market and model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Harley-Davidson Sportster S?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L service fill; 4.5 L with filter and the recommended grade is SAE 20W-50 full synthetic. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
