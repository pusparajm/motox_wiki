Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX-8S make?

The Suzuki GSX-8S is rated at 82 hp (61 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-8S?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 78 Nm (58 lb-ft) @ 6,800 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-8S?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-8S weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 202 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki GSX-8S take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-8S?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.
