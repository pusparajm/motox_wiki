Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the F 900 R make?

The BMW F 900 R is rated at 105 hp (77 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW F 900 R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 92 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW F 900 R?

The BMW F 900 R is listed with a 895 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW F 900 R?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW F 900 R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 211 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW F 900 R?

Using the listed 105 hp (77 kW) and 211 kg, the calculated ratio is about 498 hp per tonne (0.37 kW/kg).

Is the BMW F 900 R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 105 hp (77 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 211 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW F 900 R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 78.3 kW and 0.37 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW F 900 R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW F 900 R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13 L.

What tyres does the F 900 R use?

Does the BMW F 900 R have ABS?
