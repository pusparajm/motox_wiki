Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Rocket 3 R make?

The Triumph Rocket 3 R is rated at 165 hp (123 kW) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Rocket 3 R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 221 Nm @ 4,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Triumph Rocket 3 R?

The Triumph Rocket 3 R is listed with a 2,458 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Rocket 3 R?

The seat height is listed as 773 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Rocket 3 R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 291 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Triumph Rocket 3 R?

Using the listed 165 hp (123 kW) and 291 kg, the calculated ratio is about 567 hp per tonne (0.42 kW/kg).

Is the Triumph Rocket 3 R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 165 hp (123 kW) @ 6,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 291 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph Rocket 3 R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 123.0 kW and 0.42 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Triumph Rocket 3 R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 773 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Rocket 3 R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.

What tyres does the Rocket 3 R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 150/80-17 front, 240/50-16 rear.

Does the Triumph Rocket 3 R have ABS?

The Triumph Rocket 3 R is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Triumph Rocket 3 R use?

Front: 47 mm inverted Showa fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Triumph Rocket 3 R use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 300 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Rocket 3 R use?

Front: EBC FA447HH · SBS 841/901 · Brembo 07BB37 · Vesrah VD-9031. Rear: EBC FA630HH · SBS 900 · Brembo 07BB38 · Vesrah VD-9070. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
