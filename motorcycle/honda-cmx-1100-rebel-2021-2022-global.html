Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel make?

The Honda CMX 1100 Rebel is rated at 86 hp (64 kW) @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 98 Nm @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel?

The Honda CMX 1100 Rebel is listed with a 1100 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel?

The seat height is listed as 700 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 223 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel?

Using the listed 86 hp (64 kW) and 223 kg, the calculated ratio is about 386 hp per tonne (0.29 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 86 hp (64 kW) @ 7,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 223 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 64.1 kW and 0.29 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 700 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.6 L.

What tyres does the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/70-18 front, 180/65-16 rear.

Does the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel have ABS?

The Honda CMX 1100 Rebel is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel use?

Front: 330 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 256 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda CMX 1100 Rebel?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
