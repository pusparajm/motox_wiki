Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Forty-Eight make?

The Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight is rated at 66 hp (49 kW) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 96 Nm @ 3,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight?

The Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight is listed with a 1,202 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight?

The seat height is listed as 710 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 252 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight?

Using the listed 66 hp (49 kW) and 252 kg, the calculated ratio is about 262 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 66 hp (49 kW) @ 6,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 252 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 49.2 kW and 0.20 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 710 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.9 L.

What tyres does the Forty-Eight use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 150/80-16 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight use?

Front: 39 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Forty-Eight use?

Front: 300 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 292 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Forty-Eight use?

Front: EBC FA381 · SBS 807 · Brembo 07HD22 · Vesrah VD-9009. Rear: EBC FA387 · SBS 808 · Brembo 07KA21 · Vesrah VD-9010. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Forty-Eight use?

The listed spark plug is NGK DCPR7E. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.
