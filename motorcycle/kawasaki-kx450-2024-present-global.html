Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the KX450 make?

The Kawasaki KX450 is rated at 58 hp (43 kW).

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KX450?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 49 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KX450?

The Kawasaki KX450 is listed with a 449 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KX450?

The seat height is listed as 960 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KX450 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 110 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki KX450?

Using the listed 58 hp (43 kW) and 110 kg, the calculated ratio is about 527 hp per tonne (0.39 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki KX450 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 58 hp (43 kW) is above the 50 hp limit; 960 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Motocross category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki KX450 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 43.3 kW and 0.39 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki KX450 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 960 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KX450?

Fuel capacity is listed as 6.2 L.

What tyres does the KX450 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 80/100-21 front, 120/80-19 rear.

What suspension does the Kawasaki KX450 use?

Front: 49 mm inverted Showa fork, 305 mm travel. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock, 300 mm travel.

What brakes does the Kawasaki KX450 use?

Front: 270 mm disc. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What is the battery rating of the KX450?

The listed battery rating is 12V lithium-ion. The battery code is not yet verified for this entry.

What brake pads does the KX450 use?

Front: Brembo 07BB04. Rear: Brembo 07YA41. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the KX450 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CPR8EB-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.
