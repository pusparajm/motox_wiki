Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the TMAX 560 make?

The Yamaha TMAX 560 is rated at 47 hp (35 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha TMAX 560?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 55.7 Nm @ 5,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha TMAX 560?

The Yamaha TMAX 560 is listed with a 562 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha TMAX 560?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha TMAX 560 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 220 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha TMAX 560?

Using the listed 47 hp (35 kW) and 220 kg, the calculated ratio is about 214 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha TMAX 560 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 220 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha TMAX 560 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.0 kW and 0.16 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha TMAX 560 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha TMAX 560?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the TMAX 560 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-15 front, 160/60-15 rear.

Does the Yamaha TMAX 560 have ABS?

The Yamaha TMAX 560 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha TMAX 560 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha TMAX 560 use?

Front: 282 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 282 mm disc.

What battery does the TMAX 560 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ12S. Battery rating: 12V 11Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the TMAX 560 use?

Front: SBS 198MS. Rear: SBS 169MS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha TMAX 560?

Engine oil capacity is 2.7 L (2.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
