It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 91 hp (67 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 231 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the CFMOTO 800MT A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 67.9 kW and 0.29 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
