Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Z400 make?

The Kawasaki Z400 is rated at 45 hp (33 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z400?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 37 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z400?

The Kawasaki Z400 is listed with a 399 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z400?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z400 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 167 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z400?

Using the listed 45 hp (33 kW) and 167 kg, the calculated ratio is about 269 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z400 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Kawasaki Z400 qualifies at 45 hp (33 kW) @ 10,000 rpm, 167 kg and 785 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Kawasaki Z400 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 33.6 kW and 0.20 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Z400 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z400?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki Z400 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Z400 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Z400 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Z400 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Z400 use?

Front: 310 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the Kawasaki Z400 use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Z400?

Engine oil capacity is 2.0 L (2.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
