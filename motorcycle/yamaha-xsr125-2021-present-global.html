Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha XSR125 make?

The Yamaha XSR125 is rated at 14.8 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha XSR125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.5 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha XSR125?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha XSR125 weigh?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha XSR125?
