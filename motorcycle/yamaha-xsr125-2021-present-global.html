Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the XSR125 make?

The Yamaha XSR125 is rated at 14.8 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha XSR125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.5 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha XSR125?

The Yamaha XSR125 is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha XSR125?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha XSR125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 140 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha XSR125?

Using the listed 14.8 hp (11 kW) and 140 kg, the calculated ratio is about 106 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha XSR125 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha XSR125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.0 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha XSR125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha XSR125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.

What tyres does the XSR125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 140/70-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha XSR125 have ABS?

The Yamaha XSR125 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha XSR125 use?

Front: 37 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha XSR125 use?

Front: 298 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the XSR125 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ4V. Battery rating: 12V 3Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the XSR125 use?

Front: Brembo 07YA28. Rear: Brembo 07KA12. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the XSR125 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 428 O-ring, 126 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha XSR125?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
