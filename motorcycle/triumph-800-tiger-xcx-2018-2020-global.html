Is the Triumph 800 Tiger XCX suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 95 hp @ 9,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 220 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph 800 Tiger XCX A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.8 kW and 0.32 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
