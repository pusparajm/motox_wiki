Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 1290 Super Adventure S make?

The KTM 1290 Super Adventure S is rated at 160 hp (118 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S?

The KTM 1290 Super Adventure S is listed with a 1,301 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S?

The seat height is listed as 849 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 227 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S?

Using the listed 160 hp (118 kW) and 227 kg, the calculated ratio is about 705 hp per tonne (0.53 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 160 hp (118 kW) @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 227 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 849 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 119.3 kW and 0.53 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 849 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 1290 Super Adventure S?
