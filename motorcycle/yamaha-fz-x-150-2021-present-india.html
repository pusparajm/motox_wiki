Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha FZ-X 150 make?

The Yamaha FZ-X 150 is rated at 12.4 hp (9.1 kW) @ 7,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha FZ-X 150?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 13.3 Nm @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha FZ-X 150?

The Yamaha FZ-X 150 is listed with a 149 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha FZ-X 150?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha FZ-X 150 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 139 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha FZ-X 150?

Using the listed 12.4 hp (9.1 kW) and 139 kg, the calculated ratio is about 89 hp per tonne (0.07 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha FZ-X 150 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha FZ-X 150 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 9.2 kW and 0.07 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha FZ-X 150 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha FZ-X 150?

Fuel capacity is listed as 10 L.

What tyres does the Yamaha FZ-X 150 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/80-17 front, 140/60-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha FZ-X 150 have ABS?

The Yamaha FZ-X 150 is listed with Single-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha FZ-X 150 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha FZ-X 150 use?

Front: 282 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha FZ-X 150?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
