Is the Aprilia SR 125 GT suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Aprilia SR 125 GT qualifies at 14.8 hp @ 8,700 rpm, 115 kg and 799 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Aprilia SR 125 GT A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.0 kW and 0.10 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Aprilia SR 125 GT suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 799 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
