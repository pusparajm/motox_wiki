Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha YZF-R125 make?

The Yamaha YZF-R125 is rated at 14.8 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha YZF-R125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.5 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha YZF-R125?

The Yamaha YZF-R125 is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R125?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 142 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha YZF-R125?

Using the listed 14.8 hp (11 kW) and 142 kg, the calculated ratio is about 104 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha YZF-R125 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.0 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha YZF-R125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.

What tyres does the Yamaha YZF-R125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/80-17 front, 140/70-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha YZF-R125 have ABS?

The Yamaha YZF-R125 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha YZF-R125 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha YZF-R125 use?

Front: 292 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha YZF-R125?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
