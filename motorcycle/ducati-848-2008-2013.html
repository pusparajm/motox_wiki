Ducati 848 (2008-2013)

Supersport · 849 cc

The 848 brought Ducati's mid-size superbike formula to a wider audience, with a 134 hp Testastretta V-twin.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati 848 make?

The Ducati 848 is rated at 134 hp (99 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati 848?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 96 Nm @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati 848?

The Ducati 848 is listed with a 849 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Ducati 848?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati 848 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Ducati 848?

Using the listed 134 hp (99 kW) and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 691 hp per tonne (0.52 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati 848 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 134 hp (99 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati 848 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 99.9 kW and 0.52 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati 848 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati 848?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.5 L.

What tyres does the Ducati 848 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Ducati 848 have ABS?

The Ducati 848 is listed with Dual-channel ABS (later models).

What suspension does the Ducati 848 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Ducati 848 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 245 mm disc.

What engine oil does the Ducati 848 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Ducati 848 use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2009; air gap 120 mm — 2009. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Ducati 848 use?

The listed fork oil seal is 43.00 × 54.00 × 11.00 mm (SHOWA, NOK) — 2009.

What rear shock does the Ducati 848 use?

The listed rear shock is Showa (46/14) Mono Tube Piggyback — 2009.
