Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CT125 make?

The Honda Trail 125 is rated at 8.7 hp (6.5 kW) @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Trail 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11 Nm @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Trail 125?

The Honda Trail 125 is listed with a 124 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Trail 125?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Trail 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 118 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Trail 125?

Using the listed 8.7 hp (6.5 kW) and 118 kg, the calculated ratio is about 74 hp per tonne (0.05 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Trail 125 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda Trail 125 qualifies at 8.7 hp (6.5 kW) @ 6,250 rpm, 118 kg and 800 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda Trail 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 6.5 kW and 0.05 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda Trail 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Trail 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.3 L.

What tyres does the CT125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 80/90-17 front, 80/90-17 rear.

Does the Honda Trail 125 have ABS?

The Honda Trail 125 is listed with Single-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Trail 125 use?

Front: 27 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Honda Trail 125 use?

Front: 220 mm disc. Rear: 190 mm disc.

What battery does the CT125 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ5S-BS. Battery rating: 12V 3.5Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Trail 125?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
