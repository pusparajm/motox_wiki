Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Scrambler 900 make?

The Triumph Scrambler 900 is rated at 64 hp (48 kW) @ 7,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Scrambler 900?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 80 Nm @ 3,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Triumph Scrambler 900?

The Triumph Scrambler 900 is listed with a 900 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Scrambler 900?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Scrambler 900 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 223 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Triumph Scrambler 900?

Using the listed 64 hp (48 kW) and 223 kg, the calculated ratio is about 287 hp per tonne (0.21 kW/kg).

Is the Triumph Scrambler 900 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 64 hp (48 kW) @ 7,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 223 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Triumph Scrambler 900 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 47.7 kW and 0.21 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Triumph Scrambler 900 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Scrambler 900?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12 L.

What tyres does the Scrambler 900 use?

Does the Triumph Scrambler 900 have ABS?
