Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the DL 1050 V-Strom DE make?

The Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE is rated at 107 hp (79 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE?

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE?

The Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE is listed with a 1050 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE?

The seat height is listed as 880 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 252 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE?

Using the listed 107 hp (79 kW) and 252 kg, the calculated ratio is about 425 hp per tonne (0.32 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 107 hp (79 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 252 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 880 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 79.8 kW and 0.32 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 880 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the DL 1050 V-Strom DE use?

Does the Suzuki DL 1050 V-Strom DE have ABS?
