Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Interceptor 650 make?

The Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 is rated at 47 hp (35 kW) @ 7,150 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650?

The Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 is listed with a 648 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650?

The seat height is listed as 804 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 218 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650?

Using the listed 47 hp (35 kW) and 218 kg, the calculated ratio is about 216 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 218 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 804 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.0 kW and 0.16 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 804 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650?

Does the Royal Enfield Interceptor 650 have ABS?
