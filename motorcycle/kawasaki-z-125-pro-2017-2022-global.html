Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Z 125 Pro make?

The Kawasaki Z 125 Pro is rated at 14.8 hp @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro?

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro?

The Kawasaki Z 125 Pro is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 102 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro?

Using the listed 14.8 hp and 102 kg, the calculated ratio is about 145 hp per tonne (0.11 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 805 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.0 kW and 0.11 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki Z 125 Pro suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 805 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
