Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Versys 650 make?

The Kawasaki Versys 650 is rated at 69 hp (51 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Versys 650?

The seat height is listed as 845 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.
