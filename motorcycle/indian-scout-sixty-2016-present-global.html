Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Indian Scout Sixty make?

The Indian Scout Sixty is rated at 78 hp (58 kW) @ 7,300 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Indian Scout Sixty?

The Indian Scout Sixty is listed with a 999 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Indian Scout Sixty?

The seat height is listed as 649 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Indian Scout Sixty weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 249 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian Scout Sixty?

Using the listed 78 hp (58 kW) and 249 kg, the calculated ratio is about 313 hp per tonne (0.23 kW/kg).

Is the Indian Scout Sixty suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 78 hp (58 kW) @ 7,300 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 249 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian Scout Sixty A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 58.2 kW and 0.23 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian Scout Sixty suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 649 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Indian Scout Sixty?

Does the Indian Scout Sixty have ABS?
