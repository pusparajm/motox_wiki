Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW F 900 XR make?

The BMW F 900 XR is rated at 105 hp (77 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW F 900 XR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 92 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW F 900 XR?

The BMW F 900 XR is listed with a 895 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW F 900 XR?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW F 900 XR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 219 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW F 900 XR?

Using the listed 105 hp (77 kW) and 219 kg, the calculated ratio is about 479 hp per tonne (0.36 kW/kg).

Is the BMW F 900 XR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 105 hp (77 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 219 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW F 900 XR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 78.3 kW and 0.36 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW F 900 XR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW F 900 XR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.5 L.

What tyres does the BMW F 900 XR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the BMW F 900 XR have ABS?

The BMW F 900 XR is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the BMW F 900 XR use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the BMW F 900 XR use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 265 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW F 900 XR?

Engine oil capacity is 3.5 L (3.7 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
