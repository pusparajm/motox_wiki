Is the Ducati 950 Supersport S suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 110 hp @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 210 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati 950 Supersport S A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 82.0 kW and 0.39 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
