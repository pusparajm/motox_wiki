Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR make?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR is rated at 77 hp (57 kW) @ 14,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 39 Nm @ 13,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR weigh?

What engine oil does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.8 L (3.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4RR?
