Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the V-Strom 650 make?

The Suzuki V-Strom 650 is rated at 70 hp (52 kW) @ 8,800 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 62 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

The Suzuki V-Strom 650 is listed with a 645 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 216 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

Using the listed 70 hp (52 kW) and 216 kg, the calculated ratio is about 324 hp per tonne (0.24 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 650 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 70 hp (52 kW) @ 8,800 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 216 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 835 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 650 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 52.2 kW and 0.24 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 650 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 835 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the V-Strom 650 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 150/70-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 have ABS?

What brakes does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 use?
