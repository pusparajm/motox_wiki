Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 make?

The Suzuki V-Strom 650 is rated at 70 hp (52 kW) @ 8,800 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 62 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

The Suzuki V-Strom 650 is listed with a 645 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 216 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

Using the listed 70 hp (52 kW) and 216 kg, the calculated ratio is about 324 hp per tonne (0.24 kW/kg).

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki V-Strom 650?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 150/70-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 have ABS?

The Suzuki V-Strom 650 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 use?

Front: 43 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, 2-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What engine oil does the Suzuki V-Strom 650 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.9 L (3.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
