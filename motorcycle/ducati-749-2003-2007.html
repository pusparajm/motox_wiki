Ducati 749 (2003-2007)

Supersport · 748 cc

Ducati's 749 was the smaller brother of the 999, with a torquey Testastretta V-twin and razor-sharp handling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 749 make?

The Ducati 749 is rated at 103 hp (76 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati 749?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 76 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati 749?

The Ducati 749 is listed with a 748 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Ducati 749?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati 749 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 199 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Ducati 749?

Using the listed 103 hp (76 kW) and 199 kg, the calculated ratio is about 518 hp per tonne (0.39 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati 749 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 103 hp (76 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati 749 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 76.8 kW and 0.39 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati 749 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati 749?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.5 L.

What tyres does the 749 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.
