Ducati 749 (2003-2007)

Supersport · 748 cc

Ducati's 749 was the smaller brother of the 999, with a torquey Testastretta V-twin and razor-sharp handling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati 749 make?

The Ducati 749 is rated at 103 hp (76 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati 749?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 76 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Ducati 749?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati 749 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 199 kg.

What engine oil does the Ducati 749 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati 749?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.5 L.
