Is the Yamaha WR 450 F Efi suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 955 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Enduro category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha WR 450 F Efi A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 36.5 kW and 0.31 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
