Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the Husqvarna TC 50 Mini?

The Husqvarna TC 50 Mini is listed with a 50 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Husqvarna TC 50 Mini?

The seat height is listed as 558 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the Husqvarna TC 50 Mini suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 558 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Husqvarna TC 50 Mini?
