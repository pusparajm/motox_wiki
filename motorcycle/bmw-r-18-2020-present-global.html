Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW R 18 make?

The BMW R 18 is rated at 91 hp (67 kW) @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW R 18?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 158 Nm (117 lb-ft) @ 3,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the BMW R 18?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW R 18 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 345 kg.

What engine oil does the BMW R 18 take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.5 L (4.8 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW R 18?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.
