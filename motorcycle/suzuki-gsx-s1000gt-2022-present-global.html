Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX-S1000GT make?

The Suzuki GSX-S1000GT is rated at 150 hp (110 kW) @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-S1000GT?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 106 Nm (78 lb-ft) @ 9,250 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-S1000GT?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-S1000GT weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 226 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki GSX-S1000GT take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-S1000GT?

Fuel capacity is listed as 19 L.
