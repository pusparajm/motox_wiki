Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Z1000 make?

The Kawasaki Z1000 is rated at 142 hp (104 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 111 Nm @ 7,300 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Z1000?

The Kawasaki Z1000 is listed with a 1,043 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z1000?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z1000 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 221 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Z1000?

Using the listed 142 hp (104 kW) and 221 kg, the calculated ratio is about 643 hp per tonne (0.48 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Z1000 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 142 hp (104 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 221 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Z1000 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 105.9 kW and 0.48 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Z1000 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z1000?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Z1000 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Z1000 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Z1000 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Z1000 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Z1000 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What battery does the Z1000 use?

The listed battery code is YT12A-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Z1000 use?

Front: EBC FA379HH · SBS 806 · Brembo 07SU27 · Vesrah VD-355. Rear: EBC FA192 · SBS 687 · Brembo 07KA16 · Vesrah VD-434. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Z1000 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9EIA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the Z1000 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Z1000?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
