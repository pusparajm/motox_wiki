Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the PCX 150 make?

The Honda PCX 150 is rated at 15 hp @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda PCX 150?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 14 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda PCX 150?

The Honda PCX 150 is listed with a 150 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda PCX 150?

The seat height is listed as 764 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda PCX 150 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 130 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda PCX 150?

Using the listed 15 hp and 130 kg, the calculated ratio is about 115 hp per tonne (0.09 kW/kg).

Is the Honda PCX 150 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda PCX 150 qualifies at 15 hp @ 8,500 rpm, 130 kg and 764 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda PCX 150 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.2 kW and 0.09 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda PCX 150 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 764 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda PCX 150?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.95 L.
