Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special make?

The Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special is rated at 150 hp (112 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 128 Nm @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special?

The Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special is listed with a 1250 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 245 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special?

Using the listed 150 hp (112 kW) and 245 kg, the calculated ratio is about 612 hp per tonne (0.46 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 150 hp (112 kW) @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 245 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 111.9 kW and 0.46 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special?

Fuel capacity is listed as 21.2 L.

What tyres does the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-19 front, 170/60-17 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special use?

Front: 47 mm inverted fork, semi-active. Rear: Single shock, semi-active.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson RA 1250 Pan America Special use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 280 mm disc.
