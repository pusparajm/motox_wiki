Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CRF1100 make?

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L?

The Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L is listed with a 1,084 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L?

The seat height is listed as 850/870 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 226 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L?

Using the listed 101 hp (75 kW) and 226 kg, the calculated ratio is about 447 hp per tonne (0.33 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 101 hp (75 kW) @ 7,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 226 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 850/870 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 75.3 kW and 0.33 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850/870 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L?
