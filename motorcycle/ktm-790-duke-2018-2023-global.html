Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 790 Duke make?

The KTM 790 Duke is rated at 105 hp (77 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 790 Duke?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 87 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 790 Duke?

The KTM 790 Duke is listed with a 799 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 790 Duke?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 790 Duke weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 169 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 790 Duke?

Using the listed 105 hp (77 kW) and 169 kg, the calculated ratio is about 621 hp per tonne (0.46 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 790 Duke suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 105 hp (77 kW) @ 9,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 790 Duke A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 78.3 kW and 0.46 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 790 Duke suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 790 Duke?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the KTM 790 Duke use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the KTM 790 Duke have ABS?

The KTM 790 Duke is listed with Cornering ABS.
