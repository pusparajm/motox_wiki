Is the Zero DSR/X suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 100 hp (75 kW) is above the 50 hp limit; 247 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 828 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Zero DSR/X A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 74.6 kW and 0.30 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Zero DSR/X suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 828 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Zero DSR/X?

What tyres does the DSR/X use?
