Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Monster 1200 make?

The Ducati Monster 1200 is rated at 150 hp (110 kW) @ 9,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati Monster 1200?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 126 Nm @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati Monster 1200?

The Ducati Monster 1200 is listed with a 1,198 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Ducati Monster 1200?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati Monster 1200 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 211 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Ducati Monster 1200?

Using the listed 150 hp (110 kW) and 211 kg, the calculated ratio is about 711 hp per tonne (0.53 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati Monster 1200 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 150 hp (110 kW) @ 9,250 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 211 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati Monster 1200 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 111.9 kW and 0.53 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati Monster 1200 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati Monster 1200?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Monster 1200 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the Ducati Monster 1200 have ABS?

The Ducati Monster 1200 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Ducati Monster 1200 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Ducati Monster 1200 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 245 mm disc.
