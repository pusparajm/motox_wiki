Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati Streetfighter V4 make?

The Ducati Streetfighter V4 is rated at 208 hp (153 kW) @ 13,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati Streetfighter V4?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 123 Nm (91 lb-ft) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Ducati Streetfighter V4?

The seat height is listed as 845 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati Streetfighter V4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 201 kg.

What engine oil does the Ducati Streetfighter V4 take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati Streetfighter V4?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.
