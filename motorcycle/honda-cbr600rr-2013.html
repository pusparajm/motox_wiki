Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CBR600RR make?

The Honda CBR600RR is rated at 118 hp (88 kW) @ 13,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR600RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 66 Nm @ 11,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CBR600RR?

The Honda CBR600RR is listed with a 599 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR600RR?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR600RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CBR600RR?

Using the listed 118 hp (88 kW) and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 608 hp per tonne (0.45 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CBR600RR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 118 hp (88 kW) @ 13,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CBR600RR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 88.0 kW and 0.45 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CBR600RR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR600RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.

What tyres does the Honda CBR600RR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Honda CBR600RR have ABS?

The Honda CBR600RR is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda CBR600RR use?

Front: 41 mm inverted Big Piston Fork. Rear: Pro-Link single shock.

What brakes does the Honda CBR600RR use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc, 1-piston caliper.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda CBR600RR?

Engine oil capacity is 3.2 L (3.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Honda CBR600RR use?

What fork seal size does the Honda CBR600RR use?
