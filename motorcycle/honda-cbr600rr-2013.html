Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CBR600RR make?

The Honda CBR600RR is rated at 118 hp (88 kW) @ 13,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR600RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 66 Nm @ 11,250 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR600RR?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR600RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CBR600RR take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.2 L (3.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR600RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.
