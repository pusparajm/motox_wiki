Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 200 Dragster make?

The Italjet 200 Dragster is rated at 17.5 hp @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Italjet 200 Dragster?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 15.5 Nm @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Italjet 200 Dragster?

The Italjet 200 Dragster is listed with a 200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Italjet 200 Dragster?

The seat height is listed as 770 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Italjet 200 Dragster weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 133 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Italjet 200 Dragster?

Using the listed 17.5 hp and 133 kg, the calculated ratio is about 132 hp per tonne (0.10 kW/kg).

Is the Italjet 200 Dragster suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Italjet 200 Dragster qualifies at 17.5 hp @ 8,000 rpm, 133 kg and 770 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Italjet 200 Dragster A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 13.0 kW and 0.10 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Italjet 200 Dragster suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 770 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Italjet 200 Dragster?

Fuel capacity is listed as 9 L.

What tyres does the 200 Dragster use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-12 front, 140/60-13 rear.

What suspension does the Italjet 200 Dragster use?

Front: ISS single-arm with Paioli hydro-pneumatic adjustable shock. Rear: Single hydro-pneumatic adjustable shock.

What brakes does the Italjet 200 Dragster use?

Front: Single 200 mm disc, Brembo, ABS. Rear: Single 190 mm disc, Brembo, ABS.

What brake pads does the 200 Dragster use?

Front: SBS 151CT. Rear: SBS 157CT. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
