How much does the Harley-Davidson RH 1250 Sportster S weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson RH 1250 Sportster S?

Using the listed 121 hp (90 kW) and 228 kg, the calculated ratio is about 531 hp per tonne (0.40 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson RH 1250 Sportster S suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 121 hp (90 kW) @ 7,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 228 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson RH 1250 Sportster S A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 90.2 kW and 0.40 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson RH 1250 Sportster S suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 755 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
