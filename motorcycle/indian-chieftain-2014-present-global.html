Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Chieftain make?

The Indian Chieftain is rated at 100 hp (75 kW) @ 5,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Indian Chieftain?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 169 Nm @ 3,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Indian Chieftain?

The Indian Chieftain is listed with a 1,890 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Indian Chieftain?

The seat height is listed as 660 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Indian Chieftain weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 371 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Indian Chieftain?

Using the listed 100 hp (75 kW) and 371 kg, the calculated ratio is about 270 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the Indian Chieftain suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 100 hp (75 kW) @ 5,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 371 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Indian Chieftain A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 74.6 kW and 0.20 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Indian Chieftain suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 660 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Indian Chieftain?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20.8 L.

What tyres does the Chieftain use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 180/65-16 rear.

Does the Indian Chieftain have ABS?

What suspension does the Indian Chieftain use?

What brakes does the Indian Chieftain use?

What battery does the Chieftain use?
