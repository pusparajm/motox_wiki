Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Leoncino 800 make?

The Benelli Leoncino 800 is rated at 76 hp (56 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli Leoncino 800?

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli Leoncino 800?

The Benelli Leoncino 800 is listed with a 754 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli Leoncino 800?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benelli Leoncino 800 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 205 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benelli Leoncino 800?

Using the listed 76 hp (56 kW) and 205 kg, the calculated ratio is about 371 hp per tonne (0.28 kW/kg).

Is the Benelli Leoncino 800 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 76 hp (56 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 205 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Benelli Leoncino 800 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 56.7 kW and 0.28 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benelli Leoncino 800 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli Leoncino 800?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the Leoncino 800 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Benelli Leoncino 800 have ABS?

What brakes does the Benelli Leoncino 800 use?
