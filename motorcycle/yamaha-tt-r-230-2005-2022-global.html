Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Yamaha TT-R 230?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 18.6 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha TT-R 230?

The Yamaha TT-R 230 is listed with a 230 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha TT-R 230?

The seat height is listed as 870 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha TT-R 230 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 114 kg.

Is the Yamaha TT-R 230 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 870 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha TT-R 230?

Fuel capacity is listed as 8 L.

What tyres does the TT-R 230 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/100-18 59R front, 100/100-18 59R rear.

What suspension does the Yamaha TT-R 230 use?

Front: Telescopic fork, 240 mm (9.4 inches). Rear: Swingarm (link suspension), 221 mm (8.7 inches).

What brakes does the Yamaha TT-R 230 use?

Front: Hydraulic single disc brake. Rear: Mechanical leading trailing drum brake.

What battery does the TT-R 230 use?

The listed battery code is YTX5L-BS. Alternative listed codes: YTZ7S. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the TT-R 230 use?

Front: SBS 611SI. Rear: SBS 2034. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the TT-R 230 use?

The listed spark plug is 18 N·m (13 lb·ft). Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the TT-R 230 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 pitch, 106 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha TT-R 230?

Engine oil capacity is 1.1 L and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
