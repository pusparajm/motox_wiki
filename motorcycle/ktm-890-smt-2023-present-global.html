Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 890 SMT make?

The KTM 890 SMT is rated at 105 hp (77 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 890 SMT?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 100 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 890 SMT?

The KTM 890 SMT is listed with a 889 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 890 SMT?

The seat height is listed as 860 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 890 SMT weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 890 SMT?

Using the listed 105 hp (77 kW) and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 541 hp per tonne (0.40 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 890 SMT suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 105 hp (77 kW) @ 8,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 860 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 890 SMT A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 78.3 kW and 0.40 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 890 SMT suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 860 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 890 SMT?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.5 L.

What tyres does the 890 SMT use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the KTM 890 SMT have ABS?

The KTM 890 SMT is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the KTM 890 SMT use?

Front: 43 mm inverted WP APEX fork. Rear: WP APEX single shock.

What brakes does the KTM 890 SMT use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the 890 SMT use?

The listed battery code is YTZ10S. Battery rating: 12V 8.6Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What chain does the 890 SMT use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the KTM 890 SMT?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
