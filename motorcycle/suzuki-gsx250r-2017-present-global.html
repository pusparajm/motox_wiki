Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the GSX250R make?

The Suzuki GSX250R is rated at 25 hp (18 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX250R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 23.4 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX250R?

The Suzuki GSX250R is listed with a 248 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX250R?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX250R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 181 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX250R?

Using the listed 25 hp (18 kW) and 181 kg, the calculated ratio is about 138 hp per tonne (0.10 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki GSX250R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki GSX250R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 18.6 kW and 0.10 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki GSX250R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX250R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the GSX250R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-17 front, 140/70-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki GSX250R have ABS?

The Suzuki GSX250R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki GSX250R use?

Front: 37 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX250R use?

Front: 290 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the GSX250R use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the GSX250R use?

Front: EBC FA226 · Brembo 07HO30. Rear: EBC FA496 · SBS 881 · Brembo 07HO53 · Vesrah VD-147/2. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the GSX250R use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CPR7EA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the GSX250R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Suzuki GSX250R?

Engine oil capacity is 2.2 L (2.3 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
