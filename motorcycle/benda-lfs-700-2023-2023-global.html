Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Benda LFS 700 make?

The Benda LFS 700 is rated at 94 hp @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benda LFS 700?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Benda LFS 700?

The Benda LFS 700 is listed with a 700 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benda LFS 700?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benda LFS 700 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 208 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benda LFS 700?

Using the listed 94 hp and 208 kg, the calculated ratio is about 452 hp per tonne (0.34 kW/kg).

Is the Benda LFS 700 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 94 hp @ 11,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 208 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Benda LFS 700 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.1 kW and 0.34 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benda LFS 700 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benda LFS 700?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.

What tyres does the Benda LFS 700 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

What suspension does the Benda LFS 700 use?

Front: Inverted telescopic fork. Rear: Monoshock with air preload adjustment.

What brakes does the Benda LFS 700 use?

Front: Double 320 mm discs, four-piston calipers, ABS. Rear: Single 260 mm disc, two-piston caliper, ABS.

What battery does the Benda LFS 700 use?

The listed battery code is MG14L-BS-C. Battery rating: 12V 14Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.
