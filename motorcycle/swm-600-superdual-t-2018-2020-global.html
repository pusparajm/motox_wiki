Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 600 Superdual T make?

The SWM 600 Superdual T is rated at 54 hp @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the SWM 600 Superdual T?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 55 Nm @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the SWM 600 Superdual T?

The SWM 600 Superdual T is listed with a 600 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the SWM 600 Superdual T?

The seat height is listed as 898 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the SWM 600 Superdual T weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 187 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the SWM 600 Superdual T?

Using the listed 54 hp and 187 kg, the calculated ratio is about 289 hp per tonne (0.22 kW/kg).

Is the SWM 600 Superdual T suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 54 hp @ 7,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 898 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the SWM 600 Superdual T A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 40.3 kW and 0.22 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the SWM 600 Superdual T suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 898 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the SWM 600 Superdual T?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.

What tyres does the 600 Superdual T use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 140/80-17 rear.

What suspension does the SWM 600 Superdual T use?

Front: 45 mm USD fork, 210 mm travel. Rear: Sachs progressive monoshock, 220 mm travel.

What brakes does the SWM 600 Superdual T use?

Front: Single 300 mm disc, Brembo, ABS. Rear: Single 220 mm disc, Brembo.

What brake pads does the 600 Superdual T use?

Front: SBS 674HS. Rear: SBS 675LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
