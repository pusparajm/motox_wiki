Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Road Glide make?

The Harley-Davidson Road Glide is rated at 93 hp (69 kW) @ 5,020 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Road Glide?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 158 Nm @ 3,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Road Glide?

The Harley-Davidson Road Glide is listed with a 1,868 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Road Glide?

The seat height is listed as 715 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Road Glide weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 385 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Road Glide?

Using the listed 93 hp (69 kW) and 385 kg, the calculated ratio is about 242 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Road Glide suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 93 hp (69 kW) @ 5,020 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 385 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Road Glide A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 69.4 kW and 0.18 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Road Glide suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 715 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Road Glide?

Fuel capacity is listed as 22.7 L.

What tyres does the Road Glide use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/60-19 front, 180/55-18 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Road Glide have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Road Glide is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Road Glide use?

Front: 49 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Road Glide use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 292 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Road Glide use?

Front: EBC FA409 · SBS 830 · Vesrah VD-9025. Rear: EBC FA409 · SBS 830 · Vesrah VD-9025. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
