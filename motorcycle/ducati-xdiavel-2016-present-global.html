Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati XDiavel make?

The Ducati XDiavel is rated at 160 hp (118 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati XDiavel?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 128 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati XDiavel?

The Ducati XDiavel is listed with a 1,262 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Ducati XDiavel?

The seat height is listed as 755 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati XDiavel weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 245 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Ducati XDiavel?

Using the listed 160 hp (118 kW) and 245 kg, the calculated ratio is about 653 hp per tonne (0.49 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati XDiavel suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 160 hp (118 kW) @ 9,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 245 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati XDiavel A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 119.3 kW and 0.49 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati XDiavel suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 755 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati XDiavel?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18 L.

What tyres does the Ducati XDiavel use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 240/45-17 rear.

Does the Ducati XDiavel have ABS?

The Ducati XDiavel is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Ducati XDiavel use?

Front: 50 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Ducati XDiavel use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 265 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Ducati XDiavel?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
