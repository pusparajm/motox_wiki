Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki W800 make?

The Kawasaki W800 is rated at 52 hp (38 kW) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki W800?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 62 Nm @ 4,800 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki W800?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki W800 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 226 kg.

What engine oil does the Kawasaki W800 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki W800?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.
