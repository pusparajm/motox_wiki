Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the W800 make?

The Kawasaki W800 is rated at 52 hp (38 kW) @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki W800?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 62 Nm @ 4,800 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki W800?

The Kawasaki W800 is listed with a 773 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki W800?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki W800 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 226 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki W800?

Using the listed 52 hp (38 kW) and 226 kg, the calculated ratio is about 230 hp per tonne (0.17 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki W800 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 52 hp (38 kW) @ 6,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 226 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki W800 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 38.8 kW and 0.17 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki W800 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki W800?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the W800 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 100/90-18 front, 130/80-18 rear.

Does the Kawasaki W800 have ABS?

The Kawasaki W800 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki W800 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Kawasaki W800 use?

Front: 320 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 270 mm disc.

What battery does the W800 use?

What brake pads does the W800 use?

Front: Brembo 07KA18. Rear: Brembo 07YA22. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the W800 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR8E. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the W800 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 110 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki W800?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
