Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 450 SX-F make?

The KTM 450 SX-F is rated at 63 hp (47 kW).

What is the torque of the KTM 450 SX-F?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 49 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 450 SX-F?

The KTM 450 SX-F is listed with a 450 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 450 SX-F?

The seat height is listed as 958 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 450 SX-F weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 104 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 450 SX-F?

Using the listed 63 hp (47 kW) and 104 kg, the calculated ratio is about 606 hp per tonne (0.45 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 450 SX-F suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 63 hp (47 kW) is above the 50 hp limit; 958 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Motocross category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 450 SX-F A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 47.0 kW and 0.45 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 450 SX-F suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 958 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 450 SX-F?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7 L.

What tyres does the KTM 450 SX-F use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 80/100-21 front, 110/90-19 rear.

What suspension does the KTM 450 SX-F use?

Front: WP XACT 48 mm fork, 310 mm travel. Rear: WP XACT single shock, 300 mm travel.

What brakes does the KTM 450 SX-F use?

What fork oil does the KTM 450 SX-F use?
