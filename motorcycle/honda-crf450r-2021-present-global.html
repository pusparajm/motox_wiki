Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CRF450R make?

The Honda CRF450R is rated at 57 hp (42 kW).

What is the torque of the Honda CRF450R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 47 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CRF450R?

The Honda CRF450R is listed with a 450 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CRF450R?

The seat height is listed as 960 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CRF450R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 111 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CRF450R?

Using the listed 57 hp (42 kW) and 111 kg, the calculated ratio is about 514 hp per tonne (0.38 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CRF450R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 57 hp (42 kW) is above the 50 hp limit; 960 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Motocross category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CRF450R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 42.5 kW and 0.38 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CRF450R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 960 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CRF450R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 6.3 L.

What tyres does the CRF450R use?
