Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the GTV 300 make?

The Vespa GTV 300 is rated at 24 hp (18 kW) @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Vespa GTV 300?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 26 Nm @ 5,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Vespa GTV 300?

The Vespa GTV 300 is listed with a 278 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Vespa GTV 300?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Vespa GTV 300 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 175 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Vespa GTV 300?

Using the listed 24 hp (18 kW) and 175 kg, the calculated ratio is about 137 hp per tonne (0.10 kW/kg).

Is the Vespa GTV 300 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Vespa GTV 300 qualifies at 24 hp (18 kW) @ 8,250 rpm, 175 kg and 790 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Vespa GTV 300 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 17.9 kW and 0.10 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Vespa GTV 300 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Vespa GTV 300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 8.5 L.

What tyres does the GTV 300 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-12 front, 130/70-12 rear.

Does the Vespa GTV 300 have ABS?

The Vespa GTV 300 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Vespa GTV 300 use?

Front: 32 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Vespa GTV 300 use?

Front: 220 mm disc. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Vespa GTV 300?

Engine oil capacity is 1.0 L (1.05 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
