Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Z125 make?

The Kawasaki Z125 is rated at 15 hp (11 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Z125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.7 Nm @ 7,700 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Z125?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Z125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 146 kg.

What engine oil does the Kawasaki Z125 take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.1 L (1.16 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Z125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11 L.
