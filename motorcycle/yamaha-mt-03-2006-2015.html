Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the MT-03 make?

The Yamaha MT-03 is rated at 45 hp (33 kW) @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-03?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 56.4 Nm @ 5,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha MT-03?

The Yamaha MT-03 is listed with a 660 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-03?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha MT-03 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 192 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha MT-03?

Using the listed 45 hp (33 kW) and 192 kg, the calculated ratio is about 234 hp per tonne (0.17 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha MT-03 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 805 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha MT-03 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 33.6 kW and 0.17 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha MT-03 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 805 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha MT-03?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the MT-03 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

What suspension does the Yamaha MT-03 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha MT-03 use?

Front: 298 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 245 mm disc.

What brake pads does the MT-03 use?

Front: Brembo 07YA34. Rear: Brembo 07BB02. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the MT-03 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 106 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha MT-03?

Engine oil capacity is 2.4 L (2.5 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
