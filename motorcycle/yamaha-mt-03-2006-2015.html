Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha MT-03 make?

The Yamaha MT-03 is rated at 45 hp (33 kW) @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-03?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 56.4 Nm @ 5,250 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-03?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha MT-03 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 192 kg.

What engine oil does the Yamaha MT-03 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.4 L (2.5 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha MT-03?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.
