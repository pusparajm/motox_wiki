Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Z400 make?

The Suzuki DR-Z400SM is rated at 39 hp (29 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki DR-Z400SM?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 39 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki DR-Z400SM?

The Suzuki DR-Z400SM is listed with a 398 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki DR-Z400SM?

The seat height is listed as 890 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki DR-Z400SM weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 146 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki DR-Z400SM?

Using the listed 39 hp (29 kW) and 146 kg, the calculated ratio is about 267 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki DR-Z400SM suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 890 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki DR-Z400SM A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 29.1 kW and 0.20 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki DR-Z400SM suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 890 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki DR-Z400SM?

Fuel capacity is listed as 10 L.

What tyres does the Z400 use?
