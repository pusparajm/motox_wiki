Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 125 Varez make?

The SWM 125 Varez is rated at 15 hp @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the SWM 125 Varez?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the SWM 125 Varez?

The SWM 125 Varez is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the SWM 125 Varez?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the SWM 125 Varez weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 130 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the SWM 125 Varez?

Using the listed 15 hp and 130 kg, the calculated ratio is about 115 hp per tonne (0.09 kW/kg).

Is the SWM 125 Varez suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the SWM 125 Varez A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.2 kW and 0.09 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the SWM 125 Varez suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the SWM 125 Varez?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16.5 L.

What tyres does the 125 Varez use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

What brakes does the SWM 125 Varez use?

Front: Single disc, ABS. Rear: Single disc, ABS.

What brake pads does the 125 Varez use?
