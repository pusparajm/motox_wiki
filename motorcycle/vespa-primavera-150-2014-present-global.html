Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Primavera 150 make?

The Vespa Primavera 150 is rated at 12.9 hp (9.5 kW) @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Vespa Primavera 150?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 12.8 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Vespa Primavera 150?

The Vespa Primavera 150 is listed with a 155 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Vespa Primavera 150?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Vespa Primavera 150 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 114 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Vespa Primavera 150?

Using the listed 12.9 hp (9.5 kW) and 114 kg, the calculated ratio is about 113 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Vespa Primavera 150 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Vespa Primavera 150 qualifies at 12.9 hp (9.5 kW) @ 7,750 rpm, 114 kg and 790 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Vespa Primavera 150 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 9.6 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Vespa Primavera 150 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Vespa Primavera 150?

Fuel capacity is listed as 8 L.

What tyres does the Primavera 150 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-12 front, 120/70-12 rear.

Does the Vespa Primavera 150 have ABS?

The Vespa Primavera 150 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Vespa Primavera 150 use?

Front: 32 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Vespa Primavera 150 use?

Front: 200 mm disc. Rear: 140 mm drum.

What brake pads does the Primavera 150 use?

Front: EBC SFA353. Rear: EBC 816. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Vespa Primavera 150?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
