Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Vespa Primavera 150 make?

The Vespa Primavera 150 is rated at 12.9 hp (9.5 kW) @ 7,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Vespa Primavera 150?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 12.8 Nm (9 lb-ft) @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Vespa Primavera 150?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Vespa Primavera 150 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 114 kg.

What engine oil does the Vespa Primavera 150 take?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Vespa Primavera 150?

Fuel capacity is listed as 8 L.
