Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the BMW M 1000 RR make?

The BMW M 1000 RR is rated at 212 hp (156 kW) @ 14,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW M 1000 RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 113 Nm @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the BMW M 1000 RR?

The seat height is listed as 832 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW M 1000 RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 192 kg.

What engine oil does the BMW M 1000 RR take?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW M 1000 RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16.5 L.
