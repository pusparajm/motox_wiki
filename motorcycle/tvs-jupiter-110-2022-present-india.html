Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Jupiter 110 make?

The TVS Jupiter 110 is rated at 7.9 hp (5.9 kW) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the TVS Jupiter 110?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 8.8 Nm @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the TVS Jupiter 110?

The TVS Jupiter 110 is listed with a 110 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the TVS Jupiter 110?

The seat height is listed as 765 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the TVS Jupiter 110 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 107 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the TVS Jupiter 110?

Using the listed 7.9 hp (5.9 kW) and 107 kg, the calculated ratio is about 74 hp per tonne (0.06 kW/kg).

Is the TVS Jupiter 110 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the TVS Jupiter 110 qualifies at 7.9 hp (5.9 kW) @ 7,500 rpm, 107 kg and 765 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the TVS Jupiter 110 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 5.9 kW and 0.06 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the TVS Jupiter 110 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 765 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the TVS Jupiter 110?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5 L.

What tyres does the Jupiter 110 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-12 front, 90/90-12 rear.

What braking system does the TVS Jupiter 110 have?

The TVS Jupiter 110 is listed with Combined braking system.

What suspension does the TVS Jupiter 110 use?

Front: 30 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the TVS Jupiter 110 use?

Front: 130 mm drum. Rear: 130 mm drum.

What is the service interval for the TVS Jupiter 110?

What is the battery rating of the Jupiter 110?

What is the engine oil capacity of the TVS Jupiter 110?

Engine oil capacity is 0.70 L (current 113 cc); 0.75–0.80 L on earlier 109.7 cc models and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
