Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the BKX 125 make?

The Benelli BKX 125 is rated at 15 hp @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli BKX 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 12.1 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli BKX 125?

The Benelli BKX 125 is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli BKX 125?

The seat height is listed as 860 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the Benelli BKX 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 860 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli BKX 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12 L.

What tyres does the BKX 125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-19 front, 120/90-17 rear.

What suspension does the Benelli BKX 125 use?

Front: 41 mm USD fork, fully adjustable. Rear: Steel swingarm with progressive linkage, preload and rebound adjustable.

What brakes does the Benelli BKX 125 use?

Front: Single 280 mm disc, four-piston caliper, ABS. Rear: Single 240 mm disc, single-piston caliper.

What is the battery rating of the BKX 125?

The listed battery rating is 12V 11.2Ah. The battery code is not yet verified for this entry.

What brake pads does the BKX 125 use?

Front: SBS 706HF. Rear: SBS 832HF. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
