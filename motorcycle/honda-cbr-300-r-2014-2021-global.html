Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the CBR 300 R make?

The Honda CBR 300 R is rated at 34.9 hp @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR 300 R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 22.9 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CBR 300 R?

The Honda CBR 300 R is listed with a 300 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR 300 R?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR 300 R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 160.6 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CBR 300 R?

Using the listed 34.9 hp and 160.6 kg, the calculated ratio is about 217 hp per tonne (0.16 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CBR 300 R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Sport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CBR 300 R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 26.0 kW and 0.16 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda CBR 300 R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR 300 R?
