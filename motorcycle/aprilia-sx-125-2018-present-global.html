Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Aprilia SX 125 make?

The Aprilia SX 125 is rated at 15 hp (11 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia SX 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.2 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia SX 125?

The seat height is listed as 880 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia SX 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 134 kg.

What engine oil does the Aprilia SX 125 take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.2 L (1.3 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia SX 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.5 L.
