Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Aprilia SX 125 make?

The Aprilia SX 125 is rated at 15 hp (11 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia SX 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.2 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia SX 125?

The Aprilia SX 125 is listed with a 125 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia SX 125?

The seat height is listed as 880 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia SX 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 134 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia SX 125?

Using the listed 15 hp (11 kW) and 134 kg, the calculated ratio is about 112 hp per tonne (0.08 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia SX 125 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 880 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia SX 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.2 kW and 0.08 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Aprilia SX 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 880 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia SX 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.5 L.

What tyres does the Aprilia SX 125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-17 front, 130/70-17 rear.

Does the Aprilia SX 125 have ABS?

The Aprilia SX 125 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Aprilia SX 125 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork, 240 mm travel. Rear: Single shock, 220 mm travel.

What brakes does the Aprilia SX 125 use?

Front: 300 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 218 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Aprilia SX 125?

Engine oil capacity is 1.2 L (1.3 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
