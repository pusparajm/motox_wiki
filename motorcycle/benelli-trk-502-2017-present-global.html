Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the TRK 502 make?

The Benelli TRK 502 is rated at 47 hp (35 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli TRK 502?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 46 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli TRK 502?

The Benelli TRK 502 is listed with a 500 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli TRK 502?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benelli TRK 502 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 235 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benelli TRK 502?

Using the listed 47 hp (35 kW) and 235 kg, the calculated ratio is about 200 hp per tonne (0.15 kW/kg).

Is the Benelli TRK 502 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 235 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Benelli TRK 502 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.0 kW and 0.15 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benelli TRK 502 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli TRK 502?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the TRK 502 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 150/70-17 rear.

Does the Benelli TRK 502 have ABS?

The Benelli TRK 502 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Benelli TRK 502 use?

Front: 50 mm inverted fork, 145 mm travel. Rear: Single shock, 145 mm travel.

What brakes does the Benelli TRK 502 use?

Front: EBC FA322/4HH · SBS 762/782 · Brembo 07BB33 · Vesrah VD-986. Rear: EBC FA698 · SBS 832 · Brembo 07GR93 · Vesrah VD-173. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
