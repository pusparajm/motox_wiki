Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Street Rod make?

The Harley-Davidson Street Rod is rated at 68 hp (51 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Street Rod?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 65 Nm @ 4,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Street Rod?

The Harley-Davidson Street Rod is listed with a 749 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Street Rod?

The seat height is listed as 765 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Street Rod weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 238 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Street Rod?

Using the listed 68 hp (51 kW) and 238 kg, the calculated ratio is about 286 hp per tonne (0.21 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Street Rod suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 68 hp (51 kW) @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 238 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Street Rod A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 50.7 kW and 0.21 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Street Rod suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 765 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Street Rod?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.2 L.

What tyres does the Street Rod use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Street Rod have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Street Rod is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Street Rod use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Street Rod use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, 2-piston calipers. Rear: 300 mm disc.

What brake pads does the Street Rod use?

Front: EBC FA209/2 · SBS 176/674/1005 · Brembo 07BB03 · Vesrah VD-958. Rear: EBC FA181X · SBS 671/1003 · Brembo 07BB04 · Vesrah VD-947. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
