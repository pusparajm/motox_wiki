Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 300 Agility+ make?

The Kymco 300 Agility+ is rated at 23 hp @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kymco 300 Agility+?

What is the engine displacement of the Kymco 300 Agility+?

The Kymco 300 Agility+ is listed with a 300 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kymco 300 Agility+?

The seat height is listed as 795 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kymco 300 Agility+ weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 191 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kymco 300 Agility+?

Using the listed 23 hp and 191 kg, the calculated ratio is about 120 hp per tonne (0.09 kW/kg).

Is the Kymco 300 Agility+ suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Kymco 300 Agility+ qualifies at 23 hp @ 7,500 rpm, 191 kg and 795 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Kymco 300 Agility+ A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 17.2 kW and 0.09 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kymco 300 Agility+ suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 795 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kymco 300 Agility+?
