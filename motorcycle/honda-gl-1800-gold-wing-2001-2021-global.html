Is the Honda GL 1800 Gold Wing suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 118 hp @ 5,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 357 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.
