Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Monkey 125 make?

The Honda Monkey 125 is rated at 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Monkey 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11 Nm @ 5,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Monkey 125?

The Honda Monkey 125 is listed with a 124 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Monkey 125?

The seat height is listed as 776 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Monkey 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 104 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Monkey 125?

Using the listed 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) and 104 kg, the calculated ratio is about 94 hp per tonne (0.07 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Monkey 125 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda Monkey 125 qualifies at 9.8 hp (7.3 kW) @ 6,750 rpm, 104 kg and 776 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda Monkey 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 7.3 kW and 0.07 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda Monkey 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 776 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Monkey 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.6 L.

What tyres does the Monkey 125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/80-12 front, 130/80-12 rear.

Does the Honda Monkey 125 have ABS?

The Honda Monkey 125 is listed with Single-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Monkey 125 use?

Front: 31 mm inverted fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Honda Monkey 125 use?

Front: 220 mm disc. Rear: 190 mm disc.

What battery does the Monkey 125 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ5S-BS. Battery rating: 12V 3.7Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Monkey 125 use?

Front: Brembo 07HO56. Rear: Brembo 07HO49. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the Monkey 125 use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CPR6EA-9. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Monkey 125?

Engine oil capacity is 0.8 L (0.85 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
