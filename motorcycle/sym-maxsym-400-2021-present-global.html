What is the power-to-weight ratio of the SYM Maxsym 400?

Using the listed 34 hp (25 kW) and 213 kg, the calculated ratio is about 160 hp per tonne (0.12 kW/kg).

Is the SYM Maxsym 400 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 213 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the SYM Maxsym 400 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 25.4 kW and 0.12 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the SYM Maxsym 400 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
