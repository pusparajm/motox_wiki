Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the SR 200 GT make?

The Aprilia SR 200 GT is rated at 17.4 hp @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia SR 200 GT?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 16.5 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia SR 200 GT?

The Aprilia SR 200 GT is listed with a 200 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia SR 200 GT?

The seat height is listed as 799 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia SR 200 GT weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 148 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia SR 200 GT?

Using the listed 17.4 hp and 148 kg, the calculated ratio is about 118 hp per tonne (0.09 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia SR 200 GT suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Aprilia SR 200 GT qualifies at 17.4 hp @ 8,500 rpm, 148 kg and 799 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Aprilia SR 200 GT A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 13.0 kW and 0.09 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Aprilia SR 200 GT suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 799 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia SR 200 GT?

Fuel capacity is listed as 9 L.
