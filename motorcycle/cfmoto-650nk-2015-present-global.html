Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 650NK make?

The CFMOTO 650NK is rated at 60 hp (45 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the CFMOTO 650NK?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 56 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the CFMOTO 650NK?

The CFMOTO 650NK is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the CFMOTO 650NK?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the CFMOTO 650NK weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 206 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the CFMOTO 650NK?

Using the listed 60 hp (45 kW) and 206 kg, the calculated ratio is about 291 hp per tonne (0.22 kW/kg).

Is the CFMOTO 650NK suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 60 hp (45 kW) @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 206 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the CFMOTO 650NK A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 44.7 kW and 0.22 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the CFMOTO 650NK suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the CFMOTO 650NK?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the 650NK use?

Does the CFMOTO 650NK have ABS?
