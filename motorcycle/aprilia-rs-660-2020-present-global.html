Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the RS 660 make?

The Aprilia RS 660 is rated at 100 hp (74 kW) @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia RS 660?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 67 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia RS 660?

The Aprilia RS 660 is listed with a 659 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia RS 660?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia RS 660 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 183 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia RS 660?

Using the listed 100 hp (74 kW) and 183 kg, the calculated ratio is about 546 hp per tonne (0.41 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia RS 660 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 100 hp (74 kW) @ 10,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia RS 660 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 74.6 kW and 0.41 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Aprilia RS 660 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
