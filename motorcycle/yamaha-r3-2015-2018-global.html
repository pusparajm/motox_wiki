Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the YZF-R3 make?

The Yamaha YZF-R3 is rated at 42 hp (31 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha YZF-R3?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 29.6 Nm @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha YZF-R3?

The Yamaha YZF-R3 is listed with a 321 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R3?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R3 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 169 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha YZF-R3?

Using the listed 42 hp (31 kW) and 169 kg, the calculated ratio is about 249 hp per tonne (0.19 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha YZF-R3 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R3 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 31.3 kW and 0.19 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R3 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha YZF-R3?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the YZF-R3 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 140/70-17 rear.

Does the Yamaha YZF-R3 have ABS?
