Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki Ninja 650 make?

The Kawasaki Ninja 650 is rated at 68 hp (50 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 650?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 65.7 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja 650?

The Kawasaki Ninja 650 is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja 650?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja 650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 193 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja 650?

Using the listed 68 hp (50 kW) and 193 kg, the calculated ratio is about 352 hp per tonne (0.26 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 650 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 68 hp (50 kW) @ 8,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; its Sport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 650 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 50.7 kW and 0.26 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 650 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 650?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki Ninja 650 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja 650 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja 650 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja 650 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Horizontal back-link single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja 650 use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, 2-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 650?

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L (1.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
