Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Multistrada V4 make?

The Ducati Multistrada V4 is rated at 170 hp (125 kW) @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati Multistrada V4?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 125 Nm @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Ducati Multistrada V4?

The Ducati Multistrada V4 is listed with a 1,158 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Ducati Multistrada V4?

The seat height is listed as 840/860 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Ducati Multistrada V4 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 240 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Ducati Multistrada V4?

Using the listed 170 hp (125 kW) and 240 kg, the calculated ratio is about 708 hp per tonne (0.53 kW/kg).

Is the Ducati Multistrada V4 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 170 hp (125 kW) @ 10,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 240 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 840/860 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Ducati Multistrada V4 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 126.8 kW and 0.53 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Ducati Multistrada V4 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 840/860 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Ducati Multistrada V4?

Fuel capacity is listed as 22 L.

What tyres does the Multistrada V4 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-19 front, 170/60-17 rear.

Does the Ducati Multistrada V4 have ABS?

The Ducati Multistrada V4 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Ducati Multistrada V4 use?

Front: 50 mm inverted fork, electronic adjustment. Rear: Single shock, electronic adjustment.

What brakes does the Ducati Multistrada V4 use?

Front: 330 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 265 mm disc.

What battery does the Multistrada V4 use?

The listed battery code is YT12B-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the Multistrada V4 use?

Front: Brembo 07BB38. Rear: Brembo 07BB04. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the Multistrada V4 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 116 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Ducati Multistrada V4?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 15W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
