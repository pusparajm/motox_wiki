Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the ZX6R make?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R is rated at 130 hp (96 kW) @ 13,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 71 Nm @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R is listed with a 636 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R?

Using the listed 130 hp (96 kW) and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 670 hp per tonne (0.50 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 130 hp (96 kW) @ 13,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 96.9 kW and 0.50 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the ZX6R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R use?

Front: 41 mm inverted Big Piston Fork. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the ZX6R use?

The listed battery code is YTX9-BS. Battery rating: 12V 8Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the ZX6R use?

Front: Brembo 07KA28. Rear: Brembo 07KA16. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the ZX6R use?

The listed spark plug is NGK CR9E. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the ZX6R use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 110 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R?

Engine oil capacity is 3.3 L (3.5 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
