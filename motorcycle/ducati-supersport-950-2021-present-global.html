Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Ducati SuperSport 950 make?

The Ducati SuperSport 950 is rated at 110 hp (81 kW) @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Ducati SuperSport 950?

What is the seat height of the Ducati SuperSport 950?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.
