Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja make?

The Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja is rated at 228 hp (170 kW) @ 11,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 141.7 Nm @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja?

The Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja is listed with a 1000 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja?

The seat height is listed as 825 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 238 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja?

Using the listed 228 hp (170 kW) and 238 kg, the calculated ratio is about 958 hp per tonne (0.71 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 228 hp (170 kW) @ 11,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 238 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 825 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 170.0 kW and 0.71 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 825 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 200/55-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja have ABS?

The Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki ZX 1000 H2 Carbon Ninja use?

Front: 330 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.
