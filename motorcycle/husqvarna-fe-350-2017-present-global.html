Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the FE 350 make?

The Husqvarna FE 350 is rated at 45 hp (33 kW).

What is the torque of the Husqvarna FE 350?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 36 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Husqvarna FE 350?

The Husqvarna FE 350 is listed with a 350 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Husqvarna FE 350?

The seat height is listed as 950 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Husqvarna FE 350 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 106 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Husqvarna FE 350?

Using the listed 45 hp (33 kW) and 106 kg, the calculated ratio is about 425 hp per tonne (0.32 kW/kg).

Is the Husqvarna FE 350 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 950 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Enduro category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Husqvarna FE 350 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 33.6 kW and 0.32 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Husqvarna FE 350 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 950 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Husqvarna FE 350?

Fuel capacity is listed as 8.5 L.
