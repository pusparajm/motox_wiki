Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Husqvarna FE 350 make?

The Husqvarna FE 350 is rated at 45 hp (33 kW).

What is the torque of the Husqvarna FE 350?

What is the seat height of the Husqvarna FE 350?

The seat height is listed as 950 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.
