How much horsepower (BHP) does the 125 Avensis make?

The Suzuki 125 Avensis is rated at 8.7 hp @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki 125 Avensis?

What is the seat height of the Suzuki 125 Avensis?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki 125 Avensis weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki 125 Avensis?

Using the listed 8.7 hp and 107 kg, the calculated ratio is about 81 hp per tonne (0.06 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki 125 Avensis suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Suzuki 125 Avensis qualifies at 8.7 hp @ 6,750 rpm, 107 kg and 780 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Suzuki 125 Avensis A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 6.5 kW and 0.06 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki 125 Avensis suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 780 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
