Frequently Asked Questions

What is the engine displacement of the Honda XR 250 L?

The Honda XR 250 L is listed with a 250 cc engine.

What battery does the XR 250 L use?

The listed battery code is YTX4L-BS. Alternative listed codes: YTZ5S-BS. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the XR 250 L use?

Front: SBS 603HF. Rear: SBS 604HF. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What fork oil does the Honda XR 250 L use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 1991, 1996. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Honda XR 250 L use?

The listed fork oil seal is 43.00 × 54.00 × 11.00 mm (SHOWA, NOK) — 1991, 1996.
