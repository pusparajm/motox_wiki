Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the CFMOTO 675SR-R make?

The CFMOTO 675SR-R is rated at 95 hp (70 kW) @ 11,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the CFMOTO 675SR-R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 70 Nm @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the CFMOTO 675SR-R?

The CFMOTO 675SR-R is listed with a 674 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the CFMOTO 675SR-R?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the CFMOTO 675SR-R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the CFMOTO 675SR-R?

Using the listed 95 hp (70 kW) and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 490 hp per tonne (0.37 kW/kg).

Is the CFMOTO 675SR-R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 95 hp (70 kW) @ 11,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the CFMOTO 675SR-R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.8 kW and 0.37 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the CFMOTO 675SR-R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the CFMOTO 675SR-R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the CFMOTO 675SR-R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the CFMOTO 675SR-R have ABS?

The CFMOTO 675SR-R is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the CFMOTO 675SR-R use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the CFMOTO 675SR-R use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What battery does the CFMOTO 675SR-R use?

The listed battery code is YTZ14S. Battery rating: 12V 11.2Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What is the engine oil capacity of the CFMOTO 675SR-R?

Engine oil capacity is 3.1 L and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, SN or higher, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
