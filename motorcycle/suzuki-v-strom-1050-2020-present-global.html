Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the V-Strom 1050 make?

The Suzuki V-Strom 1050 is rated at 107 hp (79 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 100 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

The Suzuki V-Strom 1050 is listed with a 1,037 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 236 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

Using the listed 107 hp (79 kW) and 236 kg, the calculated ratio is about 453 hp per tonne (0.34 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 107 hp (79 kW) @ 8,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 236 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 79.8 kW and 0.34 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.

What tyres does the V-Strom 1050 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-19 front, 150/70-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 have ABS?

The Suzuki V-Strom 1050 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 260 mm disc.

What battery does the V-Strom 1050 use?

What spark plug does the V-Strom 1050 use?

What chain does the V-Strom 1050 use?
