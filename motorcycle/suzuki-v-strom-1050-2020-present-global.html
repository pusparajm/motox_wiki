Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 make?

The Suzuki V-Strom 1050 is rated at 107 hp (79 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 100 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 236 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki V-Strom 1050 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki V-Strom 1050?

Fuel capacity is listed as 20 L.
