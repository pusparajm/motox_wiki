Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Aprilia SR 50 Motard make?

The Aprilia SR 50 Motard is rated at 4.6 hp @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia SR 50 Motard?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 3.8 Nm @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia SR 50 Motard?

The Aprilia SR 50 Motard is listed with a 50 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia SR 50 Motard?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia SR 50 Motard weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 108 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia SR 50 Motard?

Using the listed 4.6 hp and 108 kg, the calculated ratio is about 43 hp per tonne (0.03 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia SR 50 Motard suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Aprilia SR 50 Motard A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 3.4 kW and 0.03 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Aprilia SR 50 Motard suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia SR 50 Motard?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7 L.

What tyres does the Aprilia SR 50 Motard use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-14 front, 120/70-14 rear.

What suspension does the Aprilia SR 50 Motard use?

Front: Hydraulic telescopic fork with straight stanchions, 110 mm (4.3 inches). Rear: Single arm with engine crankcase as swinging element, served by a single shock, 122 mm (4.8 inches).

What brakes does the Aprilia SR 50 Motard use?

Front: Single disc. Two-piston floating caliper. Rear: Single disc. ABS.

What fork oil does the Aprilia SR 50 Motard use?

The listed fork oil specification is SAE 5W. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.
