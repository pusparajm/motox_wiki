Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the NC750X make?

The Honda NC750X is rated at 58 hp (43 kW) @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda NC750X?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 69 Nm @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda NC750X?

The Honda NC750X is listed with a 745 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda NC750X?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda NC750X weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 224 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda NC750X?

Using the listed 58 hp (43 kW) and 224 kg, the calculated ratio is about 259 hp per tonne (0.19 kW/kg).

Is the Honda NC750X suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 58 hp (43 kW) @ 6,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 224 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda NC750X A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 43.3 kW and 0.19 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda NC750X suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda NC750X?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14.1 L.

What tyres does the NC750X use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Honda NC750X have ABS?

The Honda NC750X is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda NC750X use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock, preload adjustable.

What brakes does the Honda NC750X use?
