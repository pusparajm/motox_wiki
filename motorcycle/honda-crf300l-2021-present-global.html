How much horsepower (BHP) does the CRF300L make?

How much does the Honda CRF300L weigh?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CRF300L?

Using the listed 27 hp (20 kW) and 142 kg, the calculated ratio is about 190 hp per tonne (0.14 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CRF300L suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 880 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CRF300L A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 20.1 kW and 0.14 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda CRF300L suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 880 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
