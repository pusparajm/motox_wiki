Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CRF300L make?

The Honda CRF300L is rated at 27 hp (20 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CRF300L?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 26.3 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CRF300L?

The seat height is listed as 880 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CRF300L weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 142 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CRF300L take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.5 L (1.6 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CRF300L?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.8 L.
