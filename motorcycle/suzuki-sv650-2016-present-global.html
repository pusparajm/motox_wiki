Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki SV650 make?

The Suzuki SV650 is rated at 75 hp (55 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki SV650?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 64 Nm (47 lb-ft) @ 8,100 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki SV650?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki SV650 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 198 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki SV650 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.9 L (3.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki SV650?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14.5 L.
