Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CBR650R make?

The Honda CBR650R is rated at 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR650R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 63 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CBR650R?

The Honda CBR650R is listed with a 649 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR650R?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR650R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 212 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CBR650R?

Using the listed 94 hp (70 kW) and 212 kg, the calculated ratio is about 443 hp per tonne (0.33 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CBR650R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 94 hp (70 kW) @ 12,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 212 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CBR650R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.1 kW and 0.33 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CBR650R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR650R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15.4 L.

What tyres does the Honda CBR650R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Honda CBR650R have ABS?

The Honda CBR650R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda CBR650R use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock, preload adjustable.

What brakes does the Honda CBR650R use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc, 1-piston caliper.

What engine oil does the Honda CBR650R take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
