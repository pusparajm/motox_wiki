Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CB1000R make?

The Honda CB1000R is rated at 143 hp (107 kW) @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CB1000R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 104 Nm (77 lb-ft) @ 8,250 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Honda CB1000R?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CB1000R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 212 kg.

What engine oil does the Honda CB1000R take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CB1000R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16.2 L.
