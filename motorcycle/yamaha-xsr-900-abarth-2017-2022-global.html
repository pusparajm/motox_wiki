Is the Yamaha XSR 900 Abarth suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 119 hp @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha XSR 900 Abarth A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 88.7 kW and 0.46 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
