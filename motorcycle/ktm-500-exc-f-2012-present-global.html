Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 500 EXC-F make?

The KTM 500 EXC-F is rated at 58 hp (43 kW).

What is the torque of the KTM 500 EXC-F?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 48 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 500 EXC-F?

The KTM 500 EXC-F is listed with a 510 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 500 EXC-F?

The seat height is listed as 960 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 500 EXC-F weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 111 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 500 EXC-F?

Using the listed 58 hp (43 kW) and 111 kg, the calculated ratio is about 523 hp per tonne (0.39 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 500 EXC-F suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 58 hp (43 kW) is above the 50 hp limit; 960 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Enduro category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 500 EXC-F A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 43.3 kW and 0.39 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 500 EXC-F suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 960 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 500 EXC-F?

Fuel capacity is listed as 9 L.

What tyres does the 500 EXC-F use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-21 front, 140/80-18 rear.
