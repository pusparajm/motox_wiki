How much horsepower (BHP) does the ZX-6R 636 Ninja Upgrade DS make?

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki ZX-6R 636 Ninja Upgrade DS?

Using the listed 130 hp (96 kW) and 196 kg, the calculated ratio is about 663 hp per tonne (0.49 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki ZX-6R 636 Ninja Upgrade DS suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 130 hp (96 kW) @ 13,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki ZX-6R 636 Ninja Upgrade DS A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 96.9 kW and 0.49 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki ZX-6R 636 Ninja Upgrade DS suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
