Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Leoncino 500 make?

The Benelli Leoncino 500 is rated at 47.6 hp (35 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Benelli Leoncino 500?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 46 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Benelli Leoncino 500?

The Benelli Leoncino 500 is listed with a 500 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Benelli Leoncino 500?

The seat height is listed as 815 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Benelli Leoncino 500 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 207 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Benelli Leoncino 500?

Using the listed 47.6 hp (35 kW) and 207 kg, the calculated ratio is about 230 hp per tonne (0.17 kW/kg).

Is the Benelli Leoncino 500 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 207 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 815 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Benelli Leoncino 500 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.5 kW and 0.17 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Benelli Leoncino 500 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 815 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Benelli Leoncino 500?

Fuel capacity is listed as 12.7 L.

What tyres does the Leoncino 500 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Benelli Leoncino 500 have ABS?

What brakes does the Benelli Leoncino 500 use?
