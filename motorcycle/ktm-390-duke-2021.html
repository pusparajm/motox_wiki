Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the 390 Duke make?

The KTM 390 Duke is rated at 44.5 hp (33 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 390 Duke?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 39 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the KTM 390 Duke?

The KTM 390 Duke is listed with a 399 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the KTM 390 Duke?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 390 Duke weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 165 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the KTM 390 Duke?

Using the listed 44.5 hp (33 kW) and 165 kg, the calculated ratio is about 270 hp per tonne (0.20 kW/kg).

Is the KTM 390 Duke suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the KTM 390 Duke A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 33.2 kW and 0.20 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the KTM 390 Duke suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 390 Duke?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the 390 Duke use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the KTM 390 Duke have ABS?

The KTM 390 Duke is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the KTM 390 Duke use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork, adjustable. Rear: Single shock, adjustable.

What brakes does the KTM 390 Duke use?
