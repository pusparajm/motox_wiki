Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Kawasaki KLX300 make?

The Kawasaki KLX300 is rated at 26 hp (19 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KLX300?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 26 Nm @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KLX300?

The Kawasaki KLX300 is listed with a 292 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KLX300?

The seat height is listed as 900 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KLX300 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 138 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki KLX300?

Using the listed 26 hp (19 kW) and 138 kg, the calculated ratio is about 188 hp per tonne (0.14 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki KLX300 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 900 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki KLX300 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 19.4 kW and 0.14 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Kawasaki KLX300 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 900 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KLX300?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7.8 L.

What tyres does the Kawasaki KLX300 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 80/100-21 front, 120/80-18 rear.

Does the Kawasaki KLX300 have ABS?

The Kawasaki KLX300 is listed with Single-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki KLX300 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork, 285 mm travel. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock, 285 mm travel.

What brakes does the Kawasaki KLX300 use?

Front: 250 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki KLX300?

Engine oil capacity is 1.3 L (1.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
