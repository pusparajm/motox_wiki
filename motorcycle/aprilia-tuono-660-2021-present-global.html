Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Aprilia Tuono 660 make?

The Aprilia Tuono 660 is rated at 100 hp (74 kW) @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia Tuono 660?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 67 Nm @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia Tuono 660?

The Aprilia Tuono 660 is listed with a 659 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia Tuono 660?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia Tuono 660 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 183 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia Tuono 660?

Using the listed 100 hp (74 kW) and 183 kg, the calculated ratio is about 546 hp per tonne (0.41 kW/kg).

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia Tuono 660?

Fuel capacity is listed as 15 L.

What tyres does the Aprilia Tuono 660 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Aprilia Tuono 660 have ABS?

The Aprilia Tuono 660 is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Aprilia Tuono 660 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Aprilia Tuono 660 use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What engine oil does the Aprilia Tuono 660 take?

Engine oil capacity is 2.8 L (3.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
