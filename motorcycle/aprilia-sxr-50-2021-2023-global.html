Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the SXR 50 make?

The Aprilia SXR 50 is rated at 3.2 hp @ 7,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia SXR 50?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 3.3 Nm @ 6,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia SXR 50?

The Aprilia SXR 50 is listed with a 50 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia SXR 50?

The seat height is listed as 775 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia SXR 50 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 108 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia SXR 50?

Using the listed 3.2 hp and 108 kg, the calculated ratio is about 30 hp per tonne (0.02 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia SXR 50 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Aprilia SXR 50 qualifies at 3.2 hp @ 7,000 rpm, 108 kg and 775 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Aprilia SXR 50 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 2.4 kW and 0.02 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Aprilia SXR 50 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 775 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia SXR 50?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7 L.

What tyres does the SXR 50 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-12 front, 120/70-12 rear.

What suspension does the Aprilia SXR 50 use?

Front: Telescopic hydraulic fork, 76 mm (3.0 inches). Rear: Dual action single shock absorber, adjustable to 5 positions.

What brakes does the Aprilia SXR 50 use?

Front: Single disc. 2 opposing pistons. Rear: Expanding brake (drum brake).

What brake pads does the SXR 50 use?

Front: SBS 237HF. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the SXR 50 use?

The listed chain or final drive is Belt. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.
