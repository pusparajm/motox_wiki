Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy make?

The Harley-Davidson Fat Boy is rated at 94 hp (70 kW) @ 4,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 155 Nm @ 3,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy?

The Harley-Davidson Fat Boy is listed with a 1,868 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy?

The seat height is listed as 675 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 317 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy?

Using the listed 94 hp (70 kW) and 317 kg, the calculated ratio is about 297 hp per tonne (0.22 kW/kg).

Is the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 94 hp (70 kW) @ 4,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 317 kg is above the 200 kg limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 70.1 kW and 0.22 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 675 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy?

Fuel capacity is listed as 18.9 L.

What tyres does the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 160/60-18 front, 240/40-18 rear.

Does the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy have ABS?

The Harley-Davidson Fat Boy is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy use?

Front: 49 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks, preload adjustable.

What brakes does the Harley-Davidson Fat Boy use?

Front: 300 mm dual discs, 4-piston calipers. Rear: 292 mm disc.
