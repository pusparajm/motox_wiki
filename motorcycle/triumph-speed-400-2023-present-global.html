Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Speed 400 make?

The Triumph Speed 400 is rated at 40 hp (30 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Speed 400?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 37.5 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Triumph Speed 400?

The Triumph Speed 400 is listed with a 398 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Speed 400?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Speed 400 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 170 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Triumph Speed 400?

Using the listed 40 hp (30 kW) and 170 kg, the calculated ratio is about 235 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Triumph Speed 400 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Triumph Speed 400 qualifies at 40 hp (30 kW) @ 8,000 rpm, 170 kg and 790 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Triumph Speed 400 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 29.8 kW and 0.18 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Triumph Speed 400 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Speed 400?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13 L.

What tyres does the Speed 400 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the Triumph Speed 400 have ABS?

The Triumph Speed 400 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Triumph Speed 400 use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Triumph Speed 400 use?

Front: 300 mm disc, radial 4-piston caliper. Rear: 230 mm disc.

What chain does the Speed 400 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 110 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Triumph Speed 400?

Engine oil capacity is 1.8 L (1.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
