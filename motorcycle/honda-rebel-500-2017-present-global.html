Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda Rebel 500 make?

The Honda Rebel 500 is rated at 46 hp (34 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda Rebel 500?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 43.4 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda Rebel 500?

The Honda Rebel 500 is listed with a 471 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda Rebel 500?

The seat height is listed as 690 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda Rebel 500 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 191 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda Rebel 500?

Using the listed 46 hp (34 kW) and 191 kg, the calculated ratio is about 241 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Honda Rebel 500 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda Rebel 500 qualifies at 46 hp (34 kW) @ 8,500 rpm, 191 kg and 690 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda Rebel 500 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 34.3 kW and 0.18 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda Rebel 500 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 690 mm, which is low by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda Rebel 500?

Fuel capacity is listed as 11.2 L.

What tyres does the Honda Rebel 500 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 130/90-16 front, 150/80-16 rear.

Does the Honda Rebel 500 have ABS?

The Honda Rebel 500 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda Rebel 500 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Twin shocks.

What brakes does the Honda Rebel 500 use?

Front: 296 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda Rebel 500?

Engine oil capacity is 3.2 L (3.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
