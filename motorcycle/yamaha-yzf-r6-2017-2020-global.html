Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the YZF-R6 make?

What is the torque of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

The Yamaha YZF-R6 is listed with a 599 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R6 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 190 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

Using the listed 117 hp (86 kW) and 190 kg, the calculated ratio is about 616 hp per tonne (0.46 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha YZF-R6 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 117 hp (86 kW) @ 14,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 850 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R6 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 87.2 kW and 0.46 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R6 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 850 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.
