Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha YZF-R6 make?

The Yamaha YZF-R6 is rated at 117 hp (86 kW) @ 14,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 61.7 Nm @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

The seat height is listed as 850 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R6 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 190 kg.

What engine oil does the Yamaha YZF-R6 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha YZF-R6?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.
