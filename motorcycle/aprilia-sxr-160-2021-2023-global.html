Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the SXR 160 make?

The Aprilia SXR 160 is rated at 10.8 hp @ 7,100 rpm.

What is the torque of the Aprilia SXR 160?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 11.6 Nm @ 6,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Aprilia SXR 160?

The Aprilia SXR 160 is listed with a 160 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Aprilia SXR 160?

The seat height is listed as 775 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Aprilia SXR 160 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 129 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Aprilia SXR 160?

Using the listed 10.8 hp and 129 kg, the calculated ratio is about 84 hp per tonne (0.06 kW/kg).

Is the Aprilia SXR 160 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Aprilia SXR 160 qualifies at 10.8 hp @ 7,100 rpm, 129 kg and 775 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Aprilia SXR 160 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 8.1 kW and 0.06 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Aprilia SXR 160 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 775 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Aprilia SXR 160?

Fuel capacity is listed as 7 L.

What tyres does the SXR 160 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-12 front, 120/70-12 rear.

What suspension does the Aprilia SXR 160 use?

Front: Telescopic fork, 30mm. Rear: Adjustable mono shock.

What brakes does the Aprilia SXR 160 use?

Front: Single disc. ABS. Rear: Expanding brake (drum brake). ABS.

What brake pads does the SXR 160 use?
