Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Honda CBR500R make?

The Honda CBR500R is rated at 47 hp (35 kW) @ 8,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda CBR500R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 43 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda CBR500R?

The Honda CBR500R is listed with a 471 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda CBR500R?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda CBR500R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 194 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda CBR500R?

Using the listed 47 hp (35 kW) and 194 kg, the calculated ratio is about 242 hp per tonne (0.18 kW/kg).

Is the Honda CBR500R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda CBR500R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 35.0 kW and 0.18 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Honda CBR500R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda CBR500R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.1 L.

What tyres does the Honda CBR500R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 160/60-17 rear.

Does the Honda CBR500R have ABS?

The Honda CBR500R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Honda CBR500R use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock, preload adjustable.

What brakes does the Honda CBR500R use?

Front: 296 mm dual discs, 2-piston calipers. Rear: 240 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Honda CBR500R?

Engine oil capacity is 3.2 L (3.4 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MA. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
