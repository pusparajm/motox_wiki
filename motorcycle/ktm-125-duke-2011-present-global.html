Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the KTM 125 Duke make?

The KTM 125 Duke is rated at 15 hp (11 kW) @ 9,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the KTM 125 Duke?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 12 Nm (9 lb-ft) @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the KTM 125 Duke?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the KTM 125 Duke weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 159 kg.

What engine oil does the KTM 125 Duke take?

Engine oil capacity is 1.5 L (1.6 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-50, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the KTM 125 Duke?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13.4 L.
