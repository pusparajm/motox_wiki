Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX-S750 make?

The Suzuki GSX-S750 is rated at 114 hp (84 kW) @ 10,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-S750?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 81 Nm @ 9,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-S750?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-S750 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 213 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki GSX-S750 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.0 L (3.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-S750?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16 L.
