Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the ZX14R make?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R is rated at 197 hp (147 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 158 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R is listed with a 1,441 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R?

The seat height is listed as 800 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 269 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R?

Using the listed 197 hp (147 kW) and 269 kg, the calculated ratio is about 732 hp per tonne (0.55 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 197 hp (147 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 269 kg is above the 200 kg limit; its Sport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 146.9 kW and 0.55 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 800 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R?

Fuel capacity is listed as 22 L.

What tyres does the ZX14R use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/50-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R use?

Front: 43 mm inverted fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 250 mm disc.

What is the service interval for the Kawasaki Ninja ZX-14R?

The listed service interval is 12,000 km / 12 months. Confirm the schedule for your market and model year.

What battery does the ZX14R use?

What brake pads does the ZX14R use?

Front: SBS 838HS. Rear: SBS 715LS. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the ZX14R use?

What chain does the ZX14R use?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L without filter; 4.2 L with filter; 4.6 L dry and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA/MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
