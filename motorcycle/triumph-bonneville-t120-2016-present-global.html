Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Triumph Bonneville T120 make?

The Triumph Bonneville T120 is rated at 79 hp (59 kW) @ 6,550 rpm.

What is the torque of the Triumph Bonneville T120?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 105 Nm (77 lb-ft) @ 3,500 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Triumph Bonneville T120?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Triumph Bonneville T120 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 236 kg.

What engine oil does the Triumph Bonneville T120 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.8 L (4.0 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Triumph Bonneville T120?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14.5 L.
