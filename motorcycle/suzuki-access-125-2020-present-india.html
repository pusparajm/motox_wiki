Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Access 125 make?

The Suzuki Access 125 is rated at 8.6 hp (6.4 kW) @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki Access 125?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 10.2 Nm @ 5,000 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki Access 125?

The Suzuki Access 125 is listed with a 124 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki Access 125?

The seat height is listed as 773 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki Access 125 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 104 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki Access 125?

Using the listed 8.6 hp (6.4 kW) and 104 kg, the calculated ratio is about 83 hp per tonne (0.06 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki Access 125 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Suzuki Access 125 qualifies at 8.6 hp (6.4 kW) @ 6,750 rpm, 104 kg and 773 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Suzuki Access 125 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 6.4 kW and 0.06 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Suzuki Access 125 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 773 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki Access 125?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5 L.

What tyres does the Access 125 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 90/90-12 front, 90/90-12 rear.

What braking system does the Suzuki Access 125 have?

The Suzuki Access 125 is listed with Combined braking system.

What suspension does the Suzuki Access 125 use?

Front: 30 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki Access 125 use?

Front: 120 mm drum. Rear: 120 mm drum.

What is the battery rating of the Access 125?

What is the engine oil capacity of the Suzuki Access 125?
