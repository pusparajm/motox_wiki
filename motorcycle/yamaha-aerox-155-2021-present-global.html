Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Aerox 155 make?

The Yamaha Aerox 155 is rated at 15.4 hp (11.3 kW) @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha Aerox 155?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 13.9 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha Aerox 155?

The Yamaha Aerox 155 is listed with a 155 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha Aerox 155?

The seat height is listed as 790 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha Aerox 155 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 125 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha Aerox 155?

Using the listed 15.4 hp (11.3 kW) and 125 kg, the calculated ratio is about 123 hp per tonne (0.09 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha Aerox 155 suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Yamaha Aerox 155 qualifies at 15.4 hp (11.3 kW) @ 8,000 rpm, 125 kg and 790 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Yamaha Aerox 155 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 11.5 kW and 0.09 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha Aerox 155 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 790 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha Aerox 155?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.5 L.

What tyres does the Aerox 155 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/80-14 front, 140/70-14 rear.

Does the Yamaha Aerox 155 have ABS?

The Yamaha Aerox 155 is listed with Single-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Yamaha Aerox 155 use?

Front: 26 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the Yamaha Aerox 155 use?

Front: 230 mm disc. Rear: 230 mm disc.

What battery does the Aerox 155 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ7V. Battery rating: 12V 6Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Yamaha Aerox 155?

Engine oil capacity is 0.9 L (0.95 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-30, JASO MB. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
