Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX-R750 make?

The Suzuki GSX-R750 is rated at 148 hp (110 kW) @ 12,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-R750?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 86 Nm @ 11,200 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Suzuki GSX-R750?

The Suzuki GSX-R750 is listed with a 750 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-R750?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-R750 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 190 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Suzuki GSX-R750?

Using the listed 148 hp (110 kW) and 190 kg, the calculated ratio is about 779 hp per tonne (0.58 kW/kg).

Is the Suzuki GSX-R750 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 148 hp (110 kW) @ 12,500 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Suzuki GSX-R750 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 110.4 kW and 0.58 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Suzuki GSX-R750 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R750?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the Suzuki GSX-R750 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 180/55-17 rear.

Does the Suzuki GSX-R750 have ABS?

The Suzuki GSX-R750 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Suzuki GSX-R750 use?

Front: 41 mm inverted Big Piston Fork. Rear: Link-type single shock.

What brakes does the Suzuki GSX-R750 use?

Front: 310 mm dual discs, Brembo radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R750?

Engine oil capacity is 2.9 L (3.1 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What fork oil does the Suzuki GSX-R750 use?

Listed fork oil data: grade 5W — 2021–2022, 2026; air gap 95 mm — 2021–2022, 2026. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.

What fork seal size does the Suzuki GSX-R750 use?

What rear shock does the Suzuki GSX-R750 use?
