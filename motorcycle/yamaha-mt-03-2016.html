Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Yamaha MT-03 make?

The Yamaha MT-03 is rated at 42 hp (31 kW) @ 10,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha MT-03?

What is the seat height of the Yamaha MT-03?

The seat height is listed as 780 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.
