Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the SH 350 make?

The Honda SH 350 is rated at 29 hp @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda SH 350?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 32 Nm @ 5,250 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda SH 350?

The Honda SH 350 is listed with a 350 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda SH 350?

The seat height is listed as 805 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda SH 350 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 172 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda SH 350?

Using the listed 29 hp and 172 kg, the calculated ratio is about 169 hp per tonne (0.13 kW/kg).

Is the Honda SH 350 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 805 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Honda SH 350 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 21.6 kW and 0.13 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda SH 350 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 805 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda SH 350?

Fuel capacity is listed as 9.1 L.

What tyres does the SH 350 use?
