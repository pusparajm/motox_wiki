It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 70.1 hp @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 52.3 kW and 0.27 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.
