Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC make?

The MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC is rated at 140 hp @ 13,100 rpm.

What is the torque of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 86 Nm @ 10,100 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC?

The MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC is listed with a 800 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 175 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC?

Using the listed 140 hp and 175 kg, the calculated ratio is about 800 hp per tonne (0.60 kW/kg).

Is the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 140 hp @ 13,100 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 810 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Sport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 104.4 kW and 0.60 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 810 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16.6 L.

What tyres does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-ZR17 front, 180/55-ZR17 rear.

What suspension does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC use?

Front: Marzocchi, 125 mm (4.9 inches). Rear: Progressive Sachs, single shock absorber with rebound and compression damping and spring preload adjustment, 125 mm (4.9 inches).

What brakes does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale RC use?

Front: Double disc. ABS. Floating disc, 4-piston. Rear: Single disc. ABS. 4-piston.
