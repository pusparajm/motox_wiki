Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the NSC 110 Vision make?

The Honda NSC 110 Vision is rated at 8.3 hp @ 8,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Honda NSC 110 Vision?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 8.7 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Honda NSC 110 Vision?

The Honda NSC 110 Vision is listed with a 110 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Honda NSC 110 Vision?

The seat height is listed as 770 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Honda NSC 110 Vision weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 102 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Honda NSC 110 Vision?

Using the listed 8.3 hp and 102 kg, the calculated ratio is about 81 hp per tonne (0.06 kW/kg).

Is the Honda NSC 110 Vision suitable for beginners?

On the site's beginner shortlist criteria (50 hp or less, 200 kg or less and an 800 mm seat or lower), the Honda NSC 110 Vision qualifies at 8.3 hp @ 8,000 rpm, 102 kg and 770 mm. Rider training and individual fit still matter.

Is the Honda NSC 110 Vision A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 6.2 kW and 0.06 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Honda NSC 110 Vision suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 770 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Honda NSC 110 Vision?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5.5 L.

What tyres does the NSC 110 Vision use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 80/90-14 front, 90/90-14 rear.

What suspension does the Honda NSC 110 Vision use?

What brakes does the Honda NSC 110 Vision use?

Front: Single disc. Rear: Expanding brake (drum brake).

What brake pads does the NSC 110 Vision use?

Front: SBS 205HF. Rear: SBS 236HF. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.
