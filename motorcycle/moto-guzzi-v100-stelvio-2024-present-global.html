Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the V100 Stelvio make?

The Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio is rated at 115 hp (85 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 105 Nm @ 6,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio?

The Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio is listed with a 1,042 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio?

The seat height is listed as 830 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 246 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio?

Using the listed 115 hp (85 kW) and 246 kg, the calculated ratio is about 467 hp per tonne (0.35 kW/kg).

Is the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 115 hp (85 kW) @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 246 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 830 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 85.8 kW and 0.35 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 830 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17 L.

What tyres does the V100 Stelvio use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-19 front, 170/60-17 rear.

Does the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio have ABS?

The Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio is listed with Cornering ABS.

What suspension does the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio use?

Front: 46 mm inverted fork, 170 mm travel. Rear: Single shock, 170 mm travel.

What brakes does the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 280 mm disc.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Moto Guzzi V100 Stelvio?

Engine oil capacity is 3.5 L (3.7 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
