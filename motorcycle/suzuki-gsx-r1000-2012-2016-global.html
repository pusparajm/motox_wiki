Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 make?

The Suzuki GSX-R1000 is rated at 182 hp (134 kW) @ 12,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 117 Nm (86 lb-ft) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the seat height of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

The seat height is listed as 810 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 203 kg.

What engine oil does the Suzuki GSX-R1000 take?

Engine oil capacity is 3.7 L (3.9 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.

What is the fuel capacity of the Suzuki GSX-R1000?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.5 L.
