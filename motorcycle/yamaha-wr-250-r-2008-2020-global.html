Is the Yamaha WR 250 R suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 930 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Enduro category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha WR 250 R A2-legal?

On the listed figures it fits the European A2 limits: about 22.9 kW and 0.17 kW/kg. Market-specific approval and restricted variants can differ.

Is the Yamaha WR 250 R suitable for shorter riders?
