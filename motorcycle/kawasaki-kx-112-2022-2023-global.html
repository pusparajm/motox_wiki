Frequently Asked Questions

What is the torque of the Kawasaki KX 112?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 8 Nm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki KX 112?

The Kawasaki KX 112 is listed with a 112 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki KX 112?

The seat height is listed as 871 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki KX 112 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 76.7 kg.

Is the Kawasaki KX 112 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 871 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki KX 112?

Fuel capacity is listed as 5 L.

What tyres does the KX 112 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 70/100-19 front, 90/100-16 rear.

What suspension does the Kawasaki KX 112 use?

Front: 36mm inverted telescopic cartridge fork with 20-way compression damping, 274 mm (10.8 inches). Rear: Uni-Trak® gas charged shock with piggyback reservoir with 24-way compression and 21-way rebound damping plus adjustable spring preload, 274 mm (10.8 inches).

What brakes does the Kawasaki KX 112 use?

Front: Single disc. Double-piston caliper​.. Rear: Single disc. Single-piston caliper.

What brake pads does the KX 112 use?

Front: SBS 726RSI. Rear: SBS 592RSI. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What chain does the KX 112 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 420 pitch, 124 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What fork oil does the Kawasaki KX 112 use?

The listed fork oil specification is SAE 5W, 0.29-0.33 litres (0.31-0.34 US quarts) per leg. Confirm the fill procedure in the workshop manual.
