Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the YZF-R7 make?

The Yamaha YZF-R7 is rated at 72 hp (53 kW) @ 8,750 rpm.

What is the torque of the Yamaha YZF-R7?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 67 Nm @ 6,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Yamaha YZF-R7?

The Yamaha YZF-R7 is listed with a 689 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Yamaha YZF-R7?

The seat height is listed as 835 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Yamaha YZF-R7 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 188 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Yamaha YZF-R7?

Using the listed 72 hp (53 kW) and 188 kg, the calculated ratio is about 383 hp per tonne (0.29 kW/kg).

Is the Yamaha YZF-R7 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 72 hp (53 kW) @ 8,750 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 835 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R7 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 53.7 kW and 0.29 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Yamaha YZF-R7 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 835 mm, which is very tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Yamaha YZF-R7?

Fuel capacity is listed as 13 L.
