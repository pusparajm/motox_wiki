Frequently Asked Questions

How much power does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC make?

What is the torque of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC?

What is the engine displacement of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC?

The MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC is listed with a 800 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC?

The seat height is listed as 811 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

Is the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 811 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC?

Fuel capacity is listed as 16.6 L.

What tyres does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-ZR17 front, 200/50-ZR17 rear.

What suspension does the MV Agusta 800 Brutale Dragster RC use?

Front: Marzocchi, 125 mm (4.9 inches). Rear: Progressive Sachs, single shock absorber with rebound and compression damping and spring preload adjustment, 125 mm (4.9 inches).
