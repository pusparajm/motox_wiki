Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the Ninja 500 make?

The Kawasaki Ninja 500 is rated at 51 hp (38 kW) @ 10,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 42.6 Nm @ 7,500 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

The Kawasaki Ninja 500 is listed with a 451 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

The seat height is listed as 785 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the Kawasaki Ninja 500 weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 171 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

Using the listed 51 hp (38 kW) and 171 kg, the calculated ratio is about 298 hp per tonne (0.22 kW/kg).

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 500 suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 51 hp (38 kW) @ 10,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 500 A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 38.0 kW and 0.22 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the Kawasaki Ninja 500 suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 785 mm, which is moderate by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

Fuel capacity is listed as 14 L.

What tyres does the Ninja 500 use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 110/70-17 front, 150/60-17 rear.

Does the Kawasaki Ninja 500 have ABS?

The Kawasaki Ninja 500 is listed with Dual-channel ABS.

What suspension does the Kawasaki Ninja 500 use?

Front: 41 mm telescopic fork. Rear: Uni-Trak single shock.

What brakes does the Kawasaki Ninja 500 use?

Front: 310 mm disc, 2-piston caliper. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the Ninja 500 use?

The listed battery code is YTZ10S. Battery rating: 12V 8.6Ah. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What chain does the Ninja 500 use?

The listed chain or final drive is 520 O-ring, 112 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the Kawasaki Ninja 500?

Engine oil capacity is 2.4 L (2.5 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 10W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
