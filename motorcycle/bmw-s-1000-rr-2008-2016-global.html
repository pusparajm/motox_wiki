Frequently Asked Questions

How much horsepower (BHP) does the S 1000 RR make?

The BMW S 1000 RR is rated at 193 hp (142 kW) @ 13,000 rpm.

What is the torque of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Manufacturer-claimed peak torque is 112 Nm @ 9,750 rpm.

What is the engine displacement of the BMW S 1000 RR?

The BMW S 1000 RR is listed with a 999 cc engine.

What is the seat height of the BMW S 1000 RR?

The seat height is listed as 820 mm. Seat width and suspension sag also affect reach.

How much does the BMW S 1000 RR weigh?

The kerb weight is listed as 204 kg.

What is the power-to-weight ratio of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Using the listed 193 hp (142 kW) and 204 kg, the calculated ratio is about 946 hp per tonne (0.71 kW/kg).

Is the BMW S 1000 RR suitable for beginners?

It falls outside the site's beginner shortlist criteria: 193 hp (142 kW) @ 13,000 rpm is above the 50 hp limit; 204 kg is above the 200 kg limit; 820 mm is above the 800 mm seat limit; its Supersport category is excluded from the shortlist. The criteria are 50 hp or less, 200 kg or less, an 800 mm seat or lower, and a non-sport, non-competition category.

Is the BMW S 1000 RR A2-legal?

On the listed figures it exceeds the European A2 limits: about 143.9 kW and 0.71 kW/kg (limits: 35 kW and 0.2 kW/kg). A restricted version may be available; check local approval.

Is the BMW S 1000 RR suitable for shorter riders?

The seat is listed at 820 mm, which is tall by the site's seat-height bands (760 mm or lower low, 761–800 mm moderate, 801–830 mm tall, above 830 mm very tall). Seat width, suspension sag and rider inseam also affect reach.

What is the fuel capacity of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Fuel capacity is listed as 17.5 L.

What tyres does the S 1000 RR use?

Tyre sizes are listed as 120/70-17 front, 190/55-17 rear.

Does the BMW S 1000 RR have ABS?

The BMW S 1000 RR is listed with Race ABS.

What suspension does the BMW S 1000 RR use?

Front: 46 mm inverted fork. Rear: Single shock.

What brakes does the BMW S 1000 RR use?

Front: 320 mm dual discs, radial 4-piston calipers. Rear: 220 mm disc.

What battery does the S 1000 RR use?

The listed battery code is YTZ10S. Confirm terminal layout and case dimensions against the battery fitted to your machine.

What brake pads does the S 1000 RR use?

Front: EBC FA604/4HH · SBS 870 · Brembo 07BB33 · Vesrah VD-9053. Rear: EBC FA213 · Brembo 07BB02. These are pad-shape references; confirm the compound and exact model year before ordering.

What spark plug does the S 1000 RR use?

The listed spark plug is NGK LMAR9D-J. Confirm the gap and heat range for your model year.

What chain does the S 1000 RR use?

The listed chain or final drive is 525 O-ring, 120 links. Confirm the pitch, link count and adjustment procedure for your model year.

What is the engine oil capacity of the BMW S 1000 RR?

Engine oil capacity is 4.0 L (4.2 US qt) and the recommended grade is SAE 5W-40, JASO MA2. Confirm the exact fill procedure in the owner's or workshop manual.
