Reading a battery code

Codes such as YTX9-BS, YTZ10S and GYZ16H identify case size, terminal layout and performance class. They are not interchangeable just because the voltage matches. A larger-capacity code may not fit the battery box, and a different terminal layout can prevent the leads from reaching.

For models without a listed code, use the battery currently fitted as the primary reference, then check the owner's or workshop manual for the specified capacity and dimensions.

Specifications and part references can vary by model year and market. Confirm the exact part against your machine before ordering. Data updated 2026-11-21.
