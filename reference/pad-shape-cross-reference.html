Brake Pad Shape Cross-Reference

EBC, SBS, Ferodo, Dunlopad, Emgo and Vesrah shape equivalents.

This table cross-references the brake-pad backing-plate shapes used across the catalogue. The same plate can be sold in several friction compounds, and one shape can fit several models, model years and even several brands.

For the motorcycle models that use these shapes, see the brake pad fitment table.

Pad shape cross-reference

The table lists the EBC shape against the equivalent SBS, Ferodo, Dunlopad, Emgo and Vesrah references. A single EBC shape can list several numbers when the same backing plate is offered in different thicknesses or compounds. Treat those as shape-family references, not as “any of these will fit every model”.

Shape diagrams and dimensions are extracted from the EBC Buyers Guide and reproduced with written permission from EBC.

Why one shape fits several models

Brake calipers are often shared across a model family or even across manufacturers. A pad shape is defined by the backing-plate outline, hole spacing, thickness and locating tabs. Once a caliper is used on several bikes, the same shape appears under many model names. The friction compound is separate: a commuter may use an organic or semi-sintered pad, while a sport or race model uses a sintered or race compound in the same shape.

That is why a cross-reference list can show several models from one brand against one code. The compound can still differ by model or model year, so check the suffix and the intended use before mixing a touring pad into a track application or vice versa.
